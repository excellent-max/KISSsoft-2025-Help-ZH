中文翻译

特殊齿形

术语“特殊齿形”用于描述具有非渐开线齿面的轮齿。特殊齿形的参考齿廓（或通过滚刀或齿条刀的法向截面）不是直线的（不同于渐开线轮齿）。然而，两种轮齿类型采用相同的展成法制造。作为齿形计算的一部分，特殊齿形（摆线齿、圆弧齿）可以从 CAD 导入或直接定义。此外，您可以通过点击与齿轮副中的另一个齿轮一起生成（在齿形选项卡中）来生成合适的配对齿轮。

通过模拟展成过程，可以为特殊齿形定义齿形，并由此定义几何形状。强度计算没有标准或技术文献可循。此类齿形的计算可以从圆柱齿轮方法推导得出。（参见"接触分析"章）

English Original

Special toothing

The term "special toothing" is used to describe toothing with non-involute flanks. The reference profile (or the normal section through the hobbing cutter or rack-shaped cutter) of special toothing is not straight (unlike involute toothing). However, the same generating process is used to manufacture both toothing types. As part of the tooth form calculation, special toothing (cycloid, circular arc teeth) can either be imported from CAD or defined directly. In addition, you can then generate a suitable counter gear by clicking Generate with the other gear in the pair in the Tooth form tab.

By simulating the generation process, the tooth form and, from this, the geometry can then be defined for special toothing. There are no standards or technical documentation for strength calculations. The calculation for such tooth forms can be derived from the cylindrical gear method. (see chapter Contact analysis)
