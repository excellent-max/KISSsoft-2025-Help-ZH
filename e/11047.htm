中文翻译

齿轮2的齿顶角，齿轮2的齿根角

通过改变齿轮2的齿顶角和齿根角，可以改变齿宽方向上的齿高。为了计算啮合齿轮（齿轮1）的齿顶角和齿根角，输入一个恒定顶隙（啮合齿轮的齿顶圆锥面与根锥面平行）。

由于齿轮类型的限制：对于锥角无法更改的齿轮类型，您不能更改其锥角。您不能更改“标准，图1”类型的齿顶角和根锥角。虽然“标准，图4”类型的顶锥角（面锥角）可以更改，但根锥角不能更改。对于“均匀齿高，图3”类型，齿顶角和根锥角完全不能更改。

注意

这些用于在 条件 II 中改变参数的选项对差速锥齿轮很有用，差速锥齿轮的特点是制造过程中存在较大的几何变化。然而，请记住在使用螺旋齿常规制造方法时必须满足通常的条件。

English Original

Addendum angle gear 2, dedendum angle gear 2

By varying the addendum and dedendum angle on gear 2 you can then vary the tooth height along the facewidth. To calculate the addendum and dedendum angle on the mating gear (gear 1), input a constant tip clearance (parallel tip cone and root cone for the mating gear).

Restrictions due to gear type: You cannot vary the cone angle for gear types for which the angle cannot be changed. You cannot vary the addendum angle and root angle of the "Standard, Figure 1" type. Although the face angle can be varied for the "Standard, Figure 4" type, you cannot vary the root angle. The addendum angle and root angle cannot be varied at all for the "Uniform tooth depth, Figure 3" types.

Note

These options for varying the parameters in Conditions II are useful for differential bevel gears, which are characterized by major geometric variations during manufacture. However, do remember that the usual conditions must be met when using conventional manufacturing methods for spiral teeth.
