中文翻译

法向截面压力角

对于标准啮合，压力角为 αn = 20°。对于较大的齿数，可以使用较小的压力角以获得更高的重合度。较大的压力角可提高强度，并可使用较少的齿数而不发生根切。在这种情况下，重合度会减小。

对于准双曲面齿轮，单击 加号按钮可独立输入驱动齿面和从动齿面的压力角。驱动齿面是小齿轮的凹齿面和齿轮的凸齿面。从动齿面是小齿轮的凸齿面和齿轮的凹齿面。

English Original

Pressure angle at normal section

For standard meshings, the pressure angle is αn = 20°. You can use smaller pressure angles for a larger number of teeth to achieve higher contact ratios. Greater pressure angles increase the strength and enable a smaller number of teeth to be used without undercut. In this situation, the contact ratio decreases.

For hypoid gears, click the Plus button to enter the pressure angle for the driving flank and the driven flank independently from each other. The driving flank is the concave flank of the pinion and the convex flank of the gear. The driven flank is the convex flank of the pinion and the concave flank of the gear.
