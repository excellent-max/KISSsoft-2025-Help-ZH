锥齿轮与准双曲面齿轮

使用本模块计算直齿、斜齿和弧齿锥齿轮（齿轮轴线相交，偏置距为 0）以及准双曲面齿轮（齿轮轴线交错，偏置距不等于 0）的几何形状和强度。几何形状按 ISO 23509 和 DIN 3971 规定，公差按 ISO 17485 和 DIN 3975 规定，强度计算按 ISO 10300（替代圆柱齿轮轮齿法）、AGMA 2003、DIN 3991 或 Klingelnberg 内部标准 KN 3030 规定。无论使用哪种制造工艺，计算仅包括强度计算所必需的锥齿轮几何形状（参见"「「「强度计算所使用的方法」」」"章）。

English Original

Bevel and Hypoid Gears

Use this module to calculate the geometry and strength of straight, helical and spiral bevel gears (gear axes intersect, offset is 0) and hypoid gears (crossed gear axes, offset not equal to 0). Geometry as specified in ISO 23509 and DIN 3971, tolerances according to ISO 17485 and DIN 3975, strength calculation as specified in ISO 10300 (replacement cylindrical gear toothing method), AGMA 2003, DIN 3991, or Klingelnberg in-house standard KN 3030. The calculation only includes the geometry of bevel gears insofar as is necessary for the strength calculation (see chapter Methods used for strength calculation), no matter which manufacturing process is used.
