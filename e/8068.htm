中文翻译

接触系数

按 DIN 6892 [83] 定义的接触系数：

Kν=1/(i*)

= 1

一个键时

= 0.75

两个键时，用于计算等效表面压力

= 0.9

两个键时，用于计算最大表面压力

两个以上的键不常见。

KISSsoft 根据键的数量计算接触系数。

English Original

Contact coefficient

Contact coefficient as defined in DIN 6892 [83]:

Kν=1/(i*)

= 1

for one key

= 0.75

for two keys, to calculate the equivalent surface pressure

= 0.9

for two keys, to calculate the maximum surface pressure

More than two keys are unusual.

KISSsoft calculates the contact coefficient based on the number of keys.
