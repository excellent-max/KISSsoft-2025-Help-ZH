中文翻译

以科学记数法显示使用寿命

当轴承的额定寿命超过 100 万小时时，通常会截断为 100 万小时。但在某些情况下，了解未截断的额定寿命也很有用。此结果可通过激活此选项获得。

English Original

Display service life in scientific notation

When the rating life of a bearing is longer than 1 million hours it is typically truncated to 1 million hours. However, in some cases it is also useful to know the non-truncated rating life. This result can be obtained by activating this option.
