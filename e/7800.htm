中文翻译

强度值

对于承受静态或准静态载荷的弹簧（N <= 104），将计算弹簧上的最大力。该公式在 DIN EN 16984 中预先定义。然后将该力与弹簧的有效力 Fn（在 s = 0.75·h0 处）进行比较，并计算弹簧力利用率。如果所需力大于弹簧的有效力 Fn，则计算中的偏差过大。用于计算力的 DIN 公式仅适用于弹簧行程为 s = 0.8·h0 的情况。弹簧可以成组或成列使用以承受更大的力。计算出的总系统力随后显示在力-行程图中。碟簧的计算不考虑摩擦。
应力还在边缘点 I 至 IV 处计算。点 I 和 IV 承受压缩，点 II 和 III 承受拉伸。在动态载荷下，应力范围使用最大应力（在点 II 或 III 处）及其相关的较低应力水平来计算。许用永久应力范围使用 Goodman 图定义。然后将这些值进行比较。结果是弹簧在载荷下应能承受的载荷循环次数。DIN EN 16983 包含的 Goodman 图仅对 DIN 17221 和 17222 中规定的材料有效。如果涉及任何其他材料，您必须联系弹簧制造商。

English Original

Strength values

In the case of springs that are subject to static or quasi-static load (N <= 104), the maximum force on the spring is calculated. The formula is predefined in DIN EN 16984. This force then is compared to the spring's effective force Fn (at s = 0.75·h0) and the spring force utilization is calculated. If the required force is greater than the spring's effective force, Fn, the deviation in the calculation is too large. The DIN formula for calculating force only applies where the spring's travel is s = 0.8·h0. The springs can be used in packages or columns to handle larger forces. The total calculated system force is then displayed in a force travel diagram. The calculation for disc springs is performed without taking friction into account.
The stresses are also calculated at edge points I through IV. Points I and IV are subjected to compression, and points II and III are put under tension. Under dynamic load, the stress range is calculated using the maximum stress (either at point II or III) with the associated lower stress level. The permissible permanent stress range is defined using a Goodman diagram. These values are then compared. The result is the number of load cycles that the springs should be able to withstand under load. DIN EN 16983 includes Goodman diagrams which are only valid for materials specified in DIN 17221 and 17222. If any other materials are involved, you must contact the spring manufacturer.
