花键（仅强度）

花键是一种具有缩短齿高和大压力角（通常为 30°）的直齿轮啮合。在 KISSsoft 中，您可以使用两种不同计算模块之一来计算花键。制造所需的几何尺寸和公差以及强度计算在"连接"部分的"花键（几何与强度）"章 花键（几何与强度） 中描述。

对于花键，您必须计算轴和轮毂上的载荷（表面压力）。您还可以添加其他标准。轮齿数据在数据库中定义，因此您可以强制使用内部齿廓。您还可以使用 KISSsoft 花键（几何与强度）模块来计算制造质量和公差。该计算以及安全系数的定义按照经典技术文献（[8]）中所述进行。

English Original

Spline (strength only)

Splines are spur gear meshings that have a shortened tooth height and a large pressure angle (usually 30°). In KISSsoft, you can use one of two different calculation modules to calculate splines. The geometry and tolerances required for manufacture, and the strength calculation, are described in the Connections section in the Splines (Geometry and Strength) chapter Splines (Geometry and Strength).

For splines, you must calculate the load on shaft and hub (surface pressure). You can also add additional standards. Toothing data is defined in the database and therefore you can make the use of in-house profiles mandatory. You can also use the KISSsoft Spline (geometry and strength) module, to calculate the manufacturing mass and the tolerances. This calculation, along with defining the safeties, is performed as described in classic technical literature ([8]).
