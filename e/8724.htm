配置

轮齿的基准齿廓通常是预定义的。但是，您也可以定义自己的滚刀或插齿刀。插齿刀参数也用于强度计算中的齿形系数。对于精密工程，您还可以选择 构造渐开线。在这种情况下，渐开线连同齿根圆角半径一起直接定义。

English Original

Configuration

The reference profile of the gear teeth is usually predefined. However, you can also define your own hobbing cutter or pinion type cutter. The pinion type cutter parameters are also used in the strength calculation to calculate the tooth form factor. You can also select Constructed involute for precision engineering. In this case, the involute is defined directly together with a root radius.
