中文翻译

应力曲线（面齿轮）

该图形显示了沿面齿轮齿宽的应力曲线（齿根和齿面）。计算将齿宽拆分为若干单个分段，然后可按照 ISO 6336、DIN 3990 或 AGMA 2001 将其作为齿条副进行尺寸计算。计算假设恒定的线载荷（由于节圆不同，每个分段会产生略有不同的扭矩）。

当您计算用于表示接触线和应力曲线的数据时，最重要的数值会在单独的部分中计算，并保存到两个单独的表格中。此数据存储在 Z60-H1.TMP 和 Z60-H2.TMP 文件中。

English Original

Stress curve (face gear)

The graphic shows the stress curve (tooth root and flank) over the face gear's facewidth. The calculation splits the facewidth into individual segments, which can then be sized as rack pairs either according to ISO 6336, DIN 3990 or AGMA 2001. The calculation assumes a constant line load (which results in a slightly different torque for each segment due to the different pitch circle).

When you calculate data to represent the contact line and the stress curve, the most important values are calculated in separate sections and saved to two separate tables. This data is stored in the Z60-H1.TMP and Z60-H2.TMP files.
