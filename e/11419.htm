中文翻译

草图器

使用草图器可以用鼠标"绘制"齿轮装置的机构。您在草图器中所做的所有定义也会同时显示在轴视图和单元视图中。因此，您可以直接在草图器中创建或修改模型。您可以随时在窗口之间切换。

English Original

Sketcher

Use the Sketcher to "draw" the gear unit's kinematics with the mouse. All the definitions you make in the Sketcher are also displayed simultaneously in the Shaft view and Element view. As a result, you can create or modify the model directly in the Sketcher. You can switch between the windows at any time.
