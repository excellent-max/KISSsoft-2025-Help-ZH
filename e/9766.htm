中文翻译

刀片组数

刀片组数描述制造弧齿锥齿轮所用刀盘上的刀片组数；当使用端面滚切法时，该数值与刀盘半径一起影响齿长方向的锥度。必须按 ISO 23509 附录 E 或制造商说明中的规定输入刀片组数。

English Original

Number of blade groups

The number of blade groups describes the number of cutter blade groups on the cutter head used to manufacture bevel gears with spiral teeth and, when face hobbing is in use, this number, together with the cutter radius, influences the bevel of the tooth length. You must enter the number of blade groups as defined in ISO 23509, Annex E or as specified in the manufacturers' instructions.
