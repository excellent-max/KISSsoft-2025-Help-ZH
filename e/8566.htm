齿宽

齿宽通常不应大于建议值（齿宽与外锥距之比（参见"齿顶角与齿根角"章）、模数比（参见"模数比"章））。如果齿宽过大，接触区会恶化。

English Original

Facewidth

The facewidth should not usually be larger than the one given in the recommendations (facewidth ratio to outer cone distance (see chapter Addendum angle and root angle), module ratio (see chapter Module ratio)). The contact pattern deteriorates if the facewidth is too large.
