中文翻译

转角修形

在某些不太理想的情况下，刀盘可能会切入紧邻齿形的轴销。如果无法通过修改设计或齿形数据来避免，则齿轮和小齿轮在 dm 处计算点的刀尖平面可以倾斜一个微小角度 ϑk，从其预期位置 δo1,2 向参考锥角 δE1,2（见图 16.3 和图 16.4）。

English Original

Angle modification

In some less than ideal situations, it may happen that the cutter head cuts into any shaft pins that are located immediately next to the toothing. If this cannot be prevented by modifying either the design or the toothing data, the cutter tip level at the calculation point at dm of the gear and pinion can be tilted by a slight angle ϑk from its intended position δo1,2 towards the reference cone angle δE1,2 (see Figure 16.3) and (see Figure 16.4).
