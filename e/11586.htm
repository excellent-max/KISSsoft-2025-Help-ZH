中文翻译

组视图

所有部件关系都可以在一个视图中显示。因此可以将轴计算添加到该视图中，例如，与安装在其轴上的齿轮相关的所有齿轮计算随后也将被添加到同一视图中。

English Original

Group view

All component relationships can be displayed in one view. A shaft calculation can therefore be added to this view and, for example, all gear calculations related to the gears mounted on its shafts will then also be added to the same view.
