中文翻译

由齿形计算转动惯量

轮齿的转动惯量由齿顶到齿根直径范围内的齿形精确计算得出。为实现这一点，KISSsoft 会在每次计算时自动运行齿形计算，并通过转动惯量的数值积分来确定有效齿形。结果输出到计算报告中。精细选型时也会执行该计算，并记录结果。

English Original

Calculate moment of inertia from tooth form

The toothing moment of inertia is calculated exactly from the tooth form in the tip to root diameter range. To achieve this, the KISSsoft tooth form calculation is run automatically for each calculation and defines the effective tooth form by the numerical integration of the moment of inertia. The result is output in the calculation report. The calculation is also performed in fine sizing and the results are documented.
