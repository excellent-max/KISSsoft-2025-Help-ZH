中文翻译

齿面形状

齿面形状是制造工艺的结果。ZA、ZN、ZK 和 ZI 蜗杆具有非常相似的效率和齿面承载能力水平。虽然 ZC 和 ZH 蜗杆（凹形齿面）在某些情况下具有更好的承载能力，但它们确实有其他主要缺点。

ZA 型：

在车床上用刀具（直线齿面）制造，安装在轴向截面中

ZN 型：

在车床上用刀具（直线齿面）制造，安装在法向截面中

ZI 型：

用滚刀制造（蜗杆齿面为渐开线）

ZK 型：

用砂轮（直线齿面）制造，安装在法向截面中

ZC 或 ZH 型：

使用特殊刀具制造以生成凹形齿面

有关更多信息，请参阅：Dubbel [26]，附有第 G136 和 S79 页的图。

English Original

Shape of flank

The flank shape is a result of the manufacturing process. ZA, ZN, ZK and ZI worms have very similar levels of efficiency and flank load capacity. Although ZC and ZH worms (hollow flanks) have better load capacity in some situations, they do have other major disadvantages.

ZA form:

manufactured on lathe with tool (straight flanks), mounted in axial section

ZN-form:

manufactured on lathe with tool (straight flanks), mounted in normal section

ZI form:

manufactured with hobbing cutter (worm flank is involute)

ZK form:

manufactured with grinding wheel (straight flanks), mounted in normal section

ZC, or ZH form:

Manufacturing with special tools to generate a hollow flank

For more information, please refer to: Dubbel [26], with figures on pages G136 and S79.
