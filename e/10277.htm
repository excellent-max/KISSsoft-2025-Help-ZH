English Original

Improving the tooth form

You can change the tooth form of circular gears quite significantly by changing the profile shift. In the current version of the program for non-circular gears, we recommend you set the profile shift coefficient of the pinion type cutter x*0=0. Despite this, you can still modify the tooth form by changing the pressure angle αn .
