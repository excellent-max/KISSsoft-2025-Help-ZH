中文翻译

允许的齿厚减薄量

允许的齿厚减薄量（齿轮上）是计算磨损安全系数所需的，在计算齿根安全系数时会考虑。如果此输入字段包含 0，则不检查允许的齿厚减薄量。

English Original

Permissible tooth thickness decrease

The permissible tooth thickness decrease (on the gear) is needed to calculate the wear safety and taken into account when calculating the root safety. If this input field contains 0, the permissible tooth thickness decrease is not checked.
