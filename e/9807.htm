中文翻译

小齿轮 - Z1 > Z2 的面齿轮

当面齿轮（Z2）的齿数小于小齿轮（Z1）的齿数时，没有规定计算小齿轮-面齿轮副，因为这种情况并不常见。但是，在某些条件下，您仍然可以确定这种齿轮副的几何形状。

为此，选择 计算设置 并单击 发生几何错误时不中止 复选框。然后，我们建议您按照以下步骤操作：

  • 减小面齿轮的齿宽（例如，减半）
  • 从 Z2 = Z1 开始，逐步将 Z2 缩小，每步执行一次计算，并在 2D 显示中修正切齿的内侧、中间和外侧形态，必要时修正齿高。
  • 一旦达到所需的齿数 Z2，请尝试再次增大面齿轮的齿宽，并在必要时修改 bv。
English Original

Pinion - Face gear with Z1 > Z2

No provision has been made for calculating a pinion – face gear pairing when the number of teeth on the face gear (Z2) is less than the number of teeth on the pinion (Z1), because this situation does not happen very often. However, under certain conditions, you can still determine the geometry of this type of pairing.

To do this, select Calculation settings and click the Don’t abort if geometry errors occur checkbox. Then, we recommend you follow these steps:

  • Reduce the facewidth of the face gear (for example, by half)
  • Starting with Z2 = Z1, zoom Z2 out step by step, performing a calculation after every step and correcting the inside, middle, and outside aspect of the cuts and, if necessary the tooth height, in the 2D display.
  • Once you achieve the required number of teeth Z2, try to increase the facewidth of the face gear again, and modify bv if necessary.
