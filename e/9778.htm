中文翻译

箱体表面粗糙度

箱体的表面粗糙度值用于计算滚动轴承的标称工作间隙。压力按外径无限大的箱体计算。如果不同的轴承需要不同的粗糙度，或者必须定义外径，则可以在此处定义附加轴。

English Original

Housing surface roughness

The housing's surface roughness value is used to calculate the nominal operating clearance for rolling bearings. The pressure is calculated for a housing with an infinitely large external diameter. Additional shafts can be defined here if different roughnesses are required for different bearings or if the external diameter must be defined.
