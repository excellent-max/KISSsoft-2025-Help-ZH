中文翻译

静力计算中的箱体变形

静力计算中考虑箱体变形的方式基于使用有限元法（FEM）计算的箱体缩减刚度矩阵。该缩减刚度矩阵应包含与轴承中心点相对应的节点，这些轴承将齿轮单元的各轴连接到箱体。

English Original

Housing deformation in static calculations

The way that housing deformation is included in static calculations is based on the use of a reduced stiffness matrix for the housing, as calculated by the Finite Element Method (FEM). This reduced stiffness matrix should include the nodes that refer to the center point of the bearings that connect the gear unit shafts to the housing.
