中文翻译

动载能力

动载能力是整个轴承的属性。ISO 281 描述了滚动轴承在特定条件下以特定转速承受特定机械载荷时出现的多种不同属性。这些数据随后用于计算运行小时数（通常基于 10% 的失效概率）。

English Original

Dynamic load capacity

The dynamic load capacity is a property of the entire bearing. ISO 281 describes a number of various properties of a rolling bearing that occur if the bearing experiences specific mechanical loading under specific conditions at specific speeds. This data is then used to calculate the number of operating hours (this is usually based on a failure probability of 10%).
