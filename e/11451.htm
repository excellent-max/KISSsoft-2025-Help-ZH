中文翻译

拓扑修形

在对现有锥齿轮进行逆向工程时，只需将现有锥齿轮的测量网格导入 KISSsoft，然后计算拓扑修形。有关这些输入的更精确说明，请联系 KISSsoft 技术支持并索取文档 KISSsoft-anl-068-E-3D Geometry of Spiral Bevel Gear.pdf。

English Original

Topological modifications

When re-engineering existing bevel gears, simply import the measurement grid from an existing bevel gear into KISSsoft and then calculate a topological modification. For more precise instructions about these entries, please contact KISSsoft Support and request the document KISSsoft-anl-068-E-3D Geometry of Spiral Bevel Gear.pdf.
