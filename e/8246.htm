中文翻译

截面

屈服安全系数和疲劳断裂安全系数在沿轴的特定截面上确定，这些截面由您定义。在元素树中，您会在组级别看到 截面。在上下文菜单中，您可以添加 自由截面 或 限定截面。

English Original

Cross sections

The yield safety and safety for fatigue fracture are determined at specific cross sections along a shaft that are defined by you. In the Element Tree, you will see Cross sections at group level. In the context menu, you can either add a Free cross section or a Limited cross section.
