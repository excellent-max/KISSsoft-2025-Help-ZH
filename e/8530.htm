中文翻译

冠齿轮方法 (DIN 3990)

此计算方法产生的结果与 Crown Gear 程序产生的结果一致。计算的基本原理已在前文的 "ISO 6336/Literature"（参见"方法 ISO 6336-B/Literature"章）中作说明。

该方法与 "ISO 6336/Literature" 方法之间的主要区别如下：

  • 该计算基于 DIN 3990 中定义的方法。
  • 计算齿宽（点蚀）与齿宽一致（即使最小接触线长度短于齿宽）。
  • 圆周力 Ft 由 dPd（分度圆 = 模数 × 齿数）确定，即使 dPd 不是齿宽的中间位置。
English Original

Crown Gear Method (DIN 3990)

This calculation method produces results that correspond to those produced by the Crown Gear program. The underlying principle of calculation is described earlier in the "ISO 6336/Literature" (see chapter Method ISO 6336-B/Literature) method.

The main differences between it and the "ISO 6336/Literature" method are:

  • The calculation is based on the method defined in DIN 3990.
  • The arithmetical facewidth (pitting) corresponds to the facewidth (even if the minimum contact line length is shorter than the facewidth).
  • The circumferential force Ft is determined from dPd (reference circle = module * number of teeth), even if dPd is not the middle of the facewidth.
