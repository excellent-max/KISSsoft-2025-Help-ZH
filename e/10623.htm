中文翻译

油位

如果选择使用 SKF 2018 目录或 Schaeffler 2017 目录（INA、FAG）中描述的计算方法来计算摩擦，油位将影响摩擦力矩，该力矩由过程中损失的油量决定。这在“摩擦力矩”一章中有更详细的说明（见“摩擦力矩”章节）。

油位的输入以第一根轴的左端为参考（但仅在指定了油浴润滑时）。然后根据轴的位置为每个轴承（h 和 H）定义单独的油位，该油位在计算损失时予以考虑。油位在轴编辑器中显示，以便您进行检查。

English Original

Oil level

If you select the calculation method described in the SKF catalog 2018 or the Schaeffler catalog 2017 (INA, FAG) to calculate friction, the oil level has an effect on the moment of friction which is determined by the amount of oil lost in the process. This is described in greater detail in the Moment of Friction chapter. (see chapter Moment of friction).

You input the oil level with reference to the left-hand end of the first shaft (but only if Oil bath lubrication has been specified). The position of the shaft is then used to define a separate oil level for each bearing (h and H) which is then taken into account when calculating the loss. The oil level is displayed in the Shaft Editor, so you can check it.
