中文翻译

轴用元件

不同类型的元件可以放置在轴上。它们也会传递到 KISSsoft 轴计算中。轴上的位置通过 position 变量定义。

  • kSysHelicalGear：圆柱齿轮。
  • kSysBevelGear：锥齿轮。锥顶的位置通过 direction 变量定义。
  • kSysWorm：蜗杆。
  • kSysWormGear：蜗轮。
  • kSysCoupling：联轴器。可为 3D 显示输入直径 d 和宽度 b 。
  • kSysBearing：普通类型的轴承。损耗可记录在 Tloss 中。损耗扭矩的方向应在表达式中用 -sign(speed) 来定义。
  • kSysRollerBearing：滚动轴承。每次刷新计算时，轴承几何数据都会从 KISSsoft 轴承数据库中导入。损耗可记录在 Tloss 中。损耗扭矩的方向应在表达式中用 -sign(speed) 来定义。
  • kSysCentricalLoad：中心载荷。
  • kSysMass：轴上的附加质量。
  • kSysRopeSheave：滑轮（绳轮）。皮带力通过连接计算得出。
  • kSysFaceGear: 端面齿轮。
English Original

Elements for shafts

Different elements can be placed onto shafts. They will also be transferred to the KISSsoft shaft calculation. The position on the shaft is defined with the position variable.

  • kSysHelicalGear: A cylindrical gear.
  • kSysBevelGear: A bevel gear. The position of the peak is defined by the direction variable.
  • kSysWorm: A worm.
  • kSysWormGear: A worm wheel.
  • kSysCoupling: A coupling. Diameter d and Width b can be entered for the 3D display.
  • kSysBearing: A normal type of bearing. Losses can be recorded in Tloss. The direction of the loss torque should be defined with a -sign(speed) in the expression.
  • kSysRollerBearing: A rolling bearing. The bearing geometry is imported from the KISSsoft bearings database every time you refresh the calculation. Losses can be recorded in Tloss. The direction of the loss torque should be defined with a -sign(speed) in the expression.
  • kSysCentricalLoad: A centrical load.
  • kSysMass: An additional mass on the shaft.
  • kSysRopeSheave: A pulley (rope sheave). The belt force is calculated via the connection.
  • kSysFaceGear: A face gear.
