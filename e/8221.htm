FKM 指南，2020 版

FKM 指南（FKM：Forschungskuratorium Maschinenbau e.V.，法兰克福（机械工程研究委员会））基于前 GDR 标准，并包含了制造工艺理论的最新知识。它很可能会成为新 VDI 指南的基础。FKM 指南篇幅很长（约 175 页，另加 400 页评注），不仅包括经典的耐久极限计算范围，还包括考虑载荷谱的疲劳强度计算和额定寿命计算。它还提供了针对特殊问题（如工作温度高于 100°C）的计算方法。

如果用户从列表中选择 为每个载荷工况单独定义数据 并为每个载荷谱定义应力比，则可以根据 FKM 指南以等效应力校核的方式执行疲劳强度校核。该校核执行的功能与通常执行的幅值校核相同。为考虑不同应力比对校核载荷侧的影响，将载荷谱转换为平均应力 Sm = 0 且应力比 R = -1。这样便可根据 FKM 指南对轴执行强度计算，并考虑雨流矩阵载荷谱的影响。

计算按 FKM 指南第 7 版（2020 年）进行，采用 Haibach 提出的解法。

疲劳强度

使用强度系数 KBK,S 按指南第 2.4 章确定。拐点处的循环次数 ND 为 106。

KBK,S 在载荷循环次数小于 ND 时大于 1.0。在 ND 以上，KBK,S 通常等于 1.0。

在给定载荷（无载荷谱）下进行的常规计算称为“单个载荷”。该计算按指南第 2.4 节进行。载荷谱可使用三种不同的处理方式。参见"计算类型"章。

English Original

FKM Guideline, 2020 Edition

The FKM guideline (FKM: Forschungskuratorium Maschinenbau e.V., Frankfurt (Board of Research in Mechanical Engineering)) is based on the former GDR standards and includes the latest knowledge on workshop theory. It will probably form the basis of a new VDI guideline. The FKM Guideline is long (running to approximately 175 pages plus 400 pages of commentaries), and includes not only the classic range of endurance limit calculation, but also fatigue strength calculations and rating life calculations, taking into account load spectra. It also provides calculation approaches for special problems such as operating temperatures above 100°C.

If the user selects Define data for each load case individually from the list and defines a stress ratio for each load spectrum, the proof of fatigue strength can be performed as an equivalent stress verification, according to the FKM guideline. This proof performs the same function as the amplitude proof which is normally performed. To take into account the effect of different stress ratios on the load side of the proof, the load spectrum is converted to the mean stress Sm = 0 and the stress ratio R = -1. This enables a strength calculation to be performed for a shaft, according to the FKM guideline, with the effect of a Rainflow matrix load spectrum.

The calculation is performed according to the 7th edition (2020) of the FKM Guideline, using the solutions proposed by Haibach.

Fatigue strength

The service strength coefficient KBK,S is determined according to chapter 2.4 of the guideline. The number of cycles at knee point ND is 106.

KBK,S is greater than 1.0 if the number of load cycles is less than ND. Above ND, KBK,S usually equals 1.0.

Normal calculations with a given load (without load spectrum) are referred to as an "individual load". This is calculated in accordance with section 2.4 of the guideline. Three different processes can be used for load spectra. See (see chapter Type of calculation).
