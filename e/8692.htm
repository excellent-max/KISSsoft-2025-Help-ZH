中文翻译

动载系数

动载系数考虑轮齿啮合中由固有频率（共振）引起的附加力。它通常使用您选择的计算方法计算，但如果该值已通过更精确的测量得出，您也可以直接输入该值。若要更改该值，请单击输入字段右侧的复选框。

English Original

Dynamic factor

The dynamic factor takes into account additional forces caused by natural frequencies (resonance) in the tooth meshing. It is usually calculated using the calculation method you selected, however you can also input the value if it has already been derived from more precise measurements. To change the value, click the checkbox to the right of the input field.
