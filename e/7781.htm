中文翻译

带长

在计算 V 带之前，您需要知道带长。如果您未指定长度，或者更改为涉及张紧轮的配置，则必须重新计算。

English Original

Belt length

You need to know the belt length before you can calculate a V-belt. If you have not specified a length, or if you change to a configuration that involves a tensioning pulley, you must recalculate the belt length.
