中文翻译

非线性轴

单击此选项可使用几何非线性梁单元进行计算。由于弧长保持不变，这会导致轴向方向上的挠度或位移。在大多数使用轴的应用中，这种非线性模型是无关紧要的。

示例

一根两端固定在其安装座上的轴承受中心力。线性梁模型不考虑梁的伸长，因为它忽略了剪切和弯矩载荷下的轴向位移。如果单击 非线性轴 字段，您可以选择一种考虑轴上弯曲效应、从而考虑梁伸长的计算方法。这会产生轴向力。

English Original

Non-linear shaft

Click this option to perform a calculation using geometric non-linear beam elements. This can result in either a deflection or a displacement in the axial direction because the arc length remains constant. In most applications where shafts are used, this non-linear model is irrelevant.

Example

A shaft, which is fixed to its mounting on both sides, is subjected to centrical force. The linear beam model does not allow for an elongation of the beam because it ignores axial displacement during shear and moment loads. If you click on the Non-linear shaft field, you can select a calculation method that takes into account the bending effect on the shaft and therefore the elongation of the beam. This results in axial forces.
