中文翻译

下载许可证文件

  1. 前往 KISSsoft 网站 www.KISSsoft.com。
  2. 在那里注册 MyKISSsoft 区域，然后通知您的商业联系人代表，或发送电子邮件至 info@KISSsoft.com。
  3. 在 KISSsoft AG 为您分配所需权限后，请选择 客户区域 顶部的菜单，然后下载您的许可证文件。

注意：

您将在个人下载区域中看到不同 KISSsoft 版本的许可证文件。请务必下载与您刚安装的版本相对应的许可证文件。

English Original

Downloading a license file

  1. Go to the KISSsoft website at www.KISSsoft.com.
  2. Register in the MyKISSsoft area there, and inform your commercial contact representative or send an email to info@KISSsoft.com.
  3. After you have been assigned the required rights by KISSsoft AG, select the menu at the top of the Customer area and then download your license file.

Note:

You will see license files for different KISSsoft versions in your personal download area. Ensure that you download the appropriate license file for the version you have just installed.
