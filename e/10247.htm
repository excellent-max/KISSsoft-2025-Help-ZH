中文翻译

齿数（有公倍数的情况）

一个齿数为15:55的齿轮已进行了尺寸设计。不同文档指出，应避免使用整数齿轮比（如11:22）。你还会发现应避免使用齿数为公倍数的齿轮（在此情况下是3*5到11*5中的5）。这是否正确，且 KISSsoft 中是否也显示？

让我们假设一个齿轮的一个齿上存在缺陷。在整数减速比的情况下，这个齿总是会与配对齿轮上的同一齿接触，缺陷会随之传递给对应的齿。然而，如果带缺陷的齿在每次转动时都与不同的配对齿接触，那么随着齿轮的磨损，这个缺陷会逐渐减小。

如今，大多数齿轮都经过表面硬化处理。与强度较低的齿轮不同，它们几乎不会发生磨损。因此，这个问题已不像过去那样关键；在过去，即使使用硬化齿轮，也必须避免整数齿轮减速比（如11:22）。相比之下，具有公倍数的整数齿数组合（如15:55）对表面硬化齿轮不会造成任何问题。

在 KISSsoft 中，您可以在精算和粗算中通过关键词 "hunting"（猎齿）找到关于带猎齿倍数的整数齿数组合的说明。如果表中 "hunting" 一栏显示 YES，则表示：不存在公倍数。

English Original

Number of teeth with common multiples

A gearing with 15:55 teeth has been sized. Different documents state that you should avoid gear reductions (such as 11:22) that are whole numbers. You will also discover that you should also avoid using numbers of teeth that are common multiples (in this case the 5 in 3*5 to 11*5). Is that true, and is it displayed in KISSsoft?

Let's assume we have a gear which has a fault on one of its teeth. In a whole number reduction, this tooth will always come into contact with the same tooth in the counter gear. The error is then transmitted to the counter tooth. However, if the tooth with the fault comes into contact with a different counter tooth in every rotation, this error will be reduced as the gears wear in.

Nowadays, most gears are surface-hardened. Unlike weak gears, they hardly ever wear in. As a result, this problem is now less critical than it used to be, where it was important that whole number gear reductions (such as 11:22) were avoided even when hardened gears were used. In contrast, whole number toothing combinations with common multiples (such as 15:55) do not cause any issues for surface hardened gears.

In KISSsoft, you will find notes about whole number combinations with hunting multiples in both fine sizing and rough sizing under the keyword "hunting". If you see YES for "hunting" in the table, this means: no common multiple is present.
