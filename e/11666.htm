中文翻译

用于激励的齿轮副

在强制响应分析的实现中，对于齿轮啮合力激励，默认会考虑所有活动齿轮副的激励。然而，有时只需考虑所有活动齿轮副中的一个或几个齿轮副来激励系统。忽略那些不感兴趣或对系统激励贡献较小的齿轮副，可以加快强制响应分析的速度。在这种情况下，可以在单独的分析中轻松表征每个齿轮副的影响。计算算法保持不变。然而，对于被排除在激励之外的齿轮副，啮合刚度也可以使用“ISO 6336 方法 B”来计算。请注意，仍然需要进行接触分析来计算这些齿轮副的传动误差。另一个重要方面是，必须至少选择一个活动齿轮副。

English Original

Gear pairs for excitation

In the implementation of the forced response analysis, the excitation of all active gear pairs is considered by default for the Gear meshing forces excitation. However, sometimes it is required to consider only one or several gear pairs among all active pairs and to excite the system. Neglecting the gear pairs that are not of interest or may contribute less to the system excitation, can lead to a faster forced response analysis. In this case, one can easily characterize the effect of each gear pair in a separate analysis. The calculation algorithm remains the same. However, for gear pairs that are excluded from the excitation, the meshing stiffness can also be calculated using “ISO 6336 Method B”. Note that a contact analysis is still required to calculate the transmission error for those gear pairs. As another important aspect, at least one active gear pair must be selected.
