中文翻译

垫圈

单击相应的复选框可在螺母与元件之间或螺栓头与元件之间插入垫圈。

选择 计算 > 设置 以显示"设置"选项卡。在其中，如果选择 确定垫圈的特定热膨胀，您还可以定义用于计算预紧力差的热膨胀系数。您将在"设置"部分找到更详细的说明。

English Original

Washers

Click on the appropriate checkbox to insert a washer between the nut and the component or the head and the component.

Select Calculation > Settings to display the Settings tab. In it, if you select Determine specific thermal expansion of washers, you can also define the thermal expansion coefficients that are used to calculate the difference in pretension force. You will find a more detailed description in the "Settings" section.
