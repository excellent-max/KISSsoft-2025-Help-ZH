中文翻译

交错轴斜齿轮与齿条

单击您输入齿数的字段旁的加号按钮，以选择交错轴斜齿轮与齿条的配置。单击您输入中心距的字段旁的加号按钮，以指定齿条高度。

齿条中每个齿上的载荷循环次数可以直接输入，也可以根据使用寿命、小齿轮转速和齿条长度计算。除此之外，其操作与齿轮副的操作相同。

English Original

Crossed helical gear with rack

Click the Plus button next to the field in which you enter the number of teeth to select the crossed helical gear with rack configuration. Click the Plus button next to the field in which you enter the center distance to specify the height of the rack.

The number of load cycles on each tooth in the rack can either be input directly or calculated from the service life, pinion speed and rack length. Otherwise, the operation is identical to that used for a gear pair.
