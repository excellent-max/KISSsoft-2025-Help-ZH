轴系统模态分析

KISSsys 中的“轴系统模态分析”功能用于计算整个轴系统的特征频率和特征模态，包括轴之间齿轮连接的影响。对单个轴进行模态分析是不现实的。此分析必须针对整个轴系统结构进行。必要的计算步骤以及重要限制如下所述。

Modal analysis of shaft systems

The modal analysis of shaft systems function in KISSsys is used to calculate the eigenfrequencies and eigenmodes of an entire shaft system, including the effect of the gear connection between shafts. Performing a modal analysis for individual shafts is not realistic. This analysis must be performed for the entire shaft system structure.
