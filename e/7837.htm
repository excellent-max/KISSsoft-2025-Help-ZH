吊耳界面

利用本界面中 吊耳长度 LH 的定义，您可以确定弹簧的总长度。相反，挂钩开口 m 是一个报告值，未用于此计算。

DIN 2097 为拉伸弹簧规定了 13 种不同的吊耳形状。程序会根据吊耳形状给出不同的吊耳长度。两个吊耳开口的位置也在此 DIN 标准中单独处理。

English Original

Eyes screen

Using the definitions for the Length of eye LH in this screen, you can determine the total length of the spring. In contrast, the Hook opening m is a reported value that is not used in this calculation.

DIN 2097 defines 13 different eye shapes for tension springs. The program suggests different eye lengths depending on the shape of the eye. The position of the two eye openings is also handled separately in this DIN standard.
