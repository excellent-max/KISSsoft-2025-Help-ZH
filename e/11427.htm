中文翻译

使用草图器创建模型

您可以从一开始，或在建模过程的后期，使用鼠标作为“绘图”工具在草图绘制器中创建模型。使用鼠标“绘图”时，请注意以下几点：
要开始和结束一根轴，请双击。要在轴上插入节点，请单击一次。完成轴绘制后，鼠标指针末端会显示一条蓝色虚线。单击所需的网格线以设置相应轴线的位置。您也可以右键单击轴上的节点将其选中，然后通过列表框将其转换为相应的元素（轴承、齿轮、联轴器等）。如果之后需要更多节点，可以通过双击轴的网格点来插入节点。您可以随时通过右键单击节点然后选择“删除”来删除该节点。

您可以双击开始定义另一根轴，并按上述方式创建它。在最后一步，单击鼠标左键将该轴指定到先前定义的轴的轴线上。该轴也可以在绘图层中指定到其自身的新轴线。草图绘制器中的模型会同时在轴视图中构建。当您在草图绘制器中创建新轴线时，轴视图中会同时生成新的轴计算。

绘制好带有轴及其元素的轴线后，您就可以将齿轮相互连接起来。为此，请拖放齿轮元素的中心点以将它们相互连接。同步器和轴承最初被定义为轴上的单个元素。但这些连接元素通常完全定义在两根轴之间。要将这些元素指定到新轴，请用鼠标拖放相应的节点。未指定到任何其他轴的轴承将被视为外圈或内圈与箱体接触的轴承。您还可以通过拖放行星架图标，将行星架图标指定到行星轴的轴线上。

English Original

Creating a model with the Sketcher

You can create the model in the Sketcher, using the mouse as a "drawing" tool, from the very start, or later in the modeling process. When you are "drawing" with the mouse, note these points:
To start and end a shaft, perform a double-click. To insert nodes on the shafts, click once. When you have finished drawing the shaft, a dashed blue line is displayed on the end of the mouse pointer. Click on the required grid line to set the position of the associated shaft axis. You can also right-click on the nodes on a shaft to select them, and then convert them into the appropriate element (bearing, gear, coupling, etc.) via the selection list. If more nodes are needed at a later time, you can insert them by double-clicking on a grid point of the shaft. You can delete a node at any time by right-clicking on it and then selecting "Delete".

You can double-click to begin defining another shaft and create it as described above. In the final step, click the left mouse button to assign the shaft to the axis of a previously defined shaft. The shaft can also be assigned to its own new axis in the drawing level. The model in the Sketcher is built up simultaneously in the Shaft view. When you create a new shaft axis in the Sketcher, a new shaft calculation is simultaneously generated in Shaft view.

Once you have drawn the shaft axes with the shafts and their elements, you can now link the gears with each other. To do so, drag and drop the gear element center points to link them with each other. Synchronizers and bearings are initially defined as individual elements on a shaft. However, these connection elements are often completely defined between two shafts. To assign these elements to a new shaft, use the mouse to drag and drop the associated node. Bearings that are not assigned to any other shaft are handled as bearings that have an outer or inner ring that is in contact with the housing. You can also assign the carrier icon to the axis on your planet shaft(s) by dragging and dropping the carrier icon.
