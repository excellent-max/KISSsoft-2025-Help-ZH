硬化深度建议

标准中规定的各种不同的硬化深度 CHD 建议值均已有记录。ISO、AGMA 和 Niemann 标准中规定的数据往往差别很大，因为其中涉及非常粗略的近似。利用赫兹定律中的剪应力准则来定义所需硬化深度的最精确计算方法，记录在报告的上部。您还可以指定计算所使用的安全系数（参见章节：用于计算硬化深度处剪应力的安全系数）。对于图形显示（参见章节：表面层剪应力）。

English Original

Proposals for hardening depth

A wide range of different proposals for the hardening depth CHD as specified in the standards have been documented. The data specified in the ISO, AGMA and Niemann standards are often very different, because of the very rough approximations involved. The most accurate calculation, which uses the shearing stress criterion from the Hertzian law to define the required hardening depth, is documented in the upper part of the report. You can also specify the safety factor which is to be used for the calculation (see chapter Safety factor for the calculation of the shear stress at hardening depth). For the graphical display (see chapter Surface layer shear stress).
