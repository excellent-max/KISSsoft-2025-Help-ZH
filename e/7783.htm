中文翻译

张紧轮直径

张紧轮直径至少应与最小的带轮一样大。如果可能，您不应使用张紧轮，尤其是外侧张紧轮。但是，如果必须使用张紧轮，则外侧张紧轮的直径应至少为 1.33·d，内侧张紧轮的直径应至少为 1.0·d（d：较小带轮的直径）。

每个制造商提供的张紧轮信息略有不同。

English Original

Tensioning pulley diameter

The tensioning pulley diameter should be at least as big as the smallest belt sheave. If at all possible, you should not use tensioning pulleys, in particular outside tensioning pulleys. However, if you have to use a tensioning pulley, its diameter should be at least 1.33·d if it is an outside pulley or 1.0·d if it is an inside pulley (d: diameter of the smaller sheave).

Every manufacturer provides slightly different information about tensioning pulleys.
