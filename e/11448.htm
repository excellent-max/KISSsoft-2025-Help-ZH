中文翻译

附加几何计算，外部和内部

如果选择了差动齿轮的扩展项，则在位置 Li 和 Le 处计算几何参数。这两个位置的虚拟圆柱齿轮齿部数据也会记录在报告中。齿顶修整随后也可以一直应用到锥长以下。

English Original

Additional geometry calculations, external and internal

If the extensions for differential gears are selected, the geometry parameters are calculated at positions Li and Le. The data for the virtual cylindrical gear toothing at these two positions is then also documented in the report. The tip alteration can then also be applied up to underneath the cone length.
