中文翻译

齿数

齿数默认为整数。您也可以输入带小数的齿数（参见 带小数的齿数输入 一章）。对于内啮合齿轮，必须按照 ISO 21771 的规定将齿数输入为负值。对于小齿轮-齿圈内啮合齿轮副，中心距也必须输入为负值（例如 z1 = 20, z2 = -35, a = -7.5, mn = 1）。

最小齿数受到几何误差的限制，例如根切或齿顶齿厚。例如，对于无变位的直齿轮，当齿数少于 17 时就会发生根切。

English Original

Number of teeth

The number of teeth is, by default, a whole number. You can also enter the number of teeth as an amount with values after the decimal place (see chapter Input of number of teeth with decimal places). For internal toothed gears, you must enter the number of teeth as a negative value as stated in ISO 21771. For a pinion-ring internal gear pair, the center distance must also be entered as a negative value (e.g. z1 = 20, z2 = -35, a = -7.5, mn = 1).

The minimum number of teeth is limited by geometric errors such as undercut or tooth thickness at the tip. For example, if there are fewer than 17 teeth undercut will occur on spur gears without profile shifts.
