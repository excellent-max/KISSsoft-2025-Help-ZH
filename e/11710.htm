English Original

Gear meshing damping

Gear meshing damping is known as an important determinative factor in the dynamic response of the system under certain running conditions. With the viscous damping, the effect of lubricant film as well as the internal damping of the gear teeth in dissipating the kinetic energy into frictional heat and constraining the motion amplitudes is modeled. On the other hand, without damping, undesirable induced vibrations generate noise, increase dynamic loads and potentially damage gears and bearings.
