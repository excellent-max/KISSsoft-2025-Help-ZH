中文翻译

刀盘半径

对于弧齿锥齿轮，刀盘半径 rc0 的大小会影响齿面曲率，从而也影响锥齿轮副的性能。该效应既适用于接触区位置，也适用于强度，在计算端面系数 KFa（根据 ISO 10300）时必须予以考虑。

注意

如果您使用 Klingelnberg 方法计算强度，则不会出现该参数。在这种情况下，您可以同时选择刀盘半径与机床类型。

English Original

Cutter radius

In the case of spiral teeth bevel gears, the size of the cutter radius rc0 influences the curvature of the flanks and therefore also the properties of the bevel gear pair. This effect applies both to the position of the contact pattern and the strength, and must be taken into account when calculating the transverse coefficient KFa according to ISO 10300.

Note

This parameter is not present if you use the Klingelnberg method to calculate strength. In that case, you select the cutter radius together with the machine type.
