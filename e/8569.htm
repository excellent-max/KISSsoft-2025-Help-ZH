几何详情

单击几何区域右上方的 详细信息... 按钮以显示 定义几何细节 对话框窗口。您可以在此处输入这些参数。

锥齿轮小齿轮的 V、H 和 J 向不对中是系统数据，用于计算接触区点。

图 16.6：用于计算接触区点的锥齿轮小齿轮不对中

您可以为每个齿轮指定图纸编号和内径。必须考虑尺寸 yo、yu 和安装距（参见"分锥顶点到齿坯前端与后端/安装距"章）的数据。

English Original

Geometry details

Click the Details... button in the upper right-hand part of the Geometry area to display the Define details of geometry dialog window. You can enter these parameters here.

The V-, H- and J misalignments of the bevel gear pinion are system data and are used to calculate the contact pattern.

Figure 16.6: Misalignment of the bevel gear pinion for calculating the contact pattern

You can specify the drawing number and the internal diameter for each gear. The data for dimensions yo, yu and the mounting distance (see chapter Pitch apexes to front and back of gear blank/mounting distance) must be taken into account.
