中文翻译

带准渐开线轮齿的锥齿轮

根据 Klingelnberg 工艺对锥齿轮进行几何形状和强度计算。

根据Klingelnberg KN 3025内部标准（几何，第10版）和KN 3030（强度计算）对准渐开线轮齿进行完整计算。

  • 通过指定小直径dK和铣刀切削长度SF来考虑准渐开线铣刀尺寸。用户也可以在此输入特殊铣刀。
  • 如果刀盘在齿的内端或外端未覆盖轮冠，则会显示警告。
  • 可选择任何轴交角，或角度修正
  • 整体几何、模数（内、中、外）、螺旋角（内、中、外）、检查平衡滑动和根切空间的齿廓变位量、检查并计算内径上必要的齿顶缩短量、齿廓和重合度、齿形系数和应力修正系数。
  • 所有轮齿尺寸的计算
  • 计算接触印痕核心在基准锥距 Rpr 和 Rm 时的力
  • 计算所有 Klingelnberg 标准 KN 3030 的修形的点蚀、齿根和胶合载荷能力（按积分温度标准定义），考虑在锥距 Rpr 处的力

注意

在锥距 Rm 处的力用于传递到 KISSsys，以确保力可以独立于齿形工艺进行计算。然而，将理论接触印痕核心纳入 Klingelnberg 内部标准在制造过程中实施起来非常困难。

English Original

Bevel gears with palloid gear teeth

Geometry and strength calculation of bevel gears according to the Klingelnberg process.

A complete calculation for palloid gear teeth is performed according to the Klingelnberg KN 3025 in-house standard (Geometry, Edition No. 10) and KN 3030 (strength calculation).

  • Take into account palloid milling cutter dimensions by specifying a small diameter dK and milling cutter cut length SF. You can also input special milling cutters here.
  • A warning is displayed if the cutter does not cover the crown wheel at either the inner or outer end of the tooth
  • You can select any shaft angle, or angle modifications
  • Overall geometry, modules (inside, middle, outside), spiral angle (inside, middle, outside), checks on profile shift for balanced sliding and undercut space, checking and calculating the necessary tip shortening on the internal diameter, profile and overlap ratio, tooth form factor and stress correction factor
  • Calculation of all toothing dimensions
  • Calculate forces for contact pattern core for reference cone length Rpr and Rm
  • Calculate pitting, tooth root and scuffing load capacity (as defined by the integral temperature criterion) for all modifications in the Klingelnberg standard KN 3030 (taking into account the forces at cone distance Rpr)

Note

The forces at cone distance Rm are used for the transfer to KISSsys, to ensure that forces can be calculated independently of the toothing procedure. However, including the theoretical contact pattern core in the Klingelnberg in-house standard is very difficult to implement in the manufacturing process.
