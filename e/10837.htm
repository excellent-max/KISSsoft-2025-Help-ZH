中文翻译

为弯曲和扭转输入不同的载荷循环次数（用于有限寿命计算）

每次轴转动时，弯曲载荷循环会变化。因此，弯曲载荷循环的数量是根据额定寿命和速度计算的。扭转载荷循环的数量通常要低得多，因为并非每次转动都会引起扭转载荷循环。例如，一个齿轮装置可能在早上启动并在一天中以恒定扭矩运行，导致每天恰好有一个扭转载荷循环。相比之下，一个以 1000 rpm 运行的轴在8小时内会受到8000个弯曲载荷循环的影响。因此，在这个例子中，弯曲载荷循环与扭转载荷循环的数量比为8000:1。您可以在此处输入这个比例。

English Original

Input different numbers of load cycles for bending and torsion (for limited life calculations)

Every time a shaft rotates, the bending load cycle changes. For this reason, the number of bending load cycles is calculated using the rating life and the speed. The number of torsional load cycles is often very much lower, because not every rotation causes a torsional load cycle. For example, a gear unit may be started in the morning and run throughout the day with a constant torque, resulting in exactly one torsional load cycle per day. In contrast, a shaft running at 1000 rpm for 8 hours would be subject to 8000 bending load cycles in the same space of time. As a consequence, in this example, the ratio between the number of bending load cycles and torsion would be 8000: 1. You can enter this ratio here.
