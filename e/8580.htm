中文翻译

压力角驱动/从动齿面：准双曲面齿轮

当小齿轮以凹齿面驱动时，锥齿轮通常能更好地承受应力，即当驱动小齿轮的旋转方向与螺旋方向一致时。

小齿轮的凹齿面通常称为驱动齿面（索引 D 表示“Drive”），凸齿面称为从动齿面（索引 C 表示“Coast”）。在锥齿轮上，凹齿面是从动齿面（索引 C），凸齿面是驱动齿面（索引 D）。由于驱动齿面上的有效压力角大于法向截面中的压力角，其差值为极限压力角，而从动齿面上的有效压力角小于法向截面中的压力角，其差值也为极限压力角，因此驱动齿面和从动齿面上的压力角可以独立输入。

按 ISO 23509 的规定，对于准双曲面齿轮，应输入名义设计压力角 αdD、αdC。由此计算驱动侧（索引 D 表示“Drive”）的生成压力角 αnD 和有效压力角 αeD，以及从动侧（索引 C 表示“Coast”）的生成压力角 αnC 和有效压力角 αeC。

ISO 23509 中规定的方程为：

αnD = αdD + fαlim * αlim

αeD = αnD - αlim

因此，如果 αnD 已知，则 adD 可按如下方式计算：

αdD = αnD - fαlim * αlim

αdC = αnC + fαlim * αlim

或者，如果已指定 αeD，则 αdD 可按如下方式计算：

αdD = αeD + αlim * (1- fαlim)

αdC = αeC - αlim * (1- fαlim)

极限压力角 αlim 在报告中进行计算并输出。

引入极限压力角影响系数 fαlim，是为了在计算刀具齿面角时不必总是考虑极限压力角的总值。对于成形刀具（Klingelnberg 工艺），fαlim = 0。如果使用恒定槽宽的加工方法（Gleason），则设置 fαlim = 0.5，否则通常使用 fαlim = 1.0。

然而，如果没有更准确的数据可用，您可以在计算中使用法向截面中的压力角（取 αdD = αdC = αn 且 fαlim = 1.0）。

注意

这些输入字段仅在计算准双曲面齿轮强度时可用（参见"强度计算所用方法"章）。

English Original

Pressure angle driving/driven flank: Hypoid gears

Bevel gears are usually able to withstand stress better when driven by the concave pinion flank, i.e. when the sense of rotation and spiral direction of the driving pinion rotate in the same direction.

The concave flank of the pinion is usually called the driving flank (index D for "Drive"), and the convex flank is known as the driven flank (index C for "Coast"). On the bevel gear, the concave flank is the driven flank (index C) and the convex flank is the driving flank (index D). Since the effective pressure angle on the driving flank is greater by the amount of the limit pressure angle, and on the driven flank it is less than the pressure angle in a normal section, by the amount of the limit pressure angle, the pressure angle on the driving flank and driven flank can be entered independently.

As specified in ISO 23509, you should input the nominal design pressure angle for hypoid gears as αdD, αdC. This is used to calculate the generated pressure angle ("effective pressure angle") αnD, αnC for the driving side (index D for "Drive") and the effective pressure angle αeD, αeC for the driven side (index C for "Coast").

The equations specified in ISO 23509 are:

αnD = αdD + fαlim * αlim

αeD = αnD - αlim

If, as a result, αnD is known, adD can be calculated as follows:

αdD = αnD - fαlim * αlim

αdC = αnC + fαlim * αlim

or if αeD has been specified, αdD can be calculated like this:

αdD = αeD + αlim * (1- fαlim)

αdC = αeC - αlim * (1- fαlim)

The limit pressure angle αlim is calculated and output in the report.

The limit pressure angle influence factor fαlim has been introduced so that you do not always need to take the total value of the limit pressure angle into consideration when calculating the flank angle on the tool. For forming tools (Klingelnberg process), fαlim = 0. If you use the procedure with a constant slot width (Gleason), fαlim = 0.5 is set, otherwise fαlim = 1.0 is often used.

However, if more accurate data is not available, you can use the pressure angle in the normal section in the calculation (with αdD = αdC = αn and fαlim = 1.0).

Note

These input fields are only available if you are calculating the strength of hypoid gears (see chapter Methods used for strength calculation).
