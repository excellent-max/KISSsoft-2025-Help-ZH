中文翻译

公差标准

  • 文件名： 数据库条目引用外部表（参见 外部表）。公差表以 K10-???.dat 开头。

根据 DIN EN ISO 286 标准的公差已直接编入 KISSsoft 中。对于配合（公差）等级 H、h、JS 和 js，公差已扩展至公称长度 10,000 mm（根据标准，最大为 3,150 mm）。这些值是通过外推确定的。

English Original

Tolerances standard

  • File name: The database entries refer to external tables (see chapter External tables). Tables for tolerances begin with K10-???.dat.

The tolerances according to DIN EN ISO 286 have been programmed directly into KISSsoft. For fit (tolerance) classes H, h, JS and js, the tolerance has been extended up to the nominal length 10,000 mm (according to the standard, up to 3,150 mm). The values were determined by extrapolation.
