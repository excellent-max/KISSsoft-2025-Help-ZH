制造类型

对于这两种工艺，可以选择“展成”或“成形”作为精度等级。在传动比大于 2.5 的齿轮副中，锥齿轮（齿轮 2）通常仅“成形”。小齿轮（齿轮 1）始终为展成。在 cyclo-palloid® 轮齿中，小齿轮和锥齿轮始终为展成。


螺旋齿的制造商数据： 端面铣削与端面滚切

用于制造带螺旋齿锥齿轮的工艺与该制造工艺密切相关。这里使用两种基本工艺。圆弧齿制工艺（端面铣削，传统上称为 Gleason 工艺）和连续端面滚切（端面滚切，传统上称为 Klingelnberg 和 Oerlikon 工艺）。有关更多详细信息，请参见计算工艺（参见"根据 Klingelnberg、Gleason 和 Oerlikon 的计算"章）。

English Original

Manufacture type

Either "generated" or "formate" can be selected as the accuracy grade for the two processes. In gear sets with ratios greater than 2.5, the bevel gear (gear 2) is usually only "formate". The pinion (gear 1) is always generated. In a cyclo-palloid® toothing, the pinion and bevel gear are always generated.


Manufacturer's data for spiral teeth: Face Milling and Face Hobbing

The process used to manufacture bevel gears with spiral teeth is closely linked to this manufacturing process. There are two basic processes used here. The arc of circle toothing process (face milling, traditionally known as the Gleason process) and the continuous face hobbing (face hobbing, traditionally known as the Klingelnberg and Oerlikon process). For more details, see Calculation process (see chapter Calculation according to Klingelnberg, Gleason and Oerlikon).
