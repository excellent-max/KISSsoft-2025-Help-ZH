中文翻译

螺栓：强度等级

  • 注释： 供用户自行使用的文本字段。
  • 可定义值： 屈服点 Rp [N/mm2], 拉伸强度 Rm [N/mm2], 剪切强度比 ιb/Rm[-]

注意：

强度等级 8.8 和 SAE J429 等级 2 和 5 在直径下限处的屈服点和拉伸强度始终在数据库中显示。如果直径大于直径限值，程序会进行修正。

剪切强度比值根据强度等级从 VDI 2230 (2015) 表 5.5/2 中获取。未定义的 SAE 等级设置为比值 0.6。

English Original

Bolts: Strength grade

  • Comment: Text field for your own use.
  • Definable values: Yield point Rp [N/mm2], tensile strength Rm [N/mm2], shearing strength ratio ιb/Rm[-]

Note:

The yield point and tensile strength for the lower diameter limit for strength classes 8.8 and SAE J429 Grades 2 and 5 are always displayed in the database. If the diameter is greater than the diameter limit, this is corrected in the program.

The shearing strength ratios have been taken from Table 5.5/2 in VDI 2230 (2015) according to the strength classes. Undefined SAE classes are set to the ratio 0.6.
