中文翻译

学生版

  1. 将许可证文件（通常由您进行资格认证学习的机构提供）复制到许可证目录（参见[SETUP] 中的定义一一一章）。
  2. 选择许可证工具（在附加菜单中），然后单击"激活许可证"选项卡。
  3. 要输入在线代码（通常由您进行资格认证学习的机构提供），请选择激活测试版或学生版（需要在线代码）选项，然后单击激活许可证。
English Original

Student version

  1. Copy your license file (which you will usually be given by the place you are studying for your qualification) to your License directory (see chapter Definitions in [SETUP]).
  2. Select License tool in the Extras menu, and then click on the Activate license tab.
  3. To input your online code (which you will usually be given by the place you are studying for your qualification), select the Activate test or student version (online code required) option and then click on Activate license.
