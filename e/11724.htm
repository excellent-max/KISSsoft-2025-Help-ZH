计算

KISSsoft 通过考虑关键的和定义的公差组合，对每种情况（最小、定义、最大）执行全面的计算。它生成最终齿形以及重要参数，例如：

  • 齿顶形状直径
  • 齿根形状直径
  • 最大根切直径
  • 最大根切量
  • 不同截面处的齿厚

Calculation

KISSsoft performs comprehensive calculations for each condition (minimum, defined, maximum) by considering critical and defined tolerance combinations. It generates the final tooth form along with important parameters such as:

  • Tip form diameter
  • Root form diameter
  • Maximum undercut diameter
  • Maximum undercut amount
  • Tooth thickness at different sections

For each case (minimum, defined, maximum) different conditions are being taken into account. These conditions are presented in the following tables, for gears with pre-machining (see Table 15.22) and for gears without pre-machining (see Table 15.23).
