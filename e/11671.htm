中文翻译

根据 FVA 271 报告计算硬化层深度系数

根据 FVA 271:2001 [28]，如果齿轮材料选择渗碳钢，则可以使用两个与硬化深度相关的系数 ZCHD 和 YCHD，前提是计算不根据 AGMA 进行。

这些系数被限制在 0.5 ≤ ZCHD,YCHD ≤ 1.0 的范围内。根据 FVA 271 的计算考虑了在 550 HV 硬度下用户定义的硬化深度范围。然后将该范围与最佳硬化深度范围进行比较，后者的极限值（CHDoptimalmin / CHDoptimalmax）根据 FVA 271 计算。

由于要比较两个范围（用户通过 550 HV 硬度下的硬化深度输入的范围和根据 FVA 271 计算的范围），因此实现了以下行为：

  1. 如果两个用户输入值都小于 CHDoptimalmin，则系数 ZCHD 和 YCHD 根据方程 33 和 38 [28] 计算，并考虑两个硬化深度值中较小的一个。
  2. 如果两个用户输入值都大于 CHDoptimalmax，则系数 ZCHD 和 YCHD 根据方程 35 和 40 [28] 计算，并考虑两个硬化深度值中较大的一个。
  3. 如果用户输入值介于 CHDoptimalmin 和 CHDoptimalmax 之间，则系数 ZCHD 和 YCHD 等于 1。
  4. 如果一个用户输入值小于/大于 CHDoptimal，则系数 ZCHD 和 YCHD 根据方程 33 和 38 以及方程 35 和 40 [28] 计算，并在强度计算中使用较小的值。
English Original

Calculating the case hardening depth factor according to FVA 271 report

According to FVA 271:2001 [28], two hardening depth-dependent factors ZCHD and YCHD can be used if case hardening steels are selected for the gear material and if the calculation is not performed according to AGMA.

The factors are limited to the range 0.5 ≤ ZCHD,YCHD ≤ 1.0. The calculation according to FVA 271 considers the user-defined range of the hardening depth at the hardness of 550 HV. This range is then compared to the range of the optimal hardening depth, for which the limits (CHDoptimalmin / CHDoptimalmax) are calculated according to FVA 271.

As there is the comparison of two ranges (user input through case hardening depth at 550 HV and the one calculated according to FVA 271), the following behavior is implemented:

  1. If both user input values are smaller than CHDoptimalmin, the factors ZCHD and YCHD are calculated according to equations 33 and 38 [28], considering the smaller of the two hardening depth values.
  2. If both user input values are larger than CHDoptimalmax, the factors ZCHD and YCHD are calculated according to equations 35 and 40 [28], taking into account the larger of the two hardening depth values.
  3. If the user input values are between CHDoptimalmin and CHDoptimalmax, the factors ZCHD and YCHD are equal to 1.
  4. If one user input value is smaller/larger than CHDoptimal, the factors ZCHD and YCHD are calculated according to both equations 33 and 38 and equations 35 and 40 [28], using the smaller value in the strength calculation.
