中文翻译

图形

提供以下图形：

1. 载荷分布
这显示了滚动轴承（球/滚子）上的载荷分布。对于推力轴承，使用反作用力的大小进行绘图。

2. 变形（弹性环）
显示内圈和外圈的径向和轴向变形。
3. 滚道上的应力分布
显示内圈和外圈滚道上的应力分布。

4. 压力曲线
这展示了压力沿每个滚子长度上的分布，或在球轴承的每一个接触点上的分布。

5. 每个滚动体的压力曲线
这显示了每个滚子元件沿滚子型线上的压力曲线。

6. 刚度曲线
这显示了轴承的力-位移曲线。径向和轴向刚度均有显示。

7. 轴承频率
这显示了根据每个轴承的相对速度计算出的轴承频率（外圈过滚频率、内圈过滚频率、滚动体旋转频率和保持架频率）。

English Original

Graphics

The following graphics are provided:

1. Load distribution
This shows the load distribution on the rolling bearings (balls/rollers). For thrust bearings, the magnitude of the reaction force is used for the plot.

2. Deformation (elastic rings)
Shows the radial and axial deformation of the inner and outer ring.
3. Stress distribution on raceway
Shows the stress distribution on the inner and outer raceways.

4. Pressure curve
This shows how the pressure develops along the length of each roller, or at every contact point in a ball bearing.

5. Pressure curve for each rolling body
This shows the pressure curve on each roller element along the roller profile.

6. Stiffness curve
This shows the force-displacement curve of the bearing. Both radial and axial stiffness are shown.

7. Bearing frequencies
This shows the calculated bearing frequencies (over-rolling frequency of the outer ring, over-rolling frequency of the inner ring, rotational frequency of the rolling elements and the cage frequency) depending on the relative speed for each bearing.
