键

键是迄今为止最常用的轴毂连接。特别是，它们有助于传递扭矩。键的几何形状早已按 DIN 6885 [82] 标准化。但是，为确保传递扭矩时达到足够的安全水平，您始终需要查阅二级技术文献资料 [8]。DIN 标准 6892 [83] 记录了可用于平键连接的不同计算方法。

您必须对 键连接执行 两项检查：

  1. 通过监测轴、轮毂和键上的表面压力来检查扭矩传递。
  2. 检查由于键槽引起的缺口效应对轴耐久极限的影响。该影响已在 DIN 743 [63] 中描述。我们建议您使用该标准（而非 DIN 6892）来分析轴的强度。

按 DIN 6892 计算的特殊特性：

  • 键连接通常与轻过盈配合相结合。计算考虑了由于过盈配合导致的键上扭矩的减小。
  • 该计算验证了整个运行期间的公称转矩以及实际峰值扭矩。基于公称转矩的耐久极限计算还包括载荷变化次数，经验表明，载荷变化次数对键具有显著且有害的影响。
  • 载荷的施加或卸载方式对键的使用安全性有很大影响。通过使用各种载荷分布系数来考虑这一影响。
  • 许用压力值由屈服点推导得出。因此，您可以按标准规定，为常见和较不常见的材料推导该值。硬度影响系数用于考虑表面处理。

计算方法 B 按 DIN 6892 定义，建议您使用差异化的计算方法来验证键连接的使用可靠性。方法 C 已大大简化。

English Original

Keys

Keys are by far the most commonly used shaft-hub connections. In particular, they help to transmit the torque. Their geometry has long been standardized according to DIN 6885 [82]. However, to ensure adequate safety levels are achieved when transmitting torque, you always had to contact secondary sources of technical literature [8]. The DIN standard 6892 [83] documents the different calculation methods that can be used for feather key connections.

You must perform two checks for key connections:

  1. Check the torque transmission by monitoring surface pressure on the shaft, hub and key.
  2. Check the endurance limit of the shaft due to the notch effect caused by the key way. This effect is already described in DIN 743 [63]. We recommend you use this standard to analyze the shaft strength rather than DIN 6892.

Special characteristics of calculations according to DIN 6892:

  • Key connections are usually combined with a light interference fit. The calculation takes into account the decrease in torque on the key due to the interference fit.
  • The calculation proves the nominal torque as well as the actual pitch torque over the entire operating period. The endurance limit calculation based on the nominal torque also includes the number of load changes, which experience has shown to have a significant and damaging effect on the key.
  • The way in which load is applied or discharged has a considerable effect on the operating safety of keys. This effect is taken into account by using a wide range of load distribution coefficients.
  • The values for the permitted pressure are derived from the yield point. As a result, you can derive this for common and more unusual materials as specified in the standard. The hardness influence coefficient is used to take the surface treatment into account.

Calculation Method B as defined in DIN 6892 recommends you use a differentiated calculation method to prove the service reliability of key connections. Method C has been greatly simplified.
