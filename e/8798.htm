中文翻译

线性齿廓修形

在线性齿廓修形中，齿厚从起始直径到齿顶按线性变化减小（作为齿厚修形，每个齿面的修缘量 Ca）。

图 15.77：线性齿廓修形

English Original

Linear profile modification

In a linear profile modification, the tooth thickness is reduced in a linear progression from the starting diameter to the tip (relief Ca on each flank as a tooth thickness modification).
