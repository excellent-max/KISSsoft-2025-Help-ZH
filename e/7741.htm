中文翻译

转速/齿数/传动比

传动比范围：

有利

i = 1. . . 5,

良好

i = 1. . . 7,

不利

i > 10.

齿数：
由于多边形效应，建议最少齿数为 17 . . . 25。少于 17 的"齿数"仅用于传递低功率。链传动中使用的优选齿数，如 DIN ISO 606 所述，为：17、19、21、23、25、38、57、76、95、114。

张紧轮应至少使用三个齿。

English Original

Speed/number of teeth/ratio

Range of ratio:

favorable

i = 1. . . 5,

good

i = 1. . . 7,

unfavorable

i > 10.

Number of teeth:
Due to the polygon effect, we recommend a minimum number of teeth of 17 . . . 25. A "Number of teeth" that is fewer than 17 should only be used to produce low levels of power. The preferred numbers of teeth for use in chain gears, as stated in DIN ISO 606, are: 17, 19, 21, 23, 25, 38, 57, 76, 95, 114.

You should use at least three teeth for tensioning pulleys.
