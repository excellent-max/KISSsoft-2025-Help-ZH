中文翻译

软件功能

计算程序提供检验点 W 处静强度和疲劳强度的书面完整性证明。

强度验证按照局部应力概念提供，如 FKM 指南“Rechnerischer Festigkeitsnachweis für Maschinenbauteile”中所述。局部应力概念背后的思想是：根据零件临界点处的弹塑性局部应力，与由无缺口试样得到的 S-N 曲线（Woehler 曲线）进行比较，来估算使用寿命。局部概念在 FKM 指南框架内实现为基于应力的变体。因此，在使用之前，材料必须处于线弹性状态。在这种背景下，所采用的概念实际上并不是像弹塑性缺口根部应变概念那样的局部概念，而是一种接近名义应力概念的概念，只是理论应力集中系数位于方程的另一侧。它是计算高周疲劳范围内静强度证明和疲劳强度证明的有用工具（NN > 104）。

输入：您可以输入检验点 W 和相邻点 B 处的应力。或者，通过数学方法估算检验点处的应力和加强。在计算设计系数之前，您还必须指定表面粗糙度、零件尺寸等参数。附加载荷数据（如循环次数、载荷谱、温度等）也已预先定义。

输出：计算会得出静态工况和疲劳工况的利用率系数，并为此生成一套完整的文档。

English Original

Software functionality

The calculation program supplies a complete, written proof of integrity for static and fatigue strength at the proof point W.

The strength verification is supplied according to the local stress concept as described in the FKM "Rechnerischer Festigkeitsnachweis für Maschinenbauteile" Guideline. The idea behind the local stress concept is to estimate the service life on the basis of the elastic-plastic local stress at the critical point on the part compared to the S-N curve (Woehler lines) elongation derived from an unnotched test bar. The local concept is implemented as a stress-based variant within the framework of the FKM Guideline. Therefore, before it can be used, the material must be in a linear elastic state. In this context, the concept used is not really a local concept like the elastic-plastic notch root strain concept, but a concept close to the nominal stress concept, except that only the theoretical stress concentration factor stands on the other side of the equations. It is a useful tool for calculating a static proof and proof of fatigue strength in the high cycle range (NN > 104).

Input: You can enter the stresses at a proof point W and at a neighboring point B. Alternatively, the stresses at the proof point and the stiffening are estimated mathematically. You must also specify parameters such as surface roughness, part size, etc., before you can calculate the design coefficients. Additional load data, such as number of cycles, spectrum, temperature etc. are also predefined.

Output: The calculation calculates the utilization factors for static cases and fatigue. It creates a complete set of documentation for this.
