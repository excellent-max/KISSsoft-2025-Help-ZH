中文翻译

结果

所有解决方案都显示在结果选项卡中。然后您可以选择最符合您要求的解决方案。点击“接受”或双击该解决方案，即可将其数据传输到修改选项卡。

结果概览中最重要的结果：

  • 编号：解决方案 ID。可使用此 ID 在报告中搜索更多结果详情。
  • wt：计算解决方案的局部载荷（%）（取决于在“局部载荷步骤数”字段中指定的迭代步骤数），例如相对于在基本数据选项卡中定义的名义载荷的 50% 局部载荷。
  • H: 齿轮副实现的最小使用寿命（小时）。
  • PPTE: 从动齿轮沿从动齿轮的啮合线的传动误差振幅。
  • rel. PPTE: 传动误差/转角误差的相对振幅，相对于未修正齿形。
  • PPFE: 从动齿轮沿从动齿轮的啮合线的激励力误差振幅。
  • εa：载荷下的端面重合度
  • εβ：载荷下的纵向重合度
  • εν：载荷下的总重合度
  • KHβ：端面载荷因子（如果计算使用载荷谱，只有最后一个载荷分箱的端面载荷因子才会显示。）
  • σHmax：齿轮轮齿上出现的最大赫兹压力
  • Sλ：按ISO TR 6336-22 方法A规定的微点蚀安全性
  • η：效率
  • ΔWnA/B：齿轮A/B的磨损
  • ΔT: 从动齿轮的扭矩振幅
  • 及其他...
English Original

Results

All the solutions are displayed in the Results tab. You can then select the solution that best suits your requirements. Click on Accept or double-click on the solution to transfer its data to the Modifications tab.

The most important results in the result overview:

  • No: solution ID. You can use this ID to search for more details about the results in the reports.
  • wt: The partial load of the calculated solution in % (depending on the number of iteration steps specified in the "Number of steps for partial load" field), e.g. 50% partial load relative to the nominal load defined in the Basic data tab.
  • H: The minimum service life achieved by the gear pair in hours.
  • PPTE: Transmission error amplitude of the driven gear along the driven gear's path of contact.
  • rel. PPTE: Relative amplitude of the transmission error/angle of rotation error relative to the uncorrected toothing.
  • PPFE: Amplitude of the excitation force error of the driven gear along the driven gear's path of contact.
  • εa: Transverse contact ratio under load
  • εβ: Overlap ratio under load
  • εν: Total contact ratio under load
  • KHβ: Face load factor (If the calculation is performed with load spectra, only the face load factor for the last load bin is ever displayed.)
  • σHmax: Maximum Hertzian pressure that occurs in the gear teeth
  • Sλ: Safety against micropitting as specified in ISO TR 6336-22 Method A
  • η: efficiency
  • ΔWnA/B: Wear on gear A/B
  • ΔT: Torque amplitude of the driven gear
  • and others...
