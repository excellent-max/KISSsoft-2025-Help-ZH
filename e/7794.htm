中文翻译

极限值

以下扭杆端部极限比适用于扭杆弹簧：矩形、齿形：df/d > 1.3；六角形 df/d > 1.25
DIN 标准中 10-60mm 杆径的强度值适用。
头部长度参考值为 0.5 至 1.5 d。
比值 Rh/d 应介于 1 和 50 之间。

English Original

Limiting values

The following limit ratios for torsion bar tips apply to torsion bar springs: rectangular, toothed: df/d > 1.3; hexagonal df/d > 1.25
The strength values from the DIN standard for bar diameters of 10-60mm apply.
The guide value is a head length between 0.5·and 1.5 d.
The ratio Rh/d should lie between 1 and 50.
