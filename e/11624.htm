中文翻译

SKF 云服务：使用 SKF 轴承模块

使用 SKF 轴承模块进行计算：

  • 对于大多数 SKF 轴承，以下轴承性能结果通过 SKF 云服务计算：
    • SKF 额定寿命 (L10m)
    • 基本额定寿命 (L10)
    • 当量动载荷 (P) 与载荷比 C/P
    • 最小载荷
    • 黏度比 (κ) / 污染系数 (eC) / 寿命修正系数 (aSKF)
    • 摩擦与功率损失
    • 润滑脂寿命与再润滑间隔
    • 轴承频率
    • 修正参考转速
  • 与 SKF 轴承数据库直接连接，确保轴承数据准确且最新。
  • SKF 额定寿命充分考虑了 SKF Explorer 轴承的优势。一般而言，SKF 额定寿命比按 ISO 281 计算的额定寿命更接近实际，尤其是对 SKF Explorer 轴承而言。
  • SKF 额定寿命结果向下取整到 100 小时。

要求

  • 需要注册。要注册，请使用 SKF 注册工具（在 附加功能 菜单中）。
  • 需要互联网连接才能连接到 SKF 服务器。

限制

  • SKF 轴承模块不支持以下轴承：
    • 非 SKF 品牌轴承
    • SKF 超精密轴承
    • 用于极端温度的 SKF 轴承
  • 对于这些轴承，所有结果均通过 KISSsoft 中的标准方法计算。
  • 轴承性能结果按上文所列方式计算。所有其他轴承性能结果均通过 KISSsoft 中的标准方法计算。

附加信息

关于 SKF 额定寿命的更多信息可参见 此处。关于 SKF 额定寿命与按 ISO 281 计算的额定寿命之间差异的更多信息可参见 此处。如果您有任何其他问题或需要 SKF 提供支持，请联系 skfbearingmodule@skf.com。

English Original

SKF Cloud Services: using the SKF bearing module

Calculations with the SKF bearing module:

  • For most SKF bearings, the following bearing performance results are calculated via the SKF cloud service:
    • SKF rating life (L10m)
    • Basic rating life (L10)
    • Equivalent dynamic bearing load (P) & load ratio C/P
    • Minimum load
    • Viscosity ratio (κ) / contamination factor (eC) / life modification factor (aSKF)
    • Friction & power loss
    • Grease life & relubrication interval
    • Bearing frequencies
    • Adjusted reference speed
  • A direct connection is made to the SKF bearing database, ensuring accurate and up-to-date bearing data.
  • SKF rating life fully accounts for the benefits of SKF Explorer bearings. In general, the SKF rating life is more realistic than the rating life according to ISO 281, but in particular for the SKF Explorer bearings.
  • SKF rating life results are rounded down to 100 hours.

Requirements

  • Registration is required. To register, use the SKF Registration tool in the Extras menu.
  • Internet connection is required to connect to the SKF server.

Limitations

  • The SKF bearing module does not support the following bearings:
    • non-SKF branded bearings
    • SKF super-precision bearings
    • SKF bearings for extreme temperatures
  • For these bearings, all results are calculated via the standard methods in KISSsoft.
  • The bearing performance results are calculated as listed above. All other bearing performance results are calculated via the standard methods in KISSsoft.

Additional information

More information on the SKF rating life can be found here. More information on the difference between SKF rating life and rating life according to ISO 281 can be found here. If you have any additional questions or need support from SKF, please contact skfbearingmodule@skf.com.
