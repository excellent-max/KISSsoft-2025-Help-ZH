制造

此选项卡用于指定预加工和精加工的制造工艺。您还可以检查是否可以使用强力刮削或珩磨等特殊制造工艺。制造偏差（测量偏差、自然扭转、斜率和形状偏差）可以在表格中设置。
设置制造偏差后，即可利用轮齿接触分析功能确定其对啮合特性的影响。这提供了一种方法，用于确定制造偏差（根据指定的精度等级）对理论上最优尺寸的轮齿的影响。

English Original

Manufacturing

This tab is where you specify the manufacturing process for pre-machining and final machining. You can also check whether special manufacturing processes such as power skiving or honing can be used. Manufacturing deviations (measured deviations, natural twist, slope and form deviations) can be set in a table.
Setting manufacturing deviations enables the influence on the meshing properties to be determined, using the tooth contact analysis function. This provides a way to determine the effect of manufacturing deviations (according to the specified accuracy grade) on the theoretically optimally sized toothing.
