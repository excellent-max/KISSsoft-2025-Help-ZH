中文翻译

允许少量点蚀

在特定情况下，齿面上出现少量点蚀可能是允许的。在一系列材料中，由于应力-寿命曲线（Wöhler 线）的改变，在有限寿命范围内这会导致更高的齿面安全系数，如 ISO 6336-2 图 6 曲线 1 或 DIN 3990-2 图 8.1 所示。

English Original

Small amount of pitting permissible

In specific cases, the appearance of a slight amount of pitting on the flank may be permissible. In a range of materials, this results in higher flank safeties in the limited life range due to the changed S-N curve (Woehler lines), as can be seen in either ISO 6336-2, Figure 6, curve 1 or DIN 3990-2, Figure 8.1.
