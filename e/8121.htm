计算方法

您可以使用这四种方法之一来计算油润滑的流体动力滑动轴承：

  • a) 根据 G. Niemann, Maschinenelemente I, 1981, [8]。
    此方法非常适用于快速旋转的轴承。对于特殊结构类型（如可倾瓦或椭圆间隙滑动轴承），此方法也能得出相当好的结果。
    此方法根据 [8] 和 [71] 计算功率损失、油流量、油温和最小润滑间隙厚度。此计算仅适用于压力润滑轴承（循环润滑），并且还需检验使用可靠性。
  • b) 根据 ISO 7902 第 1 至 3 部分，2020 [69]。
    此方法非常适用于缓慢旋转的轴承。它还确定油耗、油流量和整个热平衡。
    根据 ISO 7902 第 1 至 3 部分（2020）对无压和压力润滑轴承进行计算。此方法考虑了润滑剂施加的方式（润滑油孔、润滑油槽、润滑脂嘴）。它根据 ISO 7902 计算所有运行数据，包括运行温度、最小润滑间隙宽度、功率损失、油流量等。它还检验使用可靠性。
  • c) 根据 DIN 31652 第 1 至 3 部分，2015，[72]。
    此方法非常适用于缓慢旋转的轴承。它还确定油耗、油流量和整个热平衡。
    根据 DIN 31652 第 1 至 3 部分（2015 版）对无压和压力润滑轴承进行计算。此方法考虑了润滑剂施加的方式（润滑油孔、润滑油槽、润滑脂嘴）。它根据 DIN 31652 计算所有运行数据，包括运行温度、最小润滑间隙宽度、功率损失、油流量等。它还检验使用可靠性。
  • d) 根据 DIN 31657 第 1-4 部分，1996，[70]。
    此方法非常适用于快速旋转的轴承。它还确定油耗、油流量和整个热平衡。该计算适用于多油叶滑动轴承和可倾瓦滑动轴承。
    根据 DIN 31657 第 1 至 4 部分（1996 版）对压力润滑轴承进行完整计算。它根据 DIN 31657 计算所有运行数据，包括运行温度、最小润滑间隙宽度、功率损失、油流量等。它还检验使用可靠性。
English Original

Calculation methods

You can use one of these four methods to calculate oil-lubricated, hydrodynamic plain journal bearings:

  • a) According to G. Niemann, Maschinenelemente I, 1981, [8].
    This method is very suitable for quickly rotating bearings. This also produces quite good results for special construction types such as tilting pad or oval-clearance plain bearings.
    This method calculates the power loss, oil flow, oil temperature, and minimum lubricant gap thickness, according to [8] and [71]. This calculation can only be used for pressure-lubricated bearings (circulatory lubrication) when the service reliability is also tested.
  • b) According to ISO 7902, Parts 1 to 3, 2020 [69].
    This method is very suitable for slowly rotating bearings. It also determines the oil consumption, the oil flow and the entire heat balance.
    Calculation according to ISO 7902, Parts 1 to 3 (2020) for pressure-less and pressure-lubricated bearings. This takes into account the way in which lubricant is applied (lubrication holes, lubrication groove, lubrication glands). It calculates all the operating data as defined in ISO 7902, including the operating temperature, minimum lubrication gap width, power loss, oil flow etc. It also checks service reliability.
  • c) According to DIN 31652, Parts 1 to 3, 2015, [72].
    This method is very suitable for slowly rotating bearings. It also determines the oil consumption, the oil flow and the entire heat balance.
    Calculation according to DIN 31652, Parts 1 to 3 (2015 Edition) for pressure-less and pressure-lubricated bearings. This takes into account the way in which lubricant is applied (lubrication holes, lubrication groove, lubrication glands). It calculates all the operating data as defined in DIN 31652, including the operating temperature, minimum lubrication gap width, power loss, oil flow etc. It also checks service reliability.
  • d) According to DIN 31657, Part 1-4, 1996, [70].
    This method is very suitable for quickly rotating bearings. It also determines the oil consumption, the oil flow and the entire heat balance. The calculation is suitable for multi-lobed plain bearings and tilting pad plain bearings.
    Complete calculation according to DIN 31657, Parts 1 to 4 (1996 Edition) for pressure-lubricated bearings. It calculates all the operating data according to DIN 31657, including the operating temperature, minimum lubrication gap width, power loss, oil flow etc. It also checks service reliability.
