中文翻译

传动精度等级数

AV（或 QV，适用于 AGMA 2001-C95 及更早版本）按 AGMA 2001 或 2101 中定义的公式计算，且高度依赖于精度等级（制造质量）。AV 允许比精度等级（制造质量）高一级或低一级，是计算动载系数所必需的。如有需要，您可以覆盖此值。

English Original

Transmission accuracy level number

AV (or QV for AGMA 2001-C95 or earlier) is calculated according to the formulae defined in AGMA 2001 or 2101 and is extremely dependent on the accuracy grade (manufacturing quality). AV is permitted to be one level higher or less than the accuracy grade (manufacturing quality) and is needed to calculate the dynamic factor. You can overwrite this value if required.
