中文翻译

计算过程

计算过程的第一步是选择 计算 > 受迫响应进入此专用计算。选择在计算过程中将改变转速的轴、相关转速范围以及计算步数。还需输入扭转、轴向和弯曲振动的材料（结构）阻尼。请注意，轴承的黏性阻尼必须针对每个轴承单独定义。为确保能够执行计算，轴上至少必须指定一个不平衡质量（“附加质量”元件，以及“质量”“偏心距”和“偏心质量的角位置”输入字段）。
请注意，只有在预定义的文档点处才能请求已完成计算的结果。在此情况下，文档点用作轴动态响应的测量探头。请注意，不同动态力产生的响应会在时域内相加，最终结果取该运算中找到的最大值。
除对参考轴转速范围进行计算外，还可以针对参考轴的特定运行转速执行计算，并确定沿其长度方向的动态响应结果。

English Original

Calculation procedure

The first step in the calculation procedure is to enter this special calculation by selecting Calculation > Forced response. Select the shaft whose speed will be changed during the calculation, the relevant speed range and the number of calculation steps, here. Another input is the material (structural) damping for torsional, axial and bending vibrations. Note that the viscous damping of bearings must be defined separately for each bearing. To ensure that the calculation can be performed, at least one unbalance mass in a shaft must be specified ("Additional mass" element, "Mass", "Eccentricity" and "Angular position of the eccentric mass" input fields).
Note that results of completed calculations can only be requested at predefined documentation points. The documentation points in this case are used as measurement probes for the dynamic response of the shafts. Note that the resulting responses from different dynamic forces are added in the time domain, and the final result is given based on the maximum value found during this operation.
Apart from the calculation for a range of speeds of the reference shaft, there is also an option for performing a calculation for a specific running speed of the reference shaft and determining the dynamic response results along its length.
