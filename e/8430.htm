中文翻译

计算基础原理

计算交叉轴斜齿轮（轴线交叉的圆柱齿轮）所用的方法在 Niemann [12] 中定义。当前版本描述了用于计算和校核任意轴交角的交叉轴斜齿轮几何尺寸的方法。用于校核和制造的量值通过算术方式确定。

尽管 Niemann [12] 中详述的方法被用于计算齿根和齿面强度以及胶合安全系数，但所用的各个方程均为 ISO 6336 中规定的方程。（Niemann 使用的是 DIN 3990 旧版中的方程。）

English Original

Underlying principles of calculation

The method used to calculate crossed helical gears (cylindrical gears with crossed axes) is defined in Niemann [12]. The current version describes the methods used to calculate and check the geometry of crossed helical gears for any axial crossing angle. The measures used for checking and fabrication are determined arithmetically.

Although the method detailed in Niemann [12] is used as an approach for calculating the root and flank strength and the scuffing safety, the individual equations used are those specified in ISO 6336. (Niemann uses the equations from an obsolete edition of DIN 3990.)
