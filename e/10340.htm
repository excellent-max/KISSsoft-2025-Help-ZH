中文翻译

边缘刚度降低

斜齿轮轮齿边缘处的法向齿厚减小，其作用是降低轮齿边缘处的抗弯刚度。

图15.91：齿轮（斜齿）的两个剖面示意图

如果选择 影响系数 刚度模型，则在模型内部计算折算刚度。

如果选择任一耦合刚度选项，则使用以下方程。

C_{red}=C\ ({S_{red} \over S_{n}})^{0.5}

指数 0.5 是在与 FEM 和 LVR 的对比分析中评估得出的。此指数的倒数（边界削弱因子（支撑作用））只有在用户未选择 影响系数 时才能由用户更改。它对斜齿轮轮齿中出现的支撑效应具有决定性影响。

English Original

Reduced stiffness on the edges

The reduced normal tooth thickness at the edge of a helical gear tooth then has the effect of reducing the bending stiffness of the tooth to the edges.

Figure 15.91: Illustration of two cuts for a gear with helical teeth

If the Influence numbers stiffness model is chosen, then the reduced stiffness is calculated internally within the model.

If either coupling stiffness option is chosen, then the following equation is used.

Exponent 0.5 was evaluated in comparative analyses with FEM and LVR. The reciprocal value of this exponent (border weakening factor (buttressing)) can be changed by the user only if Influence numbers is not chosen by the user. It has a decisive effect on the buttressing effect that occurs in helical gear teeth.
