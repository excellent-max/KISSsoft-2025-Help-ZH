中文翻译

与 ISO 10300 方法 B 类似

如前所述，您可以将 ISO 10300 用作计算锥齿轮强度的良好替代方法。端面齿轮被归类为锥齿轮，因此可视为锥角为 0°（小齿轮）和 90°（端面齿轮）的锥齿轮。锥齿轮的强度是基于当量圆柱齿轮（与锥齿轮具有相同齿形的圆柱齿轮）来计算的。然而，对于端面齿轮，小齿轮的当量齿数为 z1v = z1，而齿轮的当量齿数 z2v 为无穷大。如果您使用 Crown Gear 程序（与 DIN 3990 中定义的方法类似）和 ISO 10300 方法（在 KISSsoft 中）来验证示例，您将得到良好吻合的值。齿根和齿面安全系数的偏差小于 10%，通常小于 5%。这表明 DIN 3990 和 ISO 10300（DIN 3991）中的两种计算方法都是可靠且有效的。

English Original

Similar to ISO 10300, Method B

As already mentioned, you can use ISO 10300 as a good alternative method for calculating the strength of bevel gears. Face gears are classified as bevel gears and can therefore be regarded as bevel gears where the cone angle is 0° (pinion) and 90° (face gear). The strength of bevel gears is calculated on the basis of the virtual cylindrical gear (cylindrical gear with the same tooth form as the bevel gear). However, for a face gear the virtual gear number of teeth for the pinion is z1v = z1 and for the gear z2v it is infinite. If you verify the examples, using the Crown Gear program (similar method to the one defined in DIN 3990) and the ISO 10300 method in KISSsoft, you will get a good match of values. The deviation in root and flank safeties is less than 10% and usually less than 5%. This shows that both calculation methods in DIN 3990 and ISO 10300 (DIN 3991) are reliable and effective.
