齿廓修形的影响

齿廓修形是一个热门讨论话题。这些修形应该从哪里开始，应使用什么值来进行修形？

线性修缘是一种齿形修形。它具有以下特性：从一个特定点开始，从渐开线齿形部分到齿顶圆直径，去除的材料量逐渐增加。

当无载荷时，此修改区域的齿面之间没有接触。只有在存在合适载荷时才会发生接触。在计算接触长度以确定端面重合度 εa 时，会将该区域整体考虑在内。这难道不应该有所不同吗？

如果你使用齿形修形，你“删除”了真实的渐开线。为什么这是个好主意？

这是一个复杂的问题，必须在设计齿廓修形时予以考虑。去除的材料量（修缘 C a = 由于齿廓修形导致的齿厚减少量）必须根据齿的弯曲情况施加。

例如，如果齿具有无限刚度，并且忽略任何可能的补偿制造误差的效果，齿形修形将仅会降低端面重合度。这基本上适用于承受较低载荷的齿轮。然而，您通常需要根据工作扭矩和由此产生的齿变形来对齿轮进行尺寸设计。

如果修缘 Ca 尺寸合适，则齿廓修形可补偿齿弯曲，使齿整个高度上的齿接触不被削弱。在这种情况下，端面重合度不会减少。在此情况下，与无齿廓修形的齿轮相比，你得到的是在整个啮合过程中修改过的法向力曲线。

然而，最大力（在工况节圆上），其中仅有一个齿轮副啮合，未发生变化。因此，决定齿轮单元使用寿命的齿根和齿面最大应变保持不变。这种齿廓修形减少了啮合开始和结束时的法向力。这也会导致胶合风险显著降低。胶合风险源于接触应力和滑动速度。滑动在齿接触开始和结束时最大，因此通过减少该区域的接触应力，也可以降低胶合风险。齿廓修形可以减少齿应变对啮合过程中刚度波动的影响，因此限制了传动误差的数量。这也会降低振动和噪声的水平。

因此，如果齿廓修形已正确确定尺寸以适应齿轮箱的工作扭矩，端面重合度不会减少。然而，当涉及较低载荷时，经过齿廓修形的齿轮与未进行齿廓修形的齿轮啮合性能较差。这是由于端面重合度已显著减少。

Effect of profile modifications

Profile modifications are a popular topic of discussion. Where should these modifications start, and what values should be used to make these modifications?

Linear tip relief is a type of profile modification. It has the following properties: Starting from a particular point, ever increasing amounts of material are removed from the involute toothing part up to the tip diameter.

When there is no load, there is no contact between the tooth flanks in this modified area. Contact only occurs when suitable load is present. This entire area is taken into account when calculating the length of contact to determine the transverse contact ratio εa. Shouldn't this be different?

If you use profile modifications, you "delete" the real involute. Why is this a good idea?

This is a complex problem that must be taken into consideration when you design profile modifications. The amount of material removed (tip relief C a = the reduction in tooth thickness at the tip due to the profile modification) must be applied according to the tooth bending.

For example, if the tooth had infinite stiffness, and you ignored any of the possible effects of compensating for manufacturing errors, the profile modification would simply reduce the transverse contact ratio. This is basically correct for a gear that is subject to a lower load. However, you will usually need to size gears for optimum performance at the operating torque and the tooth deformation that then occurs.

If the tip relief Ca is properly sized, the profile modification then compensates for the tooth bending, so that the tooth contact across the entire tooth height is not compromised. In this case, the transverse contact ratio is not reduced. Here, when compared to a gear without profile modification, you have a modified normal force curve over the meshing.

However, the maximum force (in the operating pitch circle), where only one gear pair is meshing, is not changed. For this reason, the maximum root and flank strains, which determine the service life of the gear unit, remain unchanged. This profile modification reduces the normal force at the start and end of the meshing. This also leads to a significant reduction in the risk of scuffing. The risk of scuffing is due to contact stress and sliding velocity. Sliding is greatest at the start and end of the tooth contact, so, by reducing the contact stress in this area, you can also reduce the risk of scuffing. A profile modification can reduce the influence of tooth strain on stiffness fluctuations across the meshing, and therefore limit the number of transmission errors. This also lowers the levels of vibration and noise.

Therefore, if a profile modification has been properly sized for the gearbox's operating torque, the transverse contact ratio is not reduced. However, when lower loads are involved, gears whose profile has been modified do not mesh as well as gears without profile modification. This is because the transverse contact ratio has been significantly reduced.
