中文翻译

GOST 21354-87

按 GOST 21354-87 规定的质量等级
GOST 只考虑一个质量等级，因此，在计算中使用两个齿轮中较差的质量等级。
Q = max (Q1, Q2)

齿根和齿面的无限寿命强度值
无限寿命强度值 σFlim 和 σHlim 保存在 KISSsoft 数据库中，或通过选择自定义输入选项来输入。

齿根无限寿命强度 σFlim
无限寿命强度 σFlim 根据 GOST 按如下方式计算：
σFlim = σFlim0 * Yz * Yg * Yd * YA * YT
σFlim0 – 极限载荷循环次数下的名义无限寿命强度（GOST 21354-87 表 14-17）。
Yz - 毛坯系数（GOST 21354-87 表 13，公式 10.3）。
Yd – 考虑齿根过渡区的硬化（GOST 21354-87 表 14-17）。
Yg – 考虑齿根过渡区的磨削（GOST 21354-87 表 14-17）。
YT – 工艺系数（GOST 21354-87 表 13，公式 10.2）。默认工艺系数设置为 1.0，但您可以在 KISSsoft 中通过选择 Factors > Z-Y-Factors 来更改它。
YA – 交变弯曲系数（GOST 21354-87 表 13，公式 10.6）。默认交变弯曲系数设置为 1.0，但您可以将其更改为 自定义输入，方法是在 KISSsoft 中通过选择 Factors > 交变弯曲系数。
Yd、Yg、Yz 系数无法在 KISSsoft 中输入，并且必须在输入无限寿命强度时直接包含进去。
除上述系数外，GOST 中定义的无限寿命强度在输入 KISSsoft 之前必须除以 2.0。在计算中，σFlim 随后乘以应力修正系数 YST = 2，与 ISO 或 DIN 中的方式类似。

因此，按 GOST 计算时，σFlim 在 KISSsoft 中的正确输入为：
σFlim（KISSsoft 输入值）= σFlim0（按 GOST）* Yd * Yg * Yz / 2.0

所需最小安全系数
GOST 具有特殊性质：为齿根断裂和齿面设置的最小安全系数取决于材料类型和表面硬化。因此，对于 GOST，可在 设置 > 所需安全系数 下为每个齿轮单独输入最小安全系数。

根部倒圆角信息
在各种 GOST 公式中，会区分齿根倒圆角是否经过磨削。为了做出这种区分，必须选择 根和齿面强度计算细节 并输入合适的值。

齿向载荷分布系数（齿面）KHβ
齿向载荷分布系数（齿面）按 GOST 表 6、公式 7 计算。GOST 21354-87 附录 6 中所述的考虑因素被忽略。

齿向载荷分布系数（齿根）KFβ
齿向载荷分布系数（齿根）根据 GOST 21354-87 表 13、公式 4 计算。

动载系数 Kν
动载系数根据 GOST 21354-87 表6，公式6计算。如果公式6中条件(34)和(35)未满足，KISSsoft 根据 GOST 21354-87 附录5计算动载系数。

载荷谱
载荷谱的计算按照 ISO 6336-6 中定义的 Palmgren-Miner 规则进行。

硬化层的安全性
硬化层的安全性按照 DNV 41-2 计算。

English Original

GOST 21354-87

Quality according to GOST 21354-87
GOST only takes into account one quality, which is why the poorer quality of the two gears is used during the calculation.
Q = max (Q1, Q2)

Infinite life strength values for root and flank
The infinite life strength values σFlim and σHlim are saved in the KISSsoft database, or you can enter them by selecting the Own Input option.

Infinite life strength for root σFlim
The infinite life strength σFlim is calculated as follows, according to GOST:
σFlim = σFlim0 * Yz * Yg * Yd * YA * YT
σFlim0 – nominal infinite life strength at limit load cycle (GOST 21354-87 Tables 14-17).
Yz - blank coefficient (GOST 21354-87 Table 13, Formula 10.3).
Yd – takes into account the hardening of the root transition zone (GOST 21354-87 Tables 14-17).
Yg – takes into account the grinding of the root transition zone (GOST 21354-87 Tables 14-17).
YT – technology factor (GOST 21354-87 Table 13, Formula 10.2). The default technology factor setting is 1.0, but you can change it in KISSsoft by selecting Factors > Z-Y-Factors.
YA – alternating bending factor (GOST 21354-87 Table 13, Formula 10.6). The default alternating bending factor setting is 1.0, but you can change it to Own Input in KISSsoft by selecting Factors > Alternating bending factor.
The Yd, Yg, Yz factors cannot be entered in KISSsoft and must be included directly when the infinite life strength is entered.
In addition to the factors mentioned above, the infinite life strength defined in GOST must be divided by 2.0 before being entered in KISSsoft. In the calculation, σFlim is then multiplied by the stress correction factor YST = 2, in a similar way to in ISO or DIN.

Consequently, the correct entry for σFlim in KISSsoft, for calculations according to GOST, is:
σFlim (KISSsoft entry) = σFlim0 (according to GOST) * Yd * Yg * Yz / 2.0

Required minimum safeties
GOST has the special property that the minimum safety set for tooth root fracture and the flank depends on the material type and the surface hardening. For this reason, you can enter minimum safeties for every gear individually, for GOST, under Settings > Required safeties.

Information about root rounding
In various GOST formulae, a distinction is made between whether the root rounding is ground or not. To make this distinction, you must select Details for root and flank strength calculation and enter a suitable value.

Face load factor flank KHβ
The face load factor (flank) is calculated according to GOST, Table 6, Formula 7. The considerations described in GOST 21354-87 Annex 6 are ignored.

Face load factor root KFβ
The face load factor (root) is calculated according to GOST 21354-87 Table 13, Formula 4.

Dynamic factor Kν
The dynamic factor is calculated according to GOST 21354-87 Table 6, Formula 6. If conditions (34) and (35) specified in formula 6 are not fulfilled, KISSsoft calculates the dynamic factor according to GOST 21354-87 Annex 5.

Load spectra
Calculations with load spectra are performed using the rules defined by Palmgren-Miner, according to ISO 6336-6.

Safety of the hardened layer
The safety of the hardened layer is calculated according to DNV 41-2.
