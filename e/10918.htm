齿轮几何尺寸设计

活动齿顶-齿形直径差系数和齿顶间隙系数（也称为“齿形裕度”）是在“基准齿廓”选项卡和精细尺寸中用于确定基准齿廓的函数所需的设置。例如，您可能需要调整基准齿廓以实现精确的齿形裕度，并确保最小齿顶间隙也得到满足。

English Original

Sizing of gear geometry

The Coefficient for active tip - form diameter difference and Coefficient for tip clearance (also known as "form clearance") are settings that are needed for the functions used to size the reference profile, in the "Reference profile" tab and in Fine sizing. For example, you may need to adjust the reference profile to achieve an exact form clearance and to ensure that the minimum tip clearance is also exceeded.
