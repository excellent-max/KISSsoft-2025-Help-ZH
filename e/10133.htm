中文翻译

默认的顶隙值

弧齿锥齿轮的顶隙通常为中点法向模数的 0.2 至 0.3 倍。然而，对于制造时带有刀倾的齿形，会使用更大的间隙。这可以防止齿顶与配对齿轮的齿根相交。

默认值为（如Klingelnberg[49]出版的“Kegelräder”书中所述）：

"格里森（变槽宽）"工艺：0.3

"格里森（等槽宽）"工艺：0.35

"克林根贝尔格（准渐开线）"工艺：0.3

"克林根贝尔格（摆线-准渐开线）"工艺：0.25

"Oerlikon" 工艺：0.25

English Original

Default values for tip clearance

The tip clearance for spiral bevel gears is usually 0.2 to 0.3 times the mean normal module. However, a greater amount of clearance is used for toothing that is manufactured with tilt. This prevents the tooth tip intersecting with the root of the opposing gear.

Default values are (as stated in the "Kegelräder" book produced by Klingelnberg [49]):

"Gleason, modified slot width" process: 0.3

"Gleason, constant slot width" process: 0.35

"Klingelnberg, palloid" process: 0.3

"Klingelnberg, cyclo-palloid" process: 0.25

"Oerlikon" process: 0.25
