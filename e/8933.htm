中性格式接口查看器

KISSsoft 具有用于显示单个齿轮或齿轮系统的 3D 查看器。该查看器可从图形 > 3D 几何菜单激活。

在 3D 查看器中，您可以导出 STEP 和 Parasolid 格式（文本和二进制）的实体模型。支持的齿轮（参见"可用 CAD 接口及其功能概览"章）以及查看器的操作详情（参见"3D 几何"章）。您可以通过选择计算 > 设置 > 3D 生成来更改设置。

English Original

Viewer with neutral format interface

KISSsoft has a 3D viewer for displaying individual gears or a gear system. The viewer is activated from the Graphics > 3D Geometry menu.

In the 3D viewer, you can export the solid model in STEP and Parasolid formats (text and binary). Supported gears (see chapter Overview of the available CAD interfaces and their functionality) and details of how to operate the viewer (see chapter 3D Geometry). You can change the settings by selecting Calculation > Settings > 3D Generation.
