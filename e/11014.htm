中文翻译

接触分析

在锥齿轮和准双曲面齿轮模块中，接触分析计算直齿、斜齿和螺旋齿锥齿轮在载荷作用下的啮合线。准双曲面齿轮不支持。

分析中用一个具有当量圆柱齿轮齿形的锥齿轮副进行近似。该圆柱齿轮副中的每个齿轮在齿宽方向上的齿数不同，并具有一个工况节径和一个螺旋角（螺旋齿形）。有关接触分析理论的更详细说明，请参见圆柱齿轮接触分析部分（见"接触分析"章）和 [21]。

轴对中
接触分析考虑指定的 H、G 和 V 偏移量以及扭转方向。与圆柱齿轮副的接触分析一样，计算锥齿轮时也可以考虑轴的变形（见"考虑轴弯曲（面载荷因子和接触分析）"章）。当考虑轴的变形时，等效的 H、G 和 V 偏移量也会记录在接触分析报告中。

接触分析的其他设置可在“模块特定设置”中进行（见"计算"章）。

English Original

Contact analysis

In the Bevel and Hypoid gears module, the contact analysis calculates the path of contact under load for bevel gears with straight, helical, and spiral teeth. Hypoid gears are not supported.

A pair of bevel gears with virtual cylindrical gear toothings are approximated for the analysis. Each one of the gears in this cylindrical gear pair has a number of teeth that varies across the facewidth, an operating pitch diameter, and a helix angle (spiral toothing). For a more detailed description of the theory of contact analysis, refer to the Cylindrical gear contact analysis section (see chapter Contact analysis) and [21].

Axis alignment
The contact analysis takes into account the specified H, G and V misalignment, and also the direction of the torsion. As for contact analysis for cylindrical gear pairs, the deformation of the shafts can also be taken into account when calculating bevel gears (see chapter Taking into account shaft bending (face load factor and contact analysis)). When the deformation of the shafts is taken into account, the equivalent H, G and V misalignment is also documented in the contact analysis report.

Other settings for the contact analysis can be made in the Module specific settings (see chapter Calculation).
