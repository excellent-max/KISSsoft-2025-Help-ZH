中文翻译

考虑重量

这定义了在截面尺寸计算中如何考虑轴的自重。根据轴布置的取向 （参见"空间中轴的位置"章），您会看到附加的轴向力和剪切力，它们可能对弯曲和/或轴向位移图产生影响。

注意

在全局坐标系中，重力沿负 z 方向作用于轴。

English Original

Consider weight

This defines how the shaft's own weight is taken into account in the section dimension calculation. Depending on the orientation of the shaft arrangement (see chapter Position of shaft axis in space), you will see additional axial and shear forces which may have an influence on the diagrams of bending and/or axial displacement.

Note

In a global coordinates system, gravitational forces act on the shafts in the negative, z-direction.
