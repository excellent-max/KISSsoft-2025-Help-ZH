结果窗口

KISSsoft 结果窗口显示上次计算的结果。对于每个计算模块，基本计算结果和当前特殊计算的结果显示在两个结果窗口中。结果可通过上下文菜单保存和打印。

English Original

The Results window

The KISSsoft results window displays the results of the last calculation. For each calculation module, the basic calculation results and the results for the current special calculation are displayed in two results windows. The results can be saved and printed via the context menu.
