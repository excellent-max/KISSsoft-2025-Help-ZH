确定轴承游隙尺寸

轴承游隙 = d_bore - d_shaft

一般来说，较大的轴承游隙使轴承更稳定，并且能更有效地冷却。但是，这也会导致承载能力降低。

  • 根据 Niemann 的建议
    Niemann《机械工程中的金属轴承》，第 I 卷，表 15/2，[8]。

    对于其他材料，应采用以下值：

铸铁轴承

0.001 * d

轻金属轴承

0.0013 * d

烧结轴承

0.0015 * d

塑料轴承

0.003 * d

d : 轴承直径

  • 根据 ISO 7902 的建议
    根据 ISO 7902 第 3 部分表 4 的《机械工程中的金属轴承》建议，[69]。
    在此尺寸计算方法中，您可以使用根据 ISO 7902 的建议，或者根据预定义的输出温度计算游隙（仅当使用润滑剂散热时）。
  • 根据 DIN 31652 的建议
    根据 ISO 7902 第 3 部分表 4 的《机械工程中的金属轴承》建议，[69]，因为 DIN 标准不包含建议值。
    在此尺寸计算方法中，您可以使用根据 ISO 7902 的建议，或者根据预定义的输出温度计算游隙（仅当使用润滑剂散热时）。
  • 根据 DIN 31657 的建议
    根据 DIN 31657 第 4 部分《机械工程中的滑动轴承》建议，[70]。
    在此尺寸计算方法中，您可以使用根据 DIN 31657 的建议，或者根据输入的输出温度计算游隙。
  • 根据 K. Spiegel 的建议
    根据 K. Spiegel 的游隙建议：Goettner 方程
    相对轴承游隙 = (2.5+50.0/D)/1000.0 "其中 d 单位为 [mm]"
    轴承游隙（mm）：(2.5+50.0/D)/1000.0*D
English Original

Sizing the bearing clearance

Bearing clearance = d_bore - d_shaft

In general, a larger bearing clearance makes the bearing more stable and allows it to cool more effectively. However, it also results in reduced load capacity.

  • Suggestion according to Niemann
    Proposal for metal bearings in mechanical engineering according to Niemann, Volume I, Table 15/2, [8].

    The following values should be applied for other materials:

Cast iron bearing

0.001 * d

Light metal bearing

0.0013 * d

Sintered bearing

0.0015 * d

Plastic bearing

0.003 * d

d : Bearing diameter

  • Proposal according to ISO 7902
    Proposal for metal bearings in mechanical engineering according to ISO 7902, Part 3, Table 4, [69].
    In this sizing method, you can either use the proposal according to ISO 7902 or calculate the clearance from a predefined output temperature (only where the lubricant is used to dissipate the heat).
  • Proposal according to DIN 31652
    Proposal for metal bearings in mechanical engineering according to ISO 7902, Part 3, Table 4, [69], as the DIN standard does not contain a proposed value.
    In this sizing method, you can either use the proposal according to ISO 7902 or calculate the clearance from a predefined output temperature (only where the lubricant is used to dissipate the heat).
  • Proposal according to DIN 31657
    Proposal for plain bearings in mechanical engineering according to DIN 31657, Part 4, [70].
    In this sizing method you can either use the proposal according to DIN 31657 or calculate the clearance from the entered output temperature.
  • Proposal according to K. Spiegel
    Proposal for clearance according to K. Spiegel: Goettner equation
    Relative bearing clearance = (2.5+50.0/D)/1000.0 "with d [mm]"
    Bearing clearance in mm: (2.5+50.0/D)/1000.0*D
