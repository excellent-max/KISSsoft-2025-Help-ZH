中文翻译

齿条公差

从2003年10月版本起，齿条公差与配对齿轮一起定义。

这符合 DIN 3961。

齿条轮齿的公差不应大于其配对齿轮的公差。如果制造商不知道配对齿轮的值，可以将齿条长度设置为与配对齿轮圆周相同的值。

English Original

Allowances for racks

From Release 10/2003 onwards, allowances for racks are defined in conjunction with the paired gear.

This conforms to DIN 3961.

"The tolerances for the gear teeth on a rack should not be greater than the tolerances for its counter gear. If the manufacturer does not know the counter gear value, they can set the rack length to the same value as the counter gear circumference."
