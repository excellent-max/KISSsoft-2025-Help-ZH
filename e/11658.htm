中文翻译

按 DIN 743-4 的 S-N 曲线（Wöhler 线）指数

如果为按 DIN 743 的强度计算选中此选项，则钢材材料的 S-N 曲线（Wöhler 线）指数 qσ 和 qτ 将按 DIN 743-4 设置。DIN 743 仅适用于钢材的计算。

如果未激活此选项，并且选择了诸如铝、不锈钢（奥氏体化）或 ADI 的材料，则将使用按 FKM 准则的 S-N 曲线（Wöhler 线）指数。

针对上述材料，S-N 曲线（Wöhler 线）中会显示 2 个折点，这是根据 FKM 准则进行的。这意味着，S-N 曲线（Wöhler 线）在 NL > 106 处具有不同的斜率，qσ = 15 和 qτ = 25。这些指数在 FKM 准则中使用公式符号 k，而不是 DIN 标准中使用的公式符号 q。

English Original

S-N curves (Wöhler lines) exponents according to DIN 743-4

If this option is selected for the strength calculation according to DIN 743, the S-N curve (Wöhler line) exponents qσ and qτ are set according to DIN 743-4 for steel materials. DIN 743 is only valid for calculating steel.

If this option has not be activated and a material such as aluminum, stainless steel (austenitising) or ADI is selected, the S-N curve (Wöhler line) exponents according to the FKM guideline are used.

2 buckles in the S-N curve (Wöhler line) are displayed for the materials described above, according to the FKM guideline. This means, the S-N curve (Wöhler line) has a different pitch at NL > 106 qσ = 15 and qτ = 25. The exponents have the formula symbol k in the FKM guideline instead of the formula symbol q which is used in DIN standards.
