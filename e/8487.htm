中文翻译

允许的质量减少量

您可以限制蜗轮上允许的质量减少量（以 kg 为单位）（例如，通过指定换油间隔）。该极限值也用于定义磨损安全。如果此输入字段包含值 0，则不检查质量减少量。

注意

不计算蜗杆上的质量减少量，因为该标准假设蜗杆比蜗轮硬，因此不会受到磨损。

English Original

Permissible mass decrease

You can limit the permissible mass decrease in kg on the worm wheel (for example, by specifying oil change intervals). This limiting value is also used to define wear safety. If this input field contains value 0, the mass decrease is not checked.

Note

The decrease in mass experienced on the worm is not calculated, because the standard assumes that the worm is harder than the worm wheel and therefore will not be subject to wear.
