中文翻译

定义胶合载荷级（油品规格）

在试验台上，试验齿轮上的扭矩逐渐增大，直至发生胶合，如 Niemann [7] 第 166 页所述。该载荷级随后被输入到油品规格参数中（示例：载荷级 10 时无胶合，载荷级 11 时发生胶合。因此该油的胶合载荷级为 11）。

要计算胶合承载能力，您随后必须输入该载荷级（用于油品规格）。在上述示例中，该值为 11（根据 Niemann [7] 第 341 页）。抗胶合安全系数计算采用大于 1.0 的预设安全系数，以确定抗胶合安全性。。这形成必要的储备，因为试验中使用的扭矩逐渐增大仅近似于有效胶合扭矩。

English Original

Defining the load stage scuffing (oil specification)

On a test rig, the torque on the test gear is increased gradually until scuffing occurs, as described in Niemann [7], page 166. This load stage is then entered in the oil specification parameters (Example: no scuffing at load stage 10. Scuffing at load stage 11. The load stage scuffing of the oil is therefore 11).

To calculate the scuffing load capacity, you must then enter this load stage (for the oil specification). In the example described above, this is the value 11 (according to Niemann [7], page 341). The safety against scuffing calculation determines the safety against scuffing with predefined safeties greater than 1.0. This creates a necessary reserve, because the gradual increase in torque used in the test only approximates the effective scuffing torque.
