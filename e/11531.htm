中文翻译

尽管存在无旋转对称的轴段，仍继续计算

包含此选项是为了在存在非旋转对称截面时仍能对轴进行强度校核。要计算的横截面不得位于非旋转对称元件中。但是，只有在非旋转对称元件具有相同的惯性矩 Ix = Iz 时才允许这样做。否则，刚度以及整个计算都将不正确。除强度计算外的其他计算，也必须检查其准确性。

English Original

Continue calculation despite of shaft sections without rotational symmetry

This option has been included so that the shaft can be checked for strength even though non-rotationally symmetrical sections are present. The cross sections to be calculated must not be in the non-rotationally symmetrical element. However, this is only permissible if the non-rotationally symmetrical element has the same moments of inertia Ix = Iz. If not, the stiffnesses and consequently the entire calculation will be incorrect. Other calculations, apart from the strength calculation, must be checked for accuracy.
