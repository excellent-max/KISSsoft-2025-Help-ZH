中文翻译

支撑系数

DIN 6892 中轴和轮毂的支撑系数因材料和部件而异。系数在特定范围内变化。KISSsoft 使用最小值，这些值可在本窗口中覆盖。

English Original

Support factors

The support factors for the shaft and the hub in DIN 6892 differ according to the materials and parts involved. The factors vary within a specific range. The minimum values are used in KISSsoft, and these values can be overwritten in this window.
