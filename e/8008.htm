半圆键

使用半圆键的连接已不再常用，因为这些键中的深键槽会造成过大的缺口效应。但是，这种连接在精密机械中仍被广泛使用。

对于半圆键，您需要计算轴和轮毂上的载荷（表面压力）。您还可以添加其他标准。轴和轮毂上载荷（表面压力）的计算以及安全系数的确定，如经典技术文献 [8] 中所述进行。Niemann 定义的计算基于 DIN 6892（键计算）。

English Original

Woodruff Keys

Connections that use Woodruff keys are no longer commonly used, because the deep key way in these keys causes too great a notch effect. However, this connection still widely used in precision mechanics.

For Woodruff keys, you calculate the load on the shaft and hub (surface pressure). You can also add additional standards. The calculation of the load placed on the shaft and hub (surface pressure), together with determining the safeties, is performed as described in classic technical literature ([8]. The calculation defined by Niemann is based on DIN 6892 (key calculation).
