English Original

Lead angle at reference diameter

The lead angle on the reference circle in a worm (gear 1) is the complement of the helix angle and is calculated according to equation 17.1. Lead direction (see Figure 17.3).

(17.1)

Click the Convert button to open the Convert lead angle dialog window, in which you can calculate the lead angle from other gear values. These options are available here: from center distance, from reference diameter and from the reference circle and the center distance (x2* is modified). A larger lead angle produces greater efficiency, whereas you can create self-locking toothing if you use a smaller lead angle.
