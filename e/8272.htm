中文翻译

转速

以每分钟转数 (rpm) 为单位输入转速。单击"+"按钮可显示 定义转速 窗口。在此处定义所有轴的转速和旋转方向。

计算所用的轴转速也可以在相应的报告中找到：选择"报告" > "轴转速"。

注意

如果更改转速，有效扭矩和功率会相应改变。

English Original

Speed

Enter the speed in revolutions per minute (rpm). Click the Plus button to display the Define speed window. This is where you define the speed and the sense of rotation for all the shafts.

The shaft speed used for the calculation can also be found in the corresponding report: select "Report" > "Shaft speeds".

Note

If you change the speed, the effective torques and power change accordingly.
