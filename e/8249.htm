中文翻译

表面粗糙度

如果您输入符合 ISO 1302 的表面粗糙度值，则相应的表面粗糙度 RZ 会显示在列表框中。该值 RZ 随后用于计算。在根据 DIN 或 FKM 进行的计算中，某些情况下表面粗糙度已包含在缺口系数中。在这些情况下，无论您输入的表面粗糙度值是多少，表面系数始终为 1.0。

English Original

Surface roughness

If you enter a value for surface roughness as defined in ISO 1302, the corresponding surface roughness, RZ , is displayed in the selection list. This value, RZ , is then used in the calculation. In the calculation according to DIN or FKM, surface roughness has already been included in the notch factor in some cases. In these cases, the surface factor is always 1.0, no matter what value you input as the surface roughness.
