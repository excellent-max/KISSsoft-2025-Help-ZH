中文翻译

体积比热计算

在您通过选择 计算 > 设置 看到的对话框中，有两种方法可用于计算体积比热：

  • 考虑温度依赖性
  • 简化假设（按 ISO 7902）：1.8*106J/(m3*K)
English Original

Calculation of volume-specific heat

In the dialog you see by selecting Calculations > Settings, there are two methods you can use to calculate volume-specific heat:

  • Take into account dependence on temperature
  • Simplified assumption (as in ISO 7902): 1.8*106J/(m3*K)
