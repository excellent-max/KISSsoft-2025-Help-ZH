中文翻译

接触温度

齿面接触温度是在接触时刻齿面上的局部温度。它在啮合处显示。根据齿面接触温度值及其在齿面的位置，可以采取适当措施（例如齿廓修形）以减少温度。

English Original

Contact temperature

The contact temperature is the local temperature on the tooth flank at the moment of contact. It is displayed over the meshing. Based on the contact temperature values and its locations on the flank, appropriate action (e.g. profile modification) can be taken to reduce the temperature if necessary.
