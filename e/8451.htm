中文翻译

使用寿命

系统在输入字段中显示所需使用寿命。
要直接输入并进行尺寸计算，请单击"尺寸计算"按钮。此过程使用齿根和齿面强度的最小安全值，为每个齿轮以及您指定的每个载荷计算额定寿命（以小时为单位）。额定寿命根据 ISO 6336-6:2006 使用 Palmgren-Miner 法则计算。在耐久极限范围内，您还可以选择 S-N 曲线（Woehler 线）的修改形式，而不是 ISO 6336 或 DIN 3990。程序显示系统额定寿命以及配置中使用的所有齿轮的最小额定寿命。您也可以单击"尺寸计算"按钮，在定义或不定义载荷谱的情况下计算使用寿命（参见"定义载荷谱"章）。有关载荷谱的更多详细信息，参见（参见"定义载荷谱"章）。

注意

ISO 6336/Niemann 方法主要用于支持使用寿命计算。

English Original

Service life

The system displays the required service life in the input field.
To enter it directly, and perform sizing, click the Sizing button. This process uses the minimum safety value for the tooth root and flank strength to calculate the rating life (in hours) for every gear and for every load you specify. The rating life is calculated according to ISO 6336-6:2006 using the Palmgren-Miner Rule. In the range of endurance limit, you can also select a modified form of the S-N curve (Woehler line) instead of ISO 6336 or DIN 3990. The program displays the system rating life and the minimum rating life of all the gears used in the configuration. You can also click the Sizing button to size the service life with or without defining a load spectrum (see chapter Define load spectrum). For more detailed information about load spectra, see (see chapter Define load spectrum).

Note

The ISO 6336/Niemann method is primarily used to support the service life calculation.
