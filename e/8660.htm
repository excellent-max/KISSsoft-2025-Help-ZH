行星齿轮级

行星齿轮级的齿向载荷系数的计算方法与圆柱齿轮不同。变形分量 fsh 由轴上配对齿轮因扭转和弯曲产生的变形导出。为简化小齿轮-齿轮副的情况，仅考虑小齿轮的变形（该变形要大得多）。

行星齿轮级承受以下显著变形：

  • 由于太阳轮有多个齿啮合，所有径向力相互抵消。由于变形仅由扭转引起，因此不发生弯曲。然而，与行星轮数量对应的多重啮合意味着这比普通整体式小齿轮轴更大。
  • 行星轮具有两个扭矩相反的啮合，从而防止了扭转导致的变形。弯曲可以按整体式小齿轮轴相同的方式计算。然而，由于太阳轮/行星轮以及行星轮/内齿轮的存在，圆周力必须加倍。
  • 在大多数情况下，轮缘变形可以忽略。因此，对于太阳轮/行星轮啮合，必须考虑小齿轮处的扭转和行星轮销轴处的弯曲；而对于行星轮/内齿轮啮合，只需考虑行星轮销轴处的弯曲。对于大多数行星轮支承布置，弯曲可以使用与 ISO 6336 中规定类似的方法解析确定。下面显示了 4 种最常见的情况（见图 15.16）。

Planetary stages

The face load factors for planetary stages are calculated in a different way than for cylindrical gears. The deformation component fsh is derived from the deformation of the mating gears on the shaft due to torsion and bending. In order to simplify the situation for a pinion-gear pair, only the pinion deformation (which is much greater) is taken into account.

Planetary stages are subject to the following significant deformations:

  • Since the sun has several tooth meshings, all radial forces are canceled out. No bending takes place because deformation is caused solely by torsion. However, the multiple meshing which corresponds to the number of planets means this is greater than for normal integral pinion shafts.
  • A planet gear has two meshings with opposed torques, which prevents deformation due to torsion. Bending may be calculated in the same way as for integral pinion shafts. However, the circumferential force must be doubled because of the sun/planet and planet/internal gear.
  • In most cases, rim deformation can be ignored. As a result, the torsion at the pinion and the bending at the planet bolt must be taken into consideration for sun/planet meshing whereas, for planet/internal gear, only the bending at the planet bolt is important. For most support arrangements for planets, bending can be determined analytically using a procedure similar to that specified in ISO 6336.
