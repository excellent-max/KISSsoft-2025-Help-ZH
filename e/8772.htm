中文翻译

三角形端部修形，侧面 I 和 II

图（参见图 15.56）显示了齿廓图中定义的三角形端部修形，侧面 I 和侧面 II。此图中还显示了 数值、系数 1 和 系数 2 设置。该修形施加于齿轮的有效齿宽上。

三角形端部修形是指，从特定点开始，沿齿廓线和齿向线，朝前端面和后端面方向持续且逐渐地去除材料。此时，I 和 II 的编号分别对应两个端面（参见图 15.56）。

English Original

Triangular end relief, sides I and II

Figure (see Figure 15.56) shows the triangular end relief, side I and side II, as defined in the profile diagram. The Value, Factor 1 and Factor 2 settings are also shown in this figure. The modification is applied over the effective facewidth of the gear.

Triangular end relief occurs where material is removed constantly, and increasingly, from the profile line and flank line, starting from particular points, in the direction of the front and rear face surface. In this case, the numbers for I and II relate to both face surfaces (see Figure 15.56).
