中文翻译

变体

可以在单个表中为所有轴和齿轮定义子模块计算的多个变体。然后可以选择一个全局变体，将所有子模块计算文件导入模型中。随后可将结果与另一个变体进行比较。

English Original

Variants

Several variants of submodule calculations can be defined for all shafts and gears, within a single table. A global variant can then be selected to import all submodule calculation files into the model. The results can then be compared with another variant.
