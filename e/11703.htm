中文翻译

为大直径和大边缘距离定义 σzdW

按 DIN 743-3:2024 规定，对于大直径，局部拉/压疲劳极限会被高估。因此，我们建议您使用 DIN 743-3:2024 中如下所示的公式 (8) 来近似计算 σzdW，特别是对于 d > 300 mm 的锻件，其边缘距离 > 50 mm（dRoh-d）的区域。此选项可用于考虑这一点。否则，将使用标准换算公式进行计算。

English Original

Define σzdW for large diameter and large edge distance

As defined in DIN 743-3:2024, the local tension/compression fatigue limit is overestimated for large diameters. For this reason, we recommend you use formula (8) from DIN 743-3:2024, shown below, to calculate σzdW approximately, particularly for forged parts d > 300 mm for areas whose edge distance is > 50 mm (dRoh-d). This option can be used to take this into account. Otherwise, calculations are performed using the standard conversion formulae.
