中文翻译

在计算齿轮强度时考虑喷丸数据

AGMA 2004-B89指出，喷丸强化可提高齿根强度 25%。

如果使用 KISSsoft 根据 DIN 或 ISO 进行计算，可以通过输入相关工艺因子来实现喷丸强化带来的齿根强度增加。为此，请打开 因子 选项卡，并在 通用因子 组中点击"Z-Y 因子..."。

用户将在手册中找到如Linke、Bureau Veritas/RINA或ISO 6336中规定的有用条目的详细信息。如果想根据AGMA进行计算，则无法输入工艺因子。在这种情况下，必须在输入材料数据时直接指定相关百分比率来增加齿根疲劳极限。为此，请打开 基本数据 选项卡，然后在材料选择后点击加号按钮。在显示的对话框中，点击 自行输入。在齿根疲劳极限（AGMA 2001）的计算数据中设置无限寿命强度。

English Original

Taking shot peening data into account when calculating gear strength

AGMA 2004-B89 states that shot peening improves the tooth root strength by 25%.

If you use KISSsoft to perform calculations according to DIN or ISO, you can achieve the increase in tooth root strength due to shot peening by inputting the relevant technology factor. To do this, open the Factors tab and click on "Z-Y factors..." in the General factors group.

You will find the details of useful entries as specified in Linke, Bureau Veritas/RINA or ISO 6336 in the manual. If you want to perform the calculation according to AGMA, you do not have the option of inputting the technology factor. In this case, you must increase the root endurance limit by specifying the relevant percentage rate directly when you enter the material data. To do this, open the Basic data tab and then click the Plus button after the material selection. In the dialog window that is then displayed, click on Own input. Set the infinite life strength in the calculation data for Endurance limit root (AGMA 2001).
