中文翻译

工序

您可以使用不同工序的组合来计算齿形。您可以一步步地应用加工工序，例如使用滚刀或插齿刀，并应用倒圆角或齿廓修形等修形。您可以给每个工序添加标签，以便日后易于识别。

English Original

Operations

You can use a combination of different operations to calculate the tooth form. You can apply one processing step after another, for example, using a hobbing cutter or a pinion type cutter and applying modifications such as roundings or profile modifications. You can label each operation to make it easy to identify at a later point in time.
