中文翻译

宽度系数与圆周系数

如果选中这些系数输入字段右侧的复选框，则可以手动覆盖该值。否则，该值会自动计算，并可能在 [3, 5] 范围内变化。由于这些系数相乘可确定过载，因此可获得的安全系数可比 Niemann 定义的计算方法所可能达到的值小 20 倍。

English Original

Width and circumferential factor

If you select the checkbox on the right of the input field for these coefficients, you can overwrite the values manually. Otherwise, this value is calculated automatically and may vary within the range [3, 5]. As these are multiplied together to define the overload, you can achieve safeties of up to 20 times smaller than is possible with the calculation method defined in Niemann.
