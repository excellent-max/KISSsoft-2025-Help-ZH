中文翻译

齿向线鼓形修形，齿面 I 和 II

图（参见图 15.53）显示了齿向线图中定义的齿向线鼓形修形（第 I 侧和第 II 侧）。图中还显示了 值、系数 1 和 系数 2 设置。该修形应用于齿轮的有效齿宽。

该修形类似于齿向线鼓形修形（参见"齿向线鼓形修形"章），但可以针对每个齿面单独设置。系数 1 和 2 可用于设置鼓形修形的起点和 Cß 的位置。

图 15.53：齿向线鼓形修形，齿面 I 和 II

English Original

Flank line crowning, sides I and II

Figure (see Figure 15.53) shows the flank line crowning, side I and side II, as defined in the flank line diagram. The Value, Factor 1 and Factor 2 settings are also shown in this figure. The modification is applied over the effective facewidth of the gear.

This modification resembles the modification for flank line crowning (see chapter Flank line crowning), but it can be set individually for each side. Factors 1 and 2 can be used to set the crowning start point and the position of Cß.
