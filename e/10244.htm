有哪些可用的许可证？

KISSsoft 和 KISSsys 均提供单用户许可证和网络许可证。网络许可证允许软件在多个工作点上使用。

不过，部分第三方产品（例如某些 CAD 接口）不提供网络许可证。

English Original

What licenses are available?

Individual user licenses and network licenses are available for both KISSsoft and KISSsys. A network license enables the software to be used at more than one workplace.

However, network licenses are not available for some of the third party products, for example, some CAD interfaces.
