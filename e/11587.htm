中文翻译

换挡矩阵

在此选项卡中，可以定义所有转速，并针对每个转速设置所有换挡元件的状态。还提供了一个"尺寸设计"按钮，可为每条潜在功率流路径提供唯一的换挡转速及相应闭合的换挡元件。每个转速也可以重命名。可在控制面板上更改啮合齿轮。功率流随后会在草图绘制器中直接更新。

English Original

Switching matrix

In this tab, all speeds can be defined and the status of all switching elements can be set for each speed. A Sizing button is also available to provide a unique shifting speed, with particular closed switching elements, for each potential power flow path. Each speed can also be renamed. The engaged gear can be changed on the control panel. The power flow is then updated directly in the Sketcher.
