中文翻译

生成 3D 轴

到目前为止，只能在 SolidWorks、Solid Edge、Autodesk Inventor 和 Siemens NX 这些 CAD 系统中生成 3D 轴。

首先必须进行轴计算，以确保结果一致。点击 图形 > 设置 选择要导出所选单元的 CAD 系统。

然后，点击 图形 > 3D 导出 以选择所需的轴，以及配置（如果希望生成多个轴）。每个轴在配置中单独创建，按顺序生成，每个轴为独立部件。

这使用户能够通过单击按钮，在 CAD 系统中根据 KISSsoft 轴计算的数据创建 3D 轴。

English Original

Generating 3D shafts

Until now, it has only been possible to generate shafts in 3D in the SolidWorks, Solid Edge, Autodesk Inventor and Siemens NX CAD systems.

First, a shaft calculation must be performed to ensure the results are consistent. Click on Graphics > Settings to select the CAD system to which you want to export the selected element.

Then, click Graphics > 3D Export to select the shaft you require, and configuration (if you want to generate more than one shaft). Each shaft is created individually in the configuration, in sequence, in its own parts.

This enables you to create a 3D shaft in the CAD system at the click of a button, according to the data from a KISSsoft shaft calculation.
