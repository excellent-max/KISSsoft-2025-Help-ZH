中文翻译

强度细节

点击 细节 按钮（用于根强度和齿面强度计算）将显示一个对话框，可在此对强度计算进行额外设置。

其他位置所述的参数如下：

  • 有限寿命（参见"按 ISO 6336 定义的寿命系数"章）
  • 在耐久极限范围内修改 S-N 曲线（Woehler 线）
  • 带载荷谱的齿面
  • 带载荷谱的齿根
  • 轻微点蚀（参见"允许少量点蚀"章）
  • 轮齿体温度
  • 润滑剂系数 XL
  • 齿部磨合良好
  • 相对结构系数
English Original

Strength details

Click on the Details button for the root and flank strength calculation to display a dialog in which you can make additional settings for the strength calculation.

The parameters described in other places are:

  • Limited life (see chapter Life factors as defined in ISO 6336)
  • Modification of S-N curve (Woehler lines) in the range of endurance limit
  • Tooth flank with load spectrum
  • Tooth root with load spectrum
  • Minor pitting (see chapter Small amount of pitting permissible)
  • Tooth mass temperature
  • Lubricant factor XL
  • Toothing is well run in
  • Relative structural factor
