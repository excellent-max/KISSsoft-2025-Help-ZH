材料和润滑

材料

依据 ISO 14521 对蜗杆进行强度计算所用的方法，基于通过以下材料确定的经验值：

蜗杆：

  • 渗碳淬火钢（尤其是 16MnCr5），HRC = 58 至 62
  • 调质钢（尤其是 42CrMo4），调质或感应淬火，HRC = 50 至 56
  • 氮化钢（尤其是 31CrMoV9），气体氮化

蜗轮：

  • 青铜（GZ-CuSn12、GZ-CuSn12Ni、GZ-CuAl10Ni）
  • 灰铸铁（GJS40、GC25）
  • 聚酰胺（PA-12，铸造）

要进行强度计算，您需要非常特殊的材料数据，尤其是磨损值。该标准仅为最常用的蜗轮材料（主要是青铜）规定了这些值。这就是 KISSsoft 中材料选择有限的原因。由于为尚未记录的材料定义数据需要花费大量时间和精力，我们强烈建议您从列表中选择与您实际想使用的材料最接近的材料。

润滑剂

为蜗杆选择合适的润滑剂极其重要。合成润滑剂（聚乙二醇或聚α-烯烃）可以大幅减少损耗和磨损。

English Original

Material and lubrication

Materials

The strength calculation method used for worms according to ISO 14521 is based on empirical values determined using these materials:

Worm:

  • Case-hardened steels (especially 16MnCr5), HRC = 58 to 62
  • Heat treatable steels (especially 42CrMo4), heat- or induction-hardened, HRC = 50 to 56
  • Nitriding steels (especially 31CrMoV9), gas-nitrided

Worm wheel:

  • Bronze (GZ-CuSn12, GZ-CuSn12Ni, GZ-CuAl10Ni)
  • Grey cast iron (GJS40, GC25)
  • Polyamide (PA-12, cast)

To calculate strength, you require very special materials data, in particular the wear values. The standard only specifies these values for the most commonly used worm wheel materials (mostly bronze). This is why the selection of materials in KISSsoft is limited. As defining data for materials that are not already documented takes a great deal of time and effort, we strongly recommend you select a material from the list that is closest to the material you actually want to use.

Lubricants

Selecting the right lubricant for a worm is extremely important. Synthetic lubricants (polyglycols or polyalfaolephine) can reduce loss and wear enormously.
