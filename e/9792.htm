中文翻译

锥齿轮制造工艺及其所用术语概述

制造锥齿轮使用各种制造工艺。与圆柱齿轮不同，齿长形态和齿深形态根据所使用的制造工艺而有所不同。特别是，用于制造弧齿锥齿轮的工艺使用了大量术语，其中最重要的术语在下面进行说明。

最重要的差异体现在齿长形态上，它可以制造成圆弧（面铣法）、外摆线或渐开线齿形（面滚切法）。圆弧齿由 Gleason 公司开发，使用面铣法生产，其中每个齿槽单独铣削，然后齿轮再旋转该齿槽宽度的角度。Oerlikon 和 Klingelnberg 采用外摆线齿轮齿。在该工艺中，齿轮在铣削过程中持续旋转。只有 palloid 制造工艺用于制造渐开线齿长形态。尽管如今机床制造的市场领导者 Klingelnberg 和 Gleason 都可以使用面铣法和面滚切法生产齿形，但这些公司在技术文献中仍然与其传统工艺联系在一起。有关更多详细信息，请参见“按 Klingelnberg、Gleason 和 Oerlikon 计算”一章和“类型”一章。

尽管锥齿轮还有其他替代工艺，但这里未列出。

English Original

Overview of the bevel gear manufacturing process and the terminology used in it

Various manufacturing processes are used to create bevel gears. Unlike cylindrical gears, the tooth length forms and tooth depth forms differ according to which manufacturing process is used. In particular, the process used to manufacture bevel gears with spiral teeth uses a multitude of terms, the most important of which are described below.

The most important differences are shown in the tooth length form, which can be manufactured as an arc of circle (face milling procedure), epicycloid or involute toothing (face hobbing process). Circular arc teeth were developed by the company Gleason, and are produced using the face milling approach, in which every gap is milled separately, and then the gear is rotated further by the width of that tooth space. Epicycloid gear teeth are used by Oerlikon and Klingelnberg. In this process, the gear rotates constantly during the milling process. Only the palloid manufacturing process is used to create the involute tooth length form. Although, nowadays, Klingelnberg and Gleason, the market leaders in machine manufacturing, can produce toothing using both the face milling and face hobbing processes, these companies are still associated with their traditional processes in the technical literature. For more details, see (see chapter Calculation according to Klingelnberg, Gleason and Oerlikon) and (see chapter Type).

Although alternative processes for bevel gears are available, they are not listed here.
