中文翻译

纯推力轴承

如果轴承被定义为纯推力轴承（左/右/双侧布局），则无法传递径向力。外圈对轴承座的压力所产生的任何径向力以及相应的摩擦系数都将被忽略。

English Original

Pure thrust bearing

If a bearing is defined as a pure thrust bearing (left/right/both side layout) no radial forces can be transferred to it. Any radial forces created by the pressure of the outer ring on the housing and the corresponding coefficients of friction will be ignored.
