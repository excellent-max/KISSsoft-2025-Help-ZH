中文翻译

腹板参数输入

除了手动输入这些值外，还可以单击修形选项卡，使用算法输入腹板数值。推力垫圈的许用压力用于确定推力垫圈的外径。从孔到推力垫圈的距离（Delta05Bf）包括孔到推力垫圈内径的径向距离，并与锥齿轮孔一起用于确定内径。推力垫圈处所需腹板厚度的值（SBfAS）包括推力垫圈外径到齿根外侧腹板的轴向距离。该距离用于确定齿根外侧腹板的尺寸（腹板长度和高度，参见修形选项卡）。

所需的内侧壁厚（用于 dFi）包括从孔到内侧齿根处腹板的径向距离。

单击适当的自动执行适当的齿顶与齿根缩短选项，软件将在修形选项卡中定义腹板。齿根缩减量根据上文所述输入的数值确定。齿顶缩减量根据所输入的数值确定，该数值即齿根腹板（配对齿轮）所需的齿顶间隙。

English Original

Entries for the webbing

As an alternative to entering these values manually, click on the Modifications tab to use an algorithm to enter the webbing values. The permissible pressure for the thrust washer is used to determine the external diameter of the thrust washer. The distance from bore to thrust washer (Delta05Bf) includes the radial distance of the bore to the internal diameter of the thrust washer and is used, together with the bevel gear bore, to determine the internal diameter. The value for the required webbing thickness at the thrust washer (SBfAS) includes the axial distance of the external diameter of the thrust washer to the webbing on the outside of the root. This distance is used to size the webbing on the outside of the root (webbing length and height, see Modifications tab).

The required inside wall thickness (for dFi) includes the radial distance from the bore to the webbing at the inside root.

Click the appropriate Perform appropriate tip and root shortening automatically option and the software will define the webbing in the Modifications tab. The root reductions are determined using the values input as described above. Tip reductions are defined using the value input as the necessary tip clearance for the root webbing (of the counter gear).
