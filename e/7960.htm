花键（几何与强度）

您可以根据 DIN 5480（1986 年版）、ISO 4156、ANSI B92.1 或 ANSI B92.2M 计算花键和轮毂的几何及检验测量值。还包括根据 Niemann、DIN 5466 和 AGMA 6123-C16 进行的强度计算。

根据 DIN 5481（2019）和 DIN 5482（1973）的几何齿廓保存在文件中。

当您打开所需齿廓的文件时，所有必要的几何设置都会自动填入 KISSsoft 界面。

English Original

Splines (Geometry and Strength)

You can calculate the geometry and the control measurement values of splines and hubs according to DIN 5480 (1986 Edition), ISO 4156, ANSI B92.1 or ANSI B92.2M. Strength calculations according to Niemann, DIN 5466 and AGMA 6123-C16 are also included.

The geometry profiles according to DIN 5481 (2019) and DIN 5482 (1973) are saved in files.

When you open the file for the profile you require, the KISSsoft screens are filled with all the necessary geometry settings.
