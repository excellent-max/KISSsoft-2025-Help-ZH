中文翻译

轴承数据

这里是定义轴承几何形状的地方（见“按内部几何参数的滚动轴承计算”章节）。除几何数据外，还可以输入基本额定动载荷。如果不知道此值，将按照 ISO 281 的规定，根据当前几何数据计算额定值。如果需要计算修正额定寿命（见“根据 ISO 281 的修正额定寿命计算”章节），则输入疲劳载荷极限 Cu。如果 Cu 未知，也将基于 ISO 281 进行计算。

轴计算说明： 在此模块中，可通过输入维氏硬度来考虑表面硬度对静容量的影响。有关此内容的公式，请参见[68]。每个轴承的硬度值，根据其内几何形状计算后，预定义为HV 660，用于轴计算。

English Original

Bearing data

This is where the geometry of the bearing is defined (see chapter Rolling bearing calculation with internal geometry). In addition to the geometry data, you can also enter the basic dynamic load rating. If you do not know this value, the rating is calculated using the current geometry data as specified in ISO 281. If you require a modified rating life (see chapter Modified rating life calculation according to ISO 281), input the fatigue load limit Cu. If Cu is not known, it is also calculated on the basis of ISO 281.

Note for the shaft calculation: In this module, the effect of surface hardness on the static capacity can be taken into account by entering the Vickers hardness. You will find the formulae for this in [68]. The hardness value of every bearing calculated with their inner geometry is predefined as HV 660 for the shaft calculation.
