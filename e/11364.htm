4 个齿轮的几何-精细配比

在精细配比过程中无法更改中心距。此计算使用 基本数据 选项卡中输入的中心距。

但是，如果齿轮 4 是内齿轮，您还可以选择 双行星齿轮级 选项。如果选择 双行星齿轮级 选项，还会检查内齿轮的 V 圆直径，并且所需的输出减速比为 z3/z2。在这种情况下，所有中心距都会自动变化，并显示所有可能的解。结果中会显示 αM213、clearance13 和 clearance24 的值。

English Original

Geometry-Fine Sizing for 4 gears

Center distances cannot be changed in the fine sizing process. The center distances entered in the Basic data tab are used in this calculation.

However, if gear 4 is an internal toothing, you can also select the double planetary stage option. If you select the double planetary stage option, the internal gear’s V-circle diameter is also checked and the required output reduction is z3/z2. In this case, all center distances are varied automatically, and all possible solutions are displayed. Values for αM213, clearance13 and clearance24 are displayed in the results.
