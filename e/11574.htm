中文翻译

制造产生的波纹度

模拟与角度 θ（输入为 系数 2）垂直的波纹度。如果角度设置为 0，则表示使用基圆螺旋角 βb。

图 15.66：制造产生的波纹度

English Original

Waviness due to manufacturing

A waviness that is vertical, relative to angle θ (input as Factor 2) is simulated. If the angle is set to 0, this means that base helix angle βb is used.

Figure 15.66: Waviness due to manufacturing
