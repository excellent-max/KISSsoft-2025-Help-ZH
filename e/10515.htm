中文翻译

使用点（而不是曲线）进行齿形定义

齿形计算使用一个高度可靠的算法，从曲线（直线、渐开线、圆弧等）生成齿形。在极少数情况下，该算法不会产生可使用的解。在这种情况下，使用点的替代算法可能是合理的。

English Original

Use points (instead of curves) for the tooth form definition

The tooth form calculation uses a highly reliable algorithm to generate the tooth form from curves (lines, involutes, arcs of circle, etc.). In a few, rare cases, this algorithm does not produce a usable solution. In those situations, it may be sensible to use the alternative algorithm, which uses points.
