中文翻译

图形

图形表示可在"图形"菜单 图形 > 2D 几何 > 展开 中获得，用于可视化生成的齿形和公差条件。所有三种条件（最小、定义、最大）可以同时或单独查看。此外，可以自定义它们以在图形中显示或隐藏直径，以便于分析。此可视化有助于快速识别根切以及与所需齿几何形状的偏差。

English Original

Graphics

Graphical representations are available in the Graphics menu Graphics > Geometry 2D > Rollout visualizing the generated tooth forms and tolerance conditions. All three conditions (minimum, defined, maximum) can be viewed simultaneously or individually. In addition, they can be customized to show or hide diameters on the display for easier analysis. This visualization aids in quickly identifying undercuts and deviations from the desired tooth geometry.
