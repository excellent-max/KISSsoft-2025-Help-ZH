中文翻译

设置

在模块特定设置（计算 → 设置）中定义所需的抗滑动安全系数和抗屈服点安全系数。还可以考虑以下方面：

如果您已选择以壁厚作为毛坯直径计算材料强度，则将使用壁厚而非毛坯直径来计算轮毂材料的强度。

如果您选中考虑两个直径处的压力（Kollmann）标志，则大小两个锥体直径处的压力都会被考虑。否则，仅使用最大直径处的压力。

注意：

此说明仅适用于根据 Kollmann 的方法。根据 DIN 7190-2 计算时使用平均直径。

在设置下输入所需的抗滑动和抗屈服点安全系数。所需的安全系数定义了系统在尺寸设计过程中搜索的值。

对于根据 Kollmann 的方法，抗滑动所需安全系数的最小值取 1.0。抗屈服点所需安全系数的默认值为 2.0。

DIN 7190-2 建议将抗屈服点安全系数设置为 1.2，以确保元件在液压拆卸时不受塑性流动的影响。

对于中心载荷作用，抗滑动安全系数应至少设置为 1.2。

English Original

Settings

Define the required safeties against sliding and against yield point in the module-specific settings (Calculation → Settings). The following aspects can also be taken into account:

If you have selected Calculate material strength with wall thickness as raw diameter, the strength of the hub material is calculated using the wall thickness instead of the raw diameter.

If you select the Consider pressure at both diameters (Kollmann) flag, the pressure at both the large and small cone diameters is taken into account. Otherwise, only the pressure at the largest diameter is used.

Note:

This note only applies for the method according to Kollmann. The average diameter is used for the calculation according to DIN 7190-2.

Enter the required safeties for sliding and yield point under Settings. The required safeties define the values the system searches for during sizing.

For the method according to Kollmann, a minimum safety of 1.0 is used for the required safety against sliding. The default required safety against yield point value is 2.0.

DIN 7190-2 recommends that the safety against the yield point is set to 1.2, to ensure that the elements are not affected by plastic flow when they are disassembled hydraulically.

The safety against sliding for a central load application should be set to at least 1.2.
