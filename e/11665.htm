齿向载荷系数

齿向载荷系数 KHβ、KFβ 和 KBβ 考虑了齿宽上载荷分布不均对接触应力、齿根应力和胶合应力的影响。您可以指定齿向载荷系数是设为常数值还是根据其他值计算。如果已知齿向载荷系数 KHβ，请选择 自定义输入 方法并输入该值。

这里的通常设置是 按计算方法计算。齿向载荷系数随后根据强度计算标准（ISO、AGMA 或 DIN）中使用的公式计算。您需要输入若干值（安装系数）。其他值可在 齿向载荷系数设置 窗口中输入。

采用 按计算方法计算 与 轴不对中 方法根据强度计算标准（ISO、AGMA、DIN）中的公式计算齿向载荷系数。同时计算不对中量 V(E)、H(P)、J(G) 和轴交角误差。存储在轴计算中的关联轴也用于 轴线对中… 窗口中。不对中量在计算时不迭代齿接触中的载荷分布，因此不会因齿接触中的载荷分布而改变轴的弯曲挠度线。

可以考虑齿轮体的扭转。此时，计算假设涉及实心圆柱或空心圆柱（外径 = 齿根圆 + 0.4*法向模数，或啮合节圆，取决于 设置 中预定义的内容，孔径 = 内径）。换言之，内径被考虑在内，一侧的扭矩为零。扭矩沿齿宽线性分布（扭转导致的抛物线变形过程）。可以选择哪一侧承受扭转力矩。在这种情况下，I 和 II 指同一侧，输入轮齿修形时也是如此。

采用 按 ISO 6336 附录 E 及轴不对中计算 方法，基于齿接触计算齿向载荷系数。如 X 中所述，它计算啮合过程中的任何脱开，从而确定整个齿宽上的载荷分布。齿向载荷系数在迭代齿接触中载荷分布的情况下计算，因此包含齿接触中载荷分布导致的轴弯曲挠度线的变化。轴挠度和齿面修形在载荷分布中被考虑。然而，由于锥齿轮和准双曲面齿轮齿面几何（无修形）导致的齿向载荷分布未被考虑，且假定为均匀分布。由于锥齿轮和准双曲面齿轮通常具有较大的齿长修形，应在 修形 选项卡中指定常用修形，以实现齿向载荷系数的实际计算。

齿宽被划分为切片，以帮助您按 ISO 6336 附录 E 计算齿向载荷系数：您可以在 定义切片数 对话框中设置按附录 E 计算齿向载荷系数的精度。单击计算方法旁边的加号按钮打开此对话框。

在 齿向载荷系数设置 中，您可以定义载荷系数 KA 和 Kγ 的使用方式。根据 ISO 6336-1 附录 E 计算载荷分布和轴线对中时，可将其考虑在内。

同时计算不对中量 V(E)、H(P)、J(G) 和轴交角误差。存储在轴计算中的关联轴也用于 轴线对中… 窗口中。不对中量在计算时不迭代齿接触中的载荷分布，因此不会因齿接触中的载荷分布而改变轴的弯曲挠度线。

English Original

Face load factor

The face load factors KHβ, KFβ and KBβ take into account the influence of an uneven load distribution over the facewidth on the contact stress, tooth root stress and scuffing stress. You can specify that the face load factor is either to be set as a constant value or calculated from other values. If you already know the face load factor KHβ, select the Own input method and input this value.

The usual setting here is Calculation according to calculation method. The face load factor is then calculated according to the formulae used in the strength calculation standard (ISO, AGMA or DIN). You will need to input a number of values (mounting factor). Other values can be entered in the Settings for face load factor window.

The calculation according to calculation method and Shaft misalignment method calculates the face load factor according to the formulae in the strength calculation standard (ISO, AGMA, DIN). The misalignments V(E), H(P), J(G) and axis angle error are also calculated. The associated shafts that were stored in the shaft calculation are also used in the Axis alignment … window. Misalignments are calculated without iterating the load distribution in the tooth contact and therefore do not change the shaft bending line due to load distribution in the tooth contact.

Torsion of the gear body can be taken into account. Here, the calculation assumes a solid cylinder or hollow cylinder (external diameter = root circle + 0.4*normal module or operating pitch circle, depending on what has been predefined under Settings, bore = inside diameter) is involved. In other words, the inside diameter is taken into account and the torque on one side is zero. The torque is distributed in a linear fashion along the facewidth (parabolic course of deformation by torsion). You can select which side is to be subjected to torsional moment. In this case, I and II refer to the same side, as is also the case when you enter the toothing modifications.

The Calculation according to ISO 6336 Annex E and misalignment of shafts method calculates the face load factor on the basis of the tooth contact. As described in X, it calculates any gaping during meshing, and therefore defines the load distribution over the entire facewidth. The face load factor is calculated with iterating the load distribution in the tooth contact and therefore includes the change in the shaft bending line due to load distribution in the tooth contact. Shaft deflections and flank modifications are take into account in the load distribution. However, the face load distribution due to bevel and hypoid gear flank geometry (without modifications) is not taken into account and is assumed to be homogenous. As bevel gears and hypoid gears usually have higher length correction, you should specify the usual modifications in the Modifications tab to achieve a realistic calculation of the face load factor.

The facewidth is divided into slices to help you calculate the face load factor as defined in ISO 6336, Annex E: You can set the accuracy of the face load factor calculation according to Annex E in the Define number of slices dialog. Click the plus button next to the calculation method to open this dialog.

In Settings for face load factor, you can define the how load factors KA and Kγ are used. They can be taken into account when calculating load distribution and axis alignment according to ISO 6336-1, Annex E.

The misalignments V(E), H(P), J(G) and axis angle error are also calculated. The associated shafts that were stored in the shaft calculation are also used in the Axis alignment … window. Misalignments are calculated without iterating the load distribution in the tooth contact and therefore do not change the shaft bending line due to load distribution in the tooth contact.
