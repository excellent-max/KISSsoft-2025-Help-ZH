中文翻译

锥度

可以通过两种方式确定锥角：单击半锥角 β 输入字段右侧的"+"按钮。

  • 通过设置锥度：
    锥度定义如下：
    x = l/(D0-D1)。x 对应要输入的值。
  • 通过选择莫氏锥柄：
    莫氏锥度按 DIN 228 定义，锥度介于 1:19.212 和 1:20.02 之间。
English Original

Conicity

The cone angle can be determined in two ways, by clicking on the Plus button to the right of the input field for the half cone angle β:

  • by setting a conicity:
    The conicity is defined as follows:
    x = l/(D0-D1). x corresponds to the value to be input.
  • by selecting a morse taper shank:
    Morse tapers are defined in DIN 228 and have a conicity of between 1:19.212 and 1:20.02.
