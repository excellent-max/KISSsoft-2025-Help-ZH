中文翻译

胶合与滑动速度（面齿轮）

该图形显示了面齿轮抗胶合的安全系数。然而，由于滑动速度差异很大且齿面上的接触应力不断变化，实际计算抗胶合安全系数非常困难。Akahori [58] 报告了在较高滑动速度下出现严重胶合问题。因此，应当考虑如何计算胶合风险。如上文针对应力分布所述，一个合理的方案是分段计算抗胶合安全系数。

该图形显示了胶合安全系数沿齿面的变化过程，该系数按闪温和积分温度准则定义。为了使该计算获得真实的结果，必须确保每一段都以相同的本体温度进行计算。然而，当您进行该计算时，会发现基于积分温度计算时安全系数会发生显著变化。特别是当啮合线上的 E 点接近节点时会出现这种情况。如果您随后使用 DIN 3990 中的公式将 E 点的齿面温度转换为平均齿面温度，得到的结果将不是特别精确。因此，我们建议您在对面齿轮进行该计算时使用闪温作为准则。

English Original

Scuffing and sliding velocity (face gear)

The graphic displays the safety against scuffing for face gears. However, due to the very different sliding velocities and the changing contact stress across the tooth flank, calculating the safety against scuffing is actually very difficult. Akahori [58] reports massive problems with scuffing at a higher sliding velocity. For this reason, it is appropriate to think about how to calculate the risk of scuffing. One sensible option, as described above for stress distribution, is to calculate the safety against scuffing in separate sections.

The graphic shows the progression of scuffing safety as defined by the flash and integral temperature criterion along the tooth flank. To achieve realistic results from this calculation, it must be ensured that every section is calculated with the same mass temperature. However, when you work through the calculation, you will see there are significant changes in safety when the calculation is performed on the basis of the integral temperature. In particular, this happens as point E on the path of contact gets closer to the pitch point. If you then use the formulae in DIN 3990 to convert the flank temperature at point E to the average flank temperature, the results you get will not be particularly precise. For this reason, we recommend you use the flash temperature as the criterion when you perform this calculation for face gears.
