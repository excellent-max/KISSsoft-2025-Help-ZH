中文翻译

允许的最大齿厚磨损量

计算磨损安全系数时，必须指定允许的磨损阈值。塑料的常用值为 15%（分度圆处的齿厚磨损）。如果不允许磨损或只允许很小磨损，我们建议您输入 5% 到 10% 之间的值。

English Original

Permissible maximum wear of tooth thickness

When you calculate wear safety, you must specify a permitted wear threshold value. A usual value for plastic is 15% (tooth thickness wear in the reference circle). If no, or only very little, wear can be tolerated, we recommend you input a value between 5 and 10%.
