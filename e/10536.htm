中文翻译

连接元件

若干同轴轴可以由两种不同的连接元件连接："一般连接"、"连接滚动轴承"或"连接滑动轴承"。

这些轴之间的连接元件定义了连接点处轴的三个沿x、y和z轴方向的相对位移自由度，以及围绕x、y和z轴的三个相对旋转自由度。

English Original

Connection elements

A number of coaxial shafts can be connected by two different connection elements: "General connection", "Connecting rolling bearing" or "Connecting plain bearing".

The connecting element between these shafts defines the shaft's degree of freedom at the connection point for the three relative displacements along the x-, y- and z-axis, and three relative rotations around the x, y and z axis.
