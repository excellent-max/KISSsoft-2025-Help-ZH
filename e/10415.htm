中文翻译

坎贝尔图

从 计算 > 坎贝尔图 菜单选项调用此功能。在相关选项卡中，可以选择要分析的轴、轴转速范围、计算转速所用的计算步数，以及要显示的共振曲线（正向涡动）数量。

坎贝尔图在更宽的轴转速范围内显示特征频率，使用户能够跟踪与特征模态相关的正向和反向涡动。要计算 坎贝尔图，请在基本数据选项卡中设置特征频率的数量。陀螺效应会导致特征频率发生较大变化。可通过在基本数据选项卡中勾选“考虑陀螺效应”选项来考虑该效应。

在正常情况下，反向涡动的频率会降低，而正向涡动的频率会升高。在正向涡动的情况下，随着轴速的增加，陀螺效应对弹簧刚度的影响增大，从而提高特征频率。对于反向旋转，效果则相反，因此增加轴转速会降低有效刚度，从而降低特征频率。特征频率还受到轴承刚度的影响。

English Original

Campbell diagram

Call this function from the Calculations > Campbell diagram menu option. In the relevant tab, you can select the shaft to be analyzed, the range of shaft speeds, the number of calculation steps with which the speed is calculated, and the number of resonance curves (forwards whirl) to be displayed.

The Campbell diagram shows the eigenfrequencies in a wider range of shaft speeds, which enables you to follow the forward and backwards whirl associated with the eigenmodes. To calculate the Campbell diagram, set the number of eigenfrequencies in the Basic data tab. The gyroscopic effect causes large changes in the eigenfrequencies. You can take it into consideration by clicking selecting the Consider gyroscopic effect option in the Basic data tab.

In normal cases, the backwards whirl drops in frequency, while the forwards whirl increases in frequency. In the case of forwards whirl, as shaft speed increases, the gyroscopic effect increasingly affects the spring stiffness and increases the eigenfrequencies. The effect is reversed for backward rotation, so increasing shaft spin speed reduces the effective stiffness, and thus reduces the eigenfrequencies. The eigenfrequencies are also affected by the stiffness of the bearings.
