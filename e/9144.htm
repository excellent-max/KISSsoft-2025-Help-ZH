手册和搜索

手册的目录和搜索功能也可以作为停靠窗口使用。当您通过双击选择某个条目时，帮助文本查看器（参见"帮助文本查看器"章）将打开并显示手册中的相关章节。

English Original

Manual and Search

The manual's Table of Contents and search function are also available as docking windows. When you select an entry, by double-clicking on it, the Helptext viewer (see chapter Helptext Viewer) opens and the relevant section in the manual is displayed.
