中文翻译

峰值过载系数

峰值过载系数 KAP（根据 DNV 41.2）用于计算胶合，当发生应用系数 KA 未涵盖的短时高过载时使用。在这种情况下，齿根和齿面用 KA 计算，但胶合用 KAP 计算。此方法在特殊应用中特别有用。它使该方法学也可用于根据 ISO 标准进行的计算。

English Original

Peak overload factor

The peak overload factor KAP according to DNV 41.2 is used when calculating scuffing, when a short-period high overload, which is not covered by the application factor KA, occurs. In this case, the root and flank are calculated with KA, but the scuffing is calculated with KAP. This procedure is especially useful in the case of special applications. It enables the methodology to also be used for calculations according to the ISO standard.
