中文翻译

无外力的延伸套筒

您可以在“延伸套筒”对话框中指定单个延伸套筒的长度。在单螺栓（螺纹孔）连接中，延伸套筒可用于螺栓头下方。在通螺栓连接中，延伸套筒可用于螺栓头下方和螺母下方。
此处定义的延伸套筒上不得承受任何外力。这些套筒用于在螺栓头或螺母与零件之间创建空间。

在确定长度、计算弹性以及工作温度下的长度膨胀时，都会计入延伸套筒的影响。

English Original

Extension sleeves without external forces

You can specify the length of an individual extension sleeve in the extension sleeves dialog. In a single-bolted (tapped thread) connection, a extension sleeve can be used under the bolt head. In a through-bolt connection, an extension sleeve can be used under the bolt head and under the nut.
No external forces are to act on the extension sleeves defined here. These sleeves are to be used to create the space between the bolt head or nut and the part.

The extension sleeves are taken into account when sizing the length, calculating the resiliences, and in the length expansion at operating temperature.
