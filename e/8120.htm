流体动压径向滑动轴承

Niemann [8] 提供了一种非常精确的计算可在高速下运行的径向滑动轴承的方法。该方法对于椭圆间隙或可倾瓦滑动轴承也能得出良好的结果。

ISO 7902 [69] 或 DIN 31652 包含一种非常完善且详细的方法，用于计算在低速至中速下运行的静态流体动压径向滑动轴承。对于在高速下运行的轴承，请使用同样出色的 DIN 31657 [70]。

English Original

Hydrodynamic Plain Journal Bearings

Niemann [8] provides a very accurate method for calculating plain radial bearings that can run at high speeds. This also produces good results for oval-clearance or tilting pad plain bearings.

ISO 7902 [69] or DIN 31652 include a very good and detailed method for calculating stationary hydrodynamic plain radial bearings that are to run at low to medium speeds. For those running at high speeds, use the equally excellent DIN 31657 [70].
