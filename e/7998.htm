螺栓和销

根据使用位置的不同，已创建五种计算类型来对螺栓/销连接进行分类：

  • 受扭矩作用的横向销
    在涉及大作用力的横向销连接中，检查轴和轮毂的表面压力以及销的剪切。
  • 受扭矩作用的纵向销
    纵向销连接承受轴和轮毂中的表面压力以及销上的剪切。
  • 受弯曲力作用的导销
    导销连接承受由力矩引起的弯曲应力和由横向力引起的剪切力。在这种情况下，计算销的剪切力、表面压力和弯曲，以及零件上的表面压力。
  • 受剪切作用的螺栓连接（双剪）
    在这种布置中，螺栓承受弯曲应力、剪切应力和表面压力。根据杆/螺栓和叉/螺栓连接的配合，可以使用不同的计算方法。经验表明，在非滑动表面的尺寸计算中，起决定作用的数值是弯曲应力。在滑动表面中，则是表面压力。
  • 圆周排列的螺栓（单剪）
    在这种布置中，有效扭矩均匀分布在各螺栓/销上。因此，轴和轮毂承受来自各螺栓/销的表面压力，从而产生剪切力。同时输出最大剪切应力（按 Roloff Matek [80] 第 254 页计算）和螺栓的最小安全系数。

作用在螺栓、轴和轮毂（或零件）上的载荷按经典技术文献（Niemann, Maschinenelemente I，第 4 版，2005 [8]）所述进行计算，并确定安全系数，但圆周排列的螺栓除外。

螺旋弹簧销和槽式弹簧销（衬套）的横截面积和抗弯截面模量按 Decker [81] 计算。在螺栓、螺旋弹簧销和槽式弹簧销（衬套）仅承受剪切的配置中，可将相关 DIN EN ISO 标准中规定的许用剪切力应用于销。

Bolts and Pins

Five types of calculation have been created to categorize bolt/pin connections, depending on where they are used:

  • Cross pin under torque
    The surface pressure of the shaft and hub, and the shearing of the pin, are checked in cross pin connections where large forces are involved.
  • Longitudinal pin under torque
    Longitudinal pin connections are subject to surface pressure in the shaft and hub and shearing on the pin.
  • Guide pin under bending force
    Guide pin connections are subject to bending stress due to a moment and to shearing force caused by transverse forces. The pin's shearing force, surface pressure and bending, and the surface pressure on the part, are calculated in this case.
  • Bolt connection under shearing action (in double shear)
    The bolt is subject to bending and shear stress and to surface pressure in this arrangement. You can use different calculation methods, depending on the fit of the rod/bolt and fork/bolt connections. Experience shows that the decisive value in sizing non-sliding surfaces is the bending stress. In sliding surfaces, it is the surface pressure.
  • Bolts in a circular layout (in single shear)
    In this arrangement, the effective torque is distributed uniformly over the individual bolts/pins. Therefore, the shaft and hub are subject to surface pressure from the individual bolts/pins, creating a shearing force. The maximum shear stress (calculated as specified in Roloff Matek [80], page 254) and the minimum safety for the bolts are also output.

The load placed on the bolts, shaft and hub (or part) is calculated, and the safeties are determined, as described in classic technical literature (Niemann, Maschinenelemente I, 4th Edition, 2005 [8]), with the exception of Bolts in a circular layout.

The cross-sectional area and moment of resistance to bending of spiral and slotted spring pins (bushes) is calculated according to Decker [81]. In configurations in which the bolts, spiral and slotted spring pins (bushes) are only subjected to shearing, the permitted shearing force specified in the relevant DIN EN ISO standard can be applied to the pins.
