压力角修形（角分）

图（参见图 15.47）显示了齿廓图中定义的压力角修形（角度）。此图中还显示了 系数 1 设置。修形在齿顶和齿根处的起点可以在 制造 选项卡中设置。

压力角修形（角分）以类似的方式设置为线性齿顶修形（参见 线性齿顶和齿根修形 一章）。然而，区别在于压力角修形施加于整个滚动长度上。请在系数 1 中输入以角分为单位的压力角修形值。

English Original

Pressure angle modification (arc minutes)

Figure (see Figure 15.47) shows the pressure angle modification (angle) as defined in the profile diagram. The Factor 1 setting is also shown in this figure. The start of the modification at the tip and root can be set in the Manufacturing tab.

The pressure angle modification (angle-minutes) is set as a linear tip relief, in a similar way (see chapter Tip and root relief, linear). The difference is, however, that the pressure angle modification is applied across the entire roll length. Enter the pressure angle modification value in minutes of an angle in Coefficient 1.
