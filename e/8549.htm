尺寸标注

在 KISSsoft 中，有多种与其他常用方法（例如圆柱齿轮的方法）差异很大的方法，用于确定面齿轮复杂齿形的尺寸。对于面齿轮，您必须选择一种几何形状，以防止在齿轮外侧面产生尖齿，并确保内侧面不发生（或极少发生）根切。您在计算齿形时必须执行这些检查。实际的几何计算过程将数据转换为当量锥齿轮和虚拟圆柱齿轮。在齿形计算过程中，面齿轮在沿其齿宽设置的若干截面中进行计算。要指定所需的截面数，请选择 计算 菜单。然后，选择 设置 > 计算设置 > 齿形 > 齿形截面数 并输入所需的数值。在 2D 几何 > 啮合 图形窗口中，您可以同时在内径、外径和齿的中部显示齿形。在这里，您可以查看齿顶面（法向齿顶厚）和根切是否可接受。

您可以采取以下措施来防止齿轮中出现尖齿或根切：

  • 更改齿宽偏移量 bv
  • 减小齿宽
  • 更改压力角
  • 更改齿宽外侧部分的齿顶

注释

  • 要生成鼓形齿形：可以通过使用比啮合小齿轮多一两个齿的插齿刀，在面齿轮的齿线上生成齿线鼓形。通过选择 图形 > 2D 几何 > 啮合 使用 2D 显示中的保存功能，以检查生成的齿形之间的差异。为此，定义一个与用于计算齿形的小齿轮具有相同齿数的插齿刀。通过单击 保存 曲线 图标 保存面齿轮齿形，然后增加插齿刀的齿数。如果面齿轮的轴向偏移量 bv 较大，您可以将鼓形移动到一侧。
English Original

Dimensioning

In KISSsoft, a wide variety of procedures that differ greatly from other commonly-used procedures, e.g. for cylindrical gears, for dimensioning the complex tooth forms in face gears. For a face gear, you must select a geometry that prevents the creation of pointed teeth on the outside face of the gear and ensures that no (or very little) undercut occurs on the inside face. You must perform these checks when you calculate the tooth form. The actual geometry calculation procedure converts the data into the equivalent bevel gear and the virtual cylindrical gear. In the tooth form calculation process, a face gear is calculated in a number of sections set along its facewidth. To specify the number of required sections, select the Calculation menu. Then, select Settings > Calculation settings > Tooth form > Number of sections for tooth form and input the required number. In the 2D Geometry > Meshing graphics window, you can display the tooth form simultaneously on the internal diameter, external diameter and in the middle of the tooth. Here you can see whether the top land (normal crest width) and undercut are acceptable.

You can take these measures to prevent pointed teeth or undercuts occurring in the gear:

  • change facewidth offset bv
  • reduce the facewidth
  • change the pressure angle
  • alter the tip in the outside part of the facewidth

Notes

  • To generate a crowned tooth form: You can generate flank line crowning on the flank line of face gears by using a pinion type cutter that has one or two more teeth than the meshing pinion. Use the save function in the 2D display by selecting Graphics > 2D Geometry > Meshing to check the difference between the generated tooth forms. To do this, define a pinion type cutter with the same number of teeth as the pinion used to calculate the tooth form. Save the face gear tooth form by clicking the Save curve icon and then increasing the number of teeth on the pinion-type cutter. If the face gear has a large axial offset bv, you can displace the crowning to one side.
