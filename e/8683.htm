中文翻译

按 ISO 6336 定义的寿命系数

设置寿命系数 ZNT，以根据 ISO 6336-2:2006 降低许用材料应力：

如 ISO 6336 所述，此值对圆柱齿轮计算至关重要，也是与 DIN 3990 相比在耐久极限范围内安全系数较低的原因。

  1. 正常（在 1010 次循环时降低至 0.85）：在耐久极限（齿根和齿面）范围内的许用材料应力再次降低。寿命系数 Y NT 和 ZNT 对于 ≥1010 次载荷循环被设为 0.85。
  2. 质量更好时提高（降低至 0.92）：Y NT 和 ZNT 在 ≥ 1010 次载荷循环被设为 0.92（依据 ISO 9085 中的数据）。
  3. 在质量和经验均最佳时（始终为 1.0）： 此选项消除了这一降低，因此与 DIN 3990 一致。然而，这要求对材料进行最佳处理和监控。
English Original

Life factors as defined in ISO 6336

Set the life factor ZNT to reduce the permitted material stress according to ISO 6336-2:2006:

As stated in ISO 6336, this value is important for cylindrical gear calculations and is the reason for the lower safeties for the range of endurance limit, compared with DIN 3990.

  1. normal (reduction to 0.85 at 1010 cycles): The permitted material stress in the range of endurance limit (root and flank) is reduced again. The life factors Y NT and ZNT for ≥1010 load cycles are set to 0.85.
  2. increased if the quality is better (reduced to 0.92): Y NT and ZNT are set to 0.92 for ≥ 1010 load cycles (in accordance with the data in ISO 9085).
  3. with optimum quality and experience (always 1.0): This removes the reduction and therefore corresponds to DIN 3990. However, this assumes the optimum treatment and monitoring of the materials.
