中文翻译

齿形

显示轮齿齿形。

注意：

单击图形上方的“属性”按钮可指定要显示的齿数。您可以显示端面、法向或轴向截面中的齿轮。如果您想导出齿形并在之后重新导入 KISSsoft，选择“导出半齿”选项也非常有用。

English Original

Tooth form

Displaying a gear tooth form.

Note:

Click the Property button above the graphic to specify the number of teeth that are to be displayed. You can display the gear in transverse section, normal section or axial section. Selecting the "Half tooth for export" option is also very useful if you want to export the tooth form and then reimport it into KISSsoft later on.
