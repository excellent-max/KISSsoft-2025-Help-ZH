中文翻译

面齿轮

面齿轮是一种特殊类型的锥齿轮。虽然面齿轮小齿轮是普通的圆柱齿轮，但它具有复杂的 3D 齿形。与锥齿轮不同，面齿轮完全不受轴向位移的影响。因此，面齿轮更容易装配。

KISSsoft 面齿轮 计算模块计算具有偏置和任意轴交角 Σ 的直齿或斜齿圆柱齿轮小齿轮与面齿轮成对啮合的几何参数。在这种情况下，针对 0 mm 的偏置和 Σ=90° 的轴交角计算强度和 2D 几何。在其他任何情况下，您都可以在这些限制条件下进行预尺寸计算，然后将 所需的准双曲面偏置和轴交角到 3D 体积模型中。在 几何 停靠窗口中，您可以同时显示面齿轮在其内径、中径和外径处或任意多个截面处的齿形。您可以使用此工具检查面齿轮内径或外径上的根切和尖齿。在 修形 选项卡中，您可以在外侧或内侧输入字段中找到齿顶修形值/长度，即 hake(i) 和 lake(i)。在此，您可以输入额外参数，以帮助防止齿轮中出现尖齿。系统通过模拟使用插齿刀的制造过程来计算面齿轮上的齿形。强度计算基于圆柱齿轮或锥齿轮的既定标准。

面齿轮计算说明
English Original

Face gears

Face gears are a special type of bevel gear. Although a face gear pinion is a normal cylindrical gear, it has a complex 3D-tooth form. Unlike a bevel gear, a face gear is absolutely unaffected by axial displacement. For this reason, face gears are much easier to assemble.

The KISSsoft Face gears calculation module calculates the geometry of pairs of straight or helical cylindrical gear pinions with face gears with offset and with any shaft angle Σ. In this case, the strength and 2D geometry are calculated for an offset of 0 mm and a shaft angle Σ=90°. In every other case, you can perform the pre-sizing with these restrictions and then add the required hypoid offset and shaft angle to the 3D volume model. In the Geometry docking window, you can display the tooth form of a face gear for its inside, middle and outside diameter or for any number of sections all at the same time. You use this tool to check for undercut and pointed teeth on the internal or external diameter of the face gear. In the Modifications tab, you will find the value/length of tip alteration at outside or inside input fields, hake(i) and lake(i). Here, you can input additional parameters that will help prevent pointed teeth occurring in the gear. The system calculates the tooth form on the face gear by simulating manufacturing using a pinion type cutter. The strength calculation is based on the use of established standards for cylindrical or bevel gears.
