类型比较

选择最合适的滚动轴承类型有时并非易事。下表概述了最重要的特性。为特定工况选择的滚动轴承通常已由其特性所决定。您可以使用这些信息来为常见工作情况和特殊要求选择所需的轴承。但是，结果可能会重叠，因此成本因素可能具有决定性作用。

Comparing types

Selecting the most suitable type of rolling bearing is sometimes no easy matter. The table below gives an overview of the most important properties. The rolling bearing you select for specific operating conditions has often already been determined by its properties and properties. You can use this information to select the bearing you require for frequently occurring working cases and for specialized requirements. However, results may overlap, and therefore the cost factor may be decisive.

In addition to the specified rolling bearing types, some hybrid bearings (with ceramic rolling bodies) have been included, for some types. The special properties of these bearings are described in the "Hybrid bearing" section.
