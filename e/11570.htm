中文翻译

齿廓形状偏差

设置双振幅 (μm)、波长（以模数为单位）以及从齿顶到波峰的距离（以模数为单位），如图 15.63 所示。波长在滚动长度方向上是恒定的，因为波纹通常源于机械加工（沿加工啮合线方向）。

图 15.64：齿廓形状偏差

English Original

Profile form deviation

The double amplitude (μm), wave length (in module) and the start, distance from the tip until the wave maximum (in module) are set, as shown in (see Figure 15.63). The wave length is constant in the direction of the roll length, as the waviness typically results from machining (in the direction of the machining path of contact).
