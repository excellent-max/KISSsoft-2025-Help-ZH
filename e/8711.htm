中文翻译

有限寿命系数

有限寿命系数决定可以在有限寿命和强度字段中输入哪些材料值。在标准应用中，高达 1010 次载荷循环的无限寿命强度值，齿根从 100% 降低到 90%，齿面降低到 85%。根据 AGMA，强度的降低还延伸到超过 1010 次载荷循环。在必须不惜一切代价防止齿轮装置失效的关键应用中，材料值比标准应用领域中使用的值降低得更多。

English Original

Limited life coefficients

The limited life coefficients determine which material values can be entered in the field for limited time and strength. In standard applications, infinite life strength values up to 1010 load cycles are reduced from 100% to 90% for the root and to 85% for the flank. According to AGMA, the reduction in strength also extends beyond 1010 load cycles. In critical applications, where a gear unit breakdown must be prevented at all cost, the material values are reduced even more, in comparison to those used in standard application areas.
