中文翻译

SKF 球面滚子推力轴承：跳动误差考虑载荷分布

选择 计算 > 设置 时，您会看到此选项。用它来指定轴的同轴度误差或跳动误差是否影响轴承中载荷的分布方式。如果选中此选项，则不考虑该影响。如果是这种情况，则乘以系数 0.88 来计算当量动载荷 P（P=0.88*(Fa+Y*Fr)）。

English Original

SKF spherical roller thrust bearings: runout error takes into account load distribution

When you select Calculation > Settings, you see this option. Use it to specify whether shaft coaxial errors or runout errors influence the way loads are distributed in the bearing. If this option is selected, the influence is not taken into account. If that is the case, a coefficient of 0.88 is multiplied to calculate the dynamic equivalent load P (P=0.88*(Fa+Y*Fr)).
