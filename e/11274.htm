缩短的齿廓鼓形修整

图（见图 15.45）显示了在齿廓图中规定的缩短的齿廓鼓形修整。图中还显示了数值、系数 1 和 系数 2 设置。可在制造选项卡中设置齿顶和齿根处修形的起始位置。

English Original

Profile crowning, shortened

Figure (see Figure 15.45) shows the shortened profile crowning as specified in the profile diagram. The Value, Factor 1 and Factor 2 settings are also shown in this figure. The start of modification at tip and at root can be set in the Manufacturing tab.

Shortened profile crowning is used in combination with a tip relief. The tip relief should start at ds.
