中文翻译

允许的质量降低

根据蜗轮的构造类型，其精度可能会随时间推移因磨损而下降。该值不得低于此输入字段中指定的值。质量下降与材料的塑性变形有关，因此对应更高的材料性能。这反过来又使齿根具有更高的抗塑性变形安全系数。

English Original

Permissible decrease in quality

Depending on the construction type of the worm wheel, it may experience a decrease in quality over time due to wear. This value must not sink below the value specified in this input field. A decrease in quality is linked to the plastic deformation of the material, and therefore a higher material property. This, in turn, results in a higher safety against plastic deformation in the root.
