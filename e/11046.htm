中文翻译

齿轮 1 的齿顶高系数（中点）、齿轮 2 的齿顶高系数（中点）

可通过改变齿轮 1 和齿轮 2 的齿顶高系数来调整锥齿轮的基准齿廓。然后，通过指定齿顶高系数和“所需顶隙”，即可计算出配对齿轮（齿轮 2 和齿轮 1）的齿根高系数。

English Original

Addendum coefficient gear 1 (middle), addendum coefficient gear 2 (middle)

You can vary the reference profile of the bevel gears by changing the addendum coefficients of gear 1 and gear 2. You can then calculate the dedendum coefficients of the counter gear (gear 2 and gear 1) by specifying the addendum coefficient and the "Required tip clearance".
