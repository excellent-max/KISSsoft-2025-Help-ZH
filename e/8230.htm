中文翻译

抗变形/断裂的安全系数

KISSsoft 使用 Hänchen 的定义，根据最大载荷的频率计算所需的抗疲劳断裂安全系数。如果频率为 100%，则规定安全系数为 2.0。在 0% 时，为 1.0。不过，安全系数在这两个极端之间并不遵循线性变化。

所需的抗过载失效安全系数为 3.5 至 5.0，具体取决于所涉及的应用类型或准则。所需的抗变形（屈服点）安全系数通常为 2.0 至 3.5。

English Original

Safety against deformation/fracture

KISSsoft calculates the required safety against fatigue fracture, depending on the frequency of the maximum load, using Hänchen's definitions. If the frequency is 100%, the specified safety is 2.0. At 0%, it is 1.0. However, the safety does not follow a linear progression between these two extremes.

The required safety against overload failure is 3.5 to 5.0, depending on the type of application or guideline involved. The required safety against deformation (yield point) is usually 2.0 to 3.5.
