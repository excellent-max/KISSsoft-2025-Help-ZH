中文翻译

尺寸计算

可以显示并采用符合 ISO/DIN 规定的轴/轮毂公差对。

KISSsoft 有一个非常方便的选型功能，您可以用它来选择合适的公差对。标准化的公差对存储在 M01-001.dat 文件中。单击公差输入字段旁边的选型按钮以打开选型屏幕。

根据名义所需安全系数（您可以在设置中更改），您可以确定所有满足要求的公差对（足够的抗滑动安全系数、足够的抗断裂和屈服点安全系数）。结果随后显示在列表中。

过盈配合的扭矩、轴向力、结合直径和长度
KISSsoft 可以选型最大可传递扭矩、可传递轴向力、所需长度和直径（根据您在设置中输入的安全系数要求值）。

English Original

Sizings

Possible shaft/hub tolerance pairs can be displayed and adopted as specified in ISO/DIN.

KISSsoft has a very convenient sizing function that you can use for suitable tolerance pairs. Standardized tolerance pairs are stored in the M01-001.dat file. Click the Sizing button next to the tolerance input field to open the sizing screen.

Based on the nominal required safety (which you can change in Settings), you can determine all the tolerance pairs which fulfill the requirements (sufficient safety against sliding, sufficient safety against fracture and yield point). The results are then displayed in a list.

Torque, axial force, joint diameter, and length of interference fit
KISSsoft can size the maximum transmittable torque, transmittable axial force, required length, and diameter (according to the required safety values you have entered in the Settings).
