必要安全系数

FKM 指南是少数几个根据失效后果等列出所需安全系数的计算指南之一。当与安全的载荷假设以及强度变量 Pü=97.5% 的平均可靠度一起使用时，它们适用于焊接和非焊接零件。安全系数根据所选材料、定义的失效后果以及特定载荷出现的概率，由检查和试验的结果确定。该指南区分了五个不同类别：钢、铸铁（延性或非延性）和铝（延性或非延性）。您也可以手动指定安全系数。

English Original

Required safeties

The FKM Guideline is one of the few calculation guidelines that lists the required safeties according to the consequences of failure etc. When used together with safe load assumptions and an average reliability of the strength variables Pü=97.5%, they apply for both welded and non welded parts. Safety factors are defined as the results of inspections and tests, on the basis of the selected material, the defined consequences of failure and the probability that the specific load will occur. This guideline differentiates between five different classes: steel, cast iron (ductile or non ductile), and aluminum (ductile or non ductile). You can also specify the safety factors manually.
