如何测试软件？

该软件的演示版可参见（"基本安装"章）。尽管演示版没有过期日期，其功能有限，例如您不能更改和存储材料数据。演示版旨在让您对软件有一个初步印象。如需详细试用，请申请试用版（见"试用版"章）。试用版可运行30天，免费且与完整版相同（不含第三方程序）。

English Original

How can I test the software?

A demo version of the software is available at (see chapter Basic installation). Although the demo version does not have an expiration date, its functionality is limited so that, for example, you cannot change and store material data. The demo version is designed to give you an initial impression of the software. For a detailed trial, request a test version (see chapter Test version). The test version runs for 30 days, is free of charge and is the same as the full version (without third party programs).
