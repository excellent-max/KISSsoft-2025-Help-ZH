输入安全系数

如果选中此选项，可在右侧输入所需的安全系数。或者，单击 Plus 按钮以显示 定义安全系数 对话框，在其中可按 FKM 的规定指定安全系数。

静态强度计算的安全系数 jm（过载失效）和 jp（变形）根据指南第 1.5 节确定，疲劳强度安全系数 jD 根据指南第 2.5 部分确定。您可以在该指南中找到详细注释。

jm、jp：这些值适用于

-

失效导致严重损坏

-

载荷出现概率高

如果断裂仅导致轻微损坏，安全系数可降低约 15%。如果相同载荷再次出现的概率较低，安全系数可降低约 10%。

jG*jF：这些值适用于

- 失效导致严重损坏

- 不定期检查

如果断裂仅导致轻微损坏，安全系数可降低约 15%。如果定期进行检查，安全系数可降低约 10%。

English Original

Enter safeties

If this option is selected, you can enter the required safeties on the right-hand side. Alternatively, click the Plus button to display the Define safeties dialog window in which you can specify safeties as defined in FKM.

The safety factors for the static strength calculation, jm (for overload failure) and jp (for deformation), are determined in accordance with section 1.5 of the guideline, and the safety factor for fatigue resistance, jD, is determined in accordance with Part 2.5 of the guideline. You will find detailed comments in the Guideline.

Steel

jm = 2.0

jp = 1.5

jF = 1.5

jF = 1.5

-non-destruction-tested

jG*jF = 2.4

-not checked

jm = 3.0

jm, jp: The values apply for

-

severe damage as the result of failure

-

high probability of load occurrence

If only minor damage results from the fracture, the safety factors can be reduced by about 15%. Provided the probability of the same load occurring again is low, the safety factors can be reduced by about 10%.

jG*jF: The values apply for

- severe damage as the result of failure

- irregular inspection

If only minor damage results from the fracture, the safety factors can be reduced by about 15%. Provided inspections are carried out regularly, safety factors can be reduced by about 10%.
