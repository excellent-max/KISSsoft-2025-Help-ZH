中文翻译

更改报告中的角度输出

是否可以在KISSsoft 角度报告中同时以度数和小数形式输出角度（在计算中）？

当前格式：##.#### °

所需格式：## ° ## ’ ## ’’

为此，请相应更改报告模板（*.rpt）。执行此操作前，请阅读手册中关于报告模板的说明（见 报告模板 一章）。随后计算将在报告中执行。下面用一个螺旋角来演示此方法：

之前的格式（小数）：

螺旋角（grd） %11.4f {Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)}=>

当前格式（度数）：

螺旋角 (度) %i° %i' %i" {Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)} {(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)-int(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)))*60} {((Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)-int(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)))*60-int((Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)-int(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)))*60))*60}

English Original

Changing the output of angles in reports

Is it possible to output angles (in calculations) in the KISSsoft angle report as degree values as well as decimal numbers?

Current form: ##.#### °

Required form: ## ° ## ’ ## ’’

To do this, change the report template (*.rpt) accordingly. Read the notes in the manual about report templates (see chapter Report templates) before you do this. The calculation is then performed in the report. A helix angle is used to show this method:

Previous format, as a decimal number:

Helix angle (grd) %11.4f {Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)}=>

Current format, now as a degree value:

Helix angle (grd) %i° %i' %i" {Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)} {(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)-int(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)))*60} {((Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)-int(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)))*60-int((Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)-int(Grad(ZS.Geo.beta)))*60))*60}
