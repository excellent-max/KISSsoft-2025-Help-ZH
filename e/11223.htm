中文翻译

常规

在安装过程中，可以修改安装距以获得额外的侧隙。还可以通过 Δj 指定所需的额外侧隙量（以模数的系数形式输入）。整体式小齿轮轴Δα1和齿轮轴Δα2的所需轴向位移则根据 ISO/TR 22849 计算。同时也会计算通过输入预定义的安装距修改值所获得的额外侧隙。

English Original

General

During the mounting process, you can modify the mounting distance to achieve additional backlash. You can also specify how much additional backlash you require with Δj (enter this as a coefficient in the module). The required axial displacement for the integral pinion shaft Δα1 and the gear shaft Δα2 is then calculated according to ISO/TR 22849. The additional backlash that would be achieved by entering a predefined modification to the mounting distance is also calculated.
