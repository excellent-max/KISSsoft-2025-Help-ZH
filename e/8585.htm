中文翻译

所需使用寿命

您直接在此输入字段中输入所需的使用寿命。

单击尺寸按钮以根据齿根和齿面强度的最低安全系数来确定使用寿命。配置中所有齿轮的使用寿命都会显示出来。 您也可以单击尺寸按钮，在定义或不定义载荷谱（见"定义载荷谱"章）的情况下确定使用寿命。关于定义载荷谱的更多详细信息，请参见“定义载荷谱”章。

English Original

Required service life

You enter the required service life directly in this input field.

Click the Sizing button to size the service life based on the minimum safeties for tooth root and flank strength. The service life for all the gears in the configuration is displayed. You can also click the Sizing button to size the service life with or without defining a load spectrum (see chapter Define load spectrum). More detailed information about defining load spectra is provided here (see chapter Define load spectrum).
