中文翻译

考虑压力角系数 YSa

按 ISO 6336 的齿根应力计算适用于法向压力角为 20°（按 ISO 53）的基准齿廓。为更全面地考虑法向压力角对齿根应力的影响，可以使用 Langheinrich [22] 定义的压力角系数 YSα。

外齿、齿条：

YSa, sym = 1+0.012(20-αn)

内齿：

YSa, sym = 1+0.001(20-αn)

English Original

Consider pressure angle factor YSa

The tooth root stress calculation according to ISO 6336 is valid for a reference profile with a normal pressure angle of 20° according to ISO 53. For an extended consideration of the influence of the normal pressure angle on the tooth root stress, the pressure angle factor YSα as defined in Langheinrich [22] can be used.

External toothing, rack:

YSa, sym = 1+0.012(20-αn)

Internal toothing:

YSa, sym = 1+0.001(20-αn)
