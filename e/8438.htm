中文翻译

变位系数

在制造过程中可对刀具进行调整。加工分度圆与刀具参考线之间的距离称为变位量。要产生正变位，刀具从材料中拉出的距离更大，从而形成齿根较厚、齿顶较窄的轮齿。要产生负变位，刀具向材料中推入更深，其结果是轮齿变窄，并且可能更早出现根切。除了对齿厚的影响外，滑动速度也会受到变位系数的影响。

单击“转换”按钮，KISSsoft 将确定变位系数（参见变位系数一章）取自实测数据还是取自图纸给定值。

注意

如果两个变位系数值中的某一个以灰色显示，则表示该值将由 KISSsoft 计算。当您选中用于输入中心距的复选框时就会出现这种情况。如果您覆盖了灰色字段，它将变为活动状态，KISSsoft 将为另一个齿轮计算该值。

English Original

Profile shift coefficient

The tool can be adjusted during the manufacturing process. The distance between the production pitch circle and the tool reference line is called the profile shift. To create a positive profile shift, the tool is pulled further out of the material, creating a tooth that is thicker at the root and narrower at the tip. To create a negative profile shift, the tool is pushed further into the material, with the result that the tooth is narrower and undercutting may occur sooner. In addition to the effect on tooth thickness, the sliding velocities will also be affected by the profile shift coefficient.

Click the Convert button and KISSsoft will determine whether the profile shift coefficients (see chapter Profile shift coefficient) are to be taken from measured data or from values given in drawings.

Note

If one of the two profile shift values appears in gray, this means it will be calculated by KISSsoft. This is what happens when you select the checkbox for entering the center distance. If you overwrite a gray field, it will become active and KISSsoft will calculate the value for one of the other gears.
