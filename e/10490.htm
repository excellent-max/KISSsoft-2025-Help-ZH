中文翻译

尺寸计算

当你选择 Own Input，此计算模块可计算长主轴在受压力时的小径d3。

它还可以根据许用面压和所需安全系数确定螺母长度。

English Original

Sizings

When you select Own Input, this calculation module can calculate the core diameter d3 of a long spindle that is subject to pressure.

It can also define the nut length on the basis of permissible surface pressure and the required safety.
