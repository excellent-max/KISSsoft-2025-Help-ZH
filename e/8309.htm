中文翻译

旋转方向

旋转方向会影响载荷沿轴的分布方式，例如，由于斜齿轮轮齿的影响，会影响轴承的额定寿命。单击转速输入字段右侧的复选框以显示下拉条目，并选择所需条目。但是，如果此复选框未激活，则该值取自“轴旋转”输入字段（参见"旋转方向"章）， 该输入字段位于基本数据输入窗口中。

English Original

Direction of rotation

The sense of rotation can influence the way loads are distributed along the shaft, for example, as the result of helical gear teeth, and therefore affect the rating life of the bearing. Click the checkbox to the right of the Speed input field to display the drop-down entries and select the one you require. However, if this checkbox is not active, the value is taken from the Shaft rotation input field (see chapter Direction of rotation) input field in the Basic data input window.
