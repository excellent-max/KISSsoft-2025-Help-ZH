中文翻译

从 M-Base 导入材料文件

来自 M-Base 材料数据中心的材料数据集也可以处理。如果有数据集可用，请单击计算 > 导入材料数据将其导入 KISSsoft 并进行处理和转换。来自 M-Base 材料数据中心的材料数据集包含相关塑料的一般信息（杨氏模量、拉伸应力等）。但这些数据集不包含无限寿命强度的信息。

English Original

Importing material files from M-Base

Material datasets from the M-Base Material Data Center can also be processed. If a dataset is available, click on Calculation > Import material data to import it into KISSsoft and process and convert it. Material datasets from the M-Base Material Data Center contain general information about the relevant plastics (Young's modulus, tensile stresses etc.). However, these datasets do not include information about infinite life strengths.
