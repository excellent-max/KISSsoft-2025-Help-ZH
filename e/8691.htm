中文翻译

齿间载荷分配系数

齿间载荷分配系数 KHα 根据所选的计算方法计算。齿间载荷分配系数考虑了多对齿之间不均匀的接触特性。当重合度增大时，齿间载荷分配系数也会根据预设的制造质量等级而变大。较高的重合度会导致齿根应力的减小。对于较大的单个法向齿距偏差，齿间载荷分配系数将补偿这一影响。

在特殊情况下，齿间载荷分配系数会高得不切实际。如果在这种情况下想减小齿间载荷分配系数，只需单击输入字段右侧的复选框。然后即可更改该值。

English Original

Transverse load factor

The transverse load factor KHα is calculated according to the selected calculation method. The transverse load factor takes into account uneven contact characteristics across a number of teeth. When the contact ratio increases, the transverse load factor also becomes larger depending on the predefined manufacturing quality. A high contact ratio will result in a reduction of the root stresses. The transverse load factor will compensate for this effect for large single normal pitch deviations.

In unusual cases, the transverse load factor will be unrealistically high. If you want to reduce the transverse load factor in this situation, simply click the checkbox to the right of the input field. You can then change this value.
