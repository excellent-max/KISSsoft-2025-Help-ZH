润滑脂寿命

润滑脂寿命是指轴承无需再加润滑脂即可保持充分润滑的时间长度。如果在没有再加润滑脂的情况下达到润滑脂寿命，可以预期轴承将失效。
就其本质而言，润滑脂寿命非常依赖于具体应用。然而，有一些方法可以用来估算其近似值。在 KISSsoft 系统中，可以使用 Schaeffler (INA/FAG) 和 SKF 目录中的方法来计算指导值。选择 计算 > 设置 以显示设置选项卡。在其中，您可以选择合适的方法。当您选择扩展寿命计算（参见"根据 ISO 281 的修正额定寿命计算"章）并在其中选择润滑脂作为润滑剂时，将执行该计算。

English Original

Grease lifetime

Grease lifetime is the length of time that a bearing remains adequately lubricated without having to be regreased. If the grease lifetime is reached without regreasing, it can be expected that the bearing will fail.
By its very nature, the grease lifetime is very application-specific. However, there are approaches which can be used to estimate its approximate value. In the KISSsoft system, methods from the Schaeffler (INA/FAG) and SKF catalogs can be used to calculate a guide value. Select Calculation > Settings to display the Settings tab. In it, you can select the appropriate method. The calculation is performed when you select the Enhanced service life calculation (see chapter Modified rating life calculation according to ISO 281) and select a grease as the lubricant in it.
