中文翻译

整体定义

整体定义必须以坐标系的定义开始。之后，您可以列出任意数量的折线图和条形图定义。每个部分定义必须像整体定义一样用方括号括起来：

[ [af_1] , | [ af_2] , | [dg_l_1] , ..., | [ dg_l_ n1 ] , | [ dg_b_ 1 ] , ..., [ dg_b_ n2 ]]

如果同时使用折线和条形图，系统将自动应用第二个坐标系。但是，可以通过定义第二个坐标系来更改此设置。下面显示了选项的示例：

[

[['x-ACHSE'],['y-Achse',0],[[40,250,150],[-1000,-10]],[30,20,0,0]],

[['x-ACHSE2'],['y-Achse2',0],['blue',[0,0]],[30,20,0,0]],

[1,[-1000,-500,0,500,1000],[5,20,40,55,71],['LINE1','red',0]],

[1,[-1000,-500,0,500,1000],[2,20,46,60,83],['LINE2',[200,5,150],3]],

[2,[5,25,16,10,4],['red',3],'group1'],

[2,[40,35,25,20,12],['red',3],'group2']

]

该示例在两个独立的坐标系中显示了两条折线和两组条形图。

English Original

Entire definition

The entire definition must begin with the definition of the axis system. After this, you can list any number of definitions for line and bar charts. Each part definition must be enclosed in square brackets, just like the entire definition:

[ [af_1] , | [ af_2] , | [dg_l_1] , ..., | [ dg_l_ n1 ] , | [ dg_b_ 1 ] , ..., [ dg_b_ n2 ]]

If lines and bars are used simultaneously, a second coordinates system will automatically be applied. This can, however, be changed by the definition of a second coordinates system. An example of the options is shown below:

[

[['x-ACHSE'],['y-Achse',0],[[40,250,150],[-1000,-10]],[30,20,0,0]],

[['x-ACHSE2'],['y-Achse2',0],['blue',[0,0]],[30,20,0,0]],

[1,[-1000,-500,0,500,1000],[5,20,40,55,71],['LINE1','red',0]],

[1,[-1000,-500,0,500,1000],[2,20,46,60,83],['LINE2',[200,5,150],3]],

[2,[5,25,16,10,4],['red',3],'group1'],

[2,[40,35,25,20,12],['red',3],'group2']

]

The example shows two lines and two groups of bars in two separate coordinates systems.
