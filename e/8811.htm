中文翻译

用齿轮副中的另一齿轮生成

您可以使用齿轮副中的另一齿轮来计算所有齿轮（齿轮 1 除外，即齿轮编号 - 1）的齿形。在这种情况下，可以覆盖制造中心距和齿顶圆。齿轮之间的侧隙既可以通过减小制造中心距来生成，也可以通过输入圆周侧隙来生成。齿顶间隙通过增大刀具的齿顶圆来实现。

English Original

Generation with the other gear in the pair

You can use the other gear in the pair to calculate the tooth form on all the gears, except on gear 1 (gear number - 1). In this case, you can overwrite the manufacturing center distance and the tip circle. The clearance between the gears can be generated either by reducing the manufacturing center distance or by inputting the circumferential backlash. The tip clearance is achieved by increasing the tip circle of the tool.
