中文翻译

报告范围

要预设报告的范围或长度（按 1 到 9 的等级），请选择 报告 > 设置 菜单选项。9 将生成完整报告，1 将生成简短报告。在报告模板中，您会在每行开头看到 1 到 9 之间的数字。该数字与上述设置共同决定该行是否被读取。

例如：如果输入 5（中等）作为报告长度，报告模板中所有以 1、2、3、4 或 5 开头的行都会被读取。以 6、7、8 和 9 开头的行将不会被读取。

English Original

Scope of a report

To preset the scope or length of a report, on a scale of 1 to 9, select the Report > Settings menu option. 9 will produce a complete report, and 1 will produce a short report. In the report template, you see a number between 1 and 9 at the beginning of every row. This number works together with the setting described above to determine whether or not the row is to be read.

Example: If you entered 5 (medium) as the report length, all the lines in the report template that start with 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 are read. Rows with 6, 7, 8 and 9 will be not read.
