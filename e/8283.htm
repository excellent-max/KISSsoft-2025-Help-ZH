中文翻译

根据 ISO 281 进行修正的额定寿命计算

修正的额定寿命 Lnm 的计算是对基本额定寿命 L10 计算的扩展，它考虑了可靠性、轴承材料的疲劳极限、润滑和污染条件的影响。有关计算的更多信息（参见"根据 ISO 281 进行修正的额定寿命计算"章）。

如果激活此选项，您需要根据自己的要求配置 润滑剂 和 污染 选择列表以及 润滑剂温度 输入字段。失效概率 用于计算可靠性寿命修正系数 a1，可在 计算 > 设置 中调整。

English Original

Modified rating life calculation according to ISO 281

The calculation of the modified rating life Lnm extends the calculation of the basic rating life L10 by considering the influence of reliability, fatigue limit of bearing material, lubrication and contamination conditions. More information on the calculation (see chapter Modified rating life calculation according to ISO 281).

If you activate this option, you need to configure the Lubricant and Contamination selection lists as well as the Lubricant temperature input field according to your requirements. Failure probability which is used for the calculation of the life modification factor for reliability a1 can be adjusted in Calculation > Settings.
