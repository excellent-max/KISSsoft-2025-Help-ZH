中文翻译

模数比

法向模数 mn 与齿宽 b 之比应处于特定范围内，除非有特殊原因，否则不能超过（或低于）该范围：

有断齿风险的表面硬化锥齿轮

7 ≤ (b/mn) ≤ 12

有点蚀风险或
整体淬硬或未淬硬的锥齿轮

10≤ (b/mn) ≤ 14

English Original

Module ratio

The normal module mn should be in a ratio to the facewidth b within specific limits which can only be exceeded (or not reached) for exceptional reasons:

surface hardened bevel gears at risk of tooth fracture

7 ≤ (b/mn) ≤ 12

bevel gears at risk of pitting or
through hardened or not hardened

10≤ (b/mn) ≤ 14
