中文翻译

强度值

DIN 2091 中的定义：

DIN 2091 仅适用于 DIN 17221（已废止）、新的 DIN EN 10089 (2003) 中定义的材料。
允许的剪切应力：
对于未预紧的扭杆：τzul = 700 N/mm2
对于预紧的扭杆：τzul = 1020 N/mm2
这些数值的热处理强度为：Rm = 1600 - 1800 N/mm2
对于剪切模量，默认使用 G = 78500 N/mm2。由于扭杆弹簧在热处理后进行预紧（超过屈服点，沿工作方向变形），工作应力的分布将更加有利，并且边界区域将得到松弛。

English Original

Strength values

Definitions in DIN 2091:

DIN 2091 only applies for the materials defined in DIN 17221 (withdrawn), new DIN EN 10089 (2003).
The permitted shear stresses:
For non-preloaded bars: τzul = 700 N/mm2
For preloaded bars: τzul = 1020 N/mm2
The heat treatment strength for these values is: Rm = 1600 - 1800 N/mm2
For the shearing modulus, G = 78500 N/mm2 is used as the default. Due to preloading (above the yield point, deformed in the direction of operation) after the torsion bar springs have been heat treated, there will be a more favorable distribution of the working stress, and a relief in the boundary zone will be achieved.
